# Recorded gunshots

Cut from **The Free Firearm Sound Library** (Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney), released CC0:
"CC0 NO RIGHTS RESERVED for this library. It may be used without royalty or credit ... for any application, personal or professional."
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library ("Prepared SFX Library.7z")

Each file here is one shot of one of the library's recordings, cut, brought to 48 kHz and encoded as Ogg Opus by `scripts/audio/cut_guns.py`.
