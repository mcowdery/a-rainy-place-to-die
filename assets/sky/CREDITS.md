# Sky assets

- `moon_albedo.jpg`: the colour map from NASA's [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720) (`lroc_color_2k.jpg`),
  made by Ernie Wright (NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio) from the Lunar
  Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera's Wide Angle Camera mosaic. NASA imagery, free to use; credit NASA's Scientific
  Visualization Studio.
- `moon_normal.jpg`: normals baked from the same kit's elevation (`ldem_3_8bit.jpg`, from the Lunar Orbiter Laser
  Altimeter) by `scripts/sky/moon_maps.py`.
