## Rigged, in the showroom only

`rigged/cat.glb` is Poly by Google's Cat (candidates/6dM1J6f6pm9.glb, CC-BY 3.0, https://poly.pizza/m/6dM1J6f6pm9) and `rigged/gull_fly.glb` its Flying seagull (6Tpj_vcWP3f, CC-BY 3.0): changed by `scripts/animals/rig.py` (a skeleton and weights added, clips Idle, Walk, Run, Sit, Loaf and Flap, Glide). The credit's note of changes: "rigged and animated for this game".

The other files in `rigged/` are the same: each is the candidate of the same id (see `scripts/animals/rigs/_make.py` for which) rigged and animated by `scripts/animals/autorig.py`, with its licence and author as in the table above.

`candidates/29hG1_J1Uiq.glb` (Capuchin, Poly by Google, CC-BY 3.0, https://poly.pizza/m/29hG1_J1Uiq) was added 2026-10-10 for the monkey rows; `candidates/24WsfFi7Q8s.glb` (Poly's Dog) was turned upright in Blender (`scripts/animals/reorient.py`), which was a change.
