# Mack: his body, guns, face and wardrobe

Covers the first-person rig, his guns, his walk, run and jump, his body in the city, the third-person camera, his hidden face, his wardrobe. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## First person, and his guns

**First person** (`models/firstPerson.ts`, `FirstPersonRig`, for the fights; not in the district yet): Mack's model under the camera with the head and neck folded away (scaled to nothing), standing `BACK` behind the eyes (more looking down), the upper body bending with the view and turned into a shooter's stance, the hands put on the gun by two-bone IK (`hold`: the elbow toward a pole, the hand's rest frame from its finger bones turned onto the hold's palm and forward, the fingers drawn together, then each knuckle closing until the finger touches what it holds (`HandHold.wrap`, a rod with an oval section, `r` and `rx`; `trigger` leaves the right index along the trigger; each hand's closing direction found by trying it, since the hands mirror), the thumb closing over the hold the same way (its base across the palm, its joints round the rod, toward it), the grip hand slanted toward the muzzle (`GRIP_SLANT`) so the forearm comes from behind, and the forearm taking part of the hand's roll (`TWIST_SHARE`: the rig has no twist bones)), a second copy of the body that keeps its head casting the shadow (never drawn: its materials write nothing), the legs standing, walking or running at real cadences (`STRIDE_HZ`: about 0.85 strides a second walking, 1.4 running, the rest of a run's speed longer strides, the body leaning in); running and not aiming, the gun is carried low in the right hand in the body's frame, swinging with the arm (`HOLD.run`, `RUN_PUMP`), the left hand leaving the forend to pump; a free left hand rests half closed (`relaxHand`, `RELAXED`) instead of the model's flat rest hand. The gun is held in the camera's frame (`HOLD`: low, or raised to aim on the right button), with both hands or in the right hand alone (`oneHand`: at rest upright at his side, muzzle to the sky, as in *Terminator 2*; swung down level to aim with the arm fully straight (the gun moved along the line from the shoulder so the wrist lands at `EXTEND` of the arm's reach; the recoil's push added after, so it still kicks), and a click while upright swings it down, fires when level and brings it back up; the left arm hanging, the chest turned the other way, a harder kick), kicks on a spring, flashes, and reloads; one-handed, the lever-action is worked with a flip-cock (the gun spun forward once round the loop, `FLIP_TIME`, about `FLIP_PIVOT` on `FLIP_AXIS`, tipped so the barrel passes outside the forearm, the arm dropping and swinging out). `models/shotgun.ts` builds Mack's sawn-off shotgun from extruded side profiles and tubes, two designs were reviewed, `lever` (approved: his; the showroom's G cycles it and the pistol, `MACK_GUNS`) (after the sawn-off 1887s in the films: the stock cut at the wrist into a rounded wooden handle (`loft`: oval sections along a line, `HANDLE`, deep at the receiver, narrowing to the wrist, flaring to the cut end, its end grain darker) running back and down at 30 degrees from the top of the receiver's rounded back, the long loop lever under it, thrown after each shot with the right hand riding it, a long slim forend, about 78 cm overall, dark blued steel) and `double` (a side-by-side, its action colour-case-hardened; not taken, left on the showroom's stand), and his handgun, `pistol` (`models/pistol.ts`: the 黒星 Type 54, the yakuza's black-market pistol: a long flat slide that kicks back on a shot (`Shotgun.slide`), an exposed hammer, star-in-a-circle grips; eight rounds, one pellet, its own holds (`Shotgun.holds`, over `HOLD`): low ready, two hands with the left wrapped over the right's fingers, one-handed by the thigh or out at arm's length; the grip's `at` sits back over the backstrap so the knuckles reach its front and the fingers wrap the grip (`debug-shots/handaudit.mjs <dir> pistol`)); lathed tapered barrels with crowned muzzles, cross-sections extruded along the bore, canvas-painted walnut and case colours; both stand on a table at the end of the showroom's cast pad (`__lab` exposes three, the scene and the builder for shots of a model on its own: `debug-shots/gunside.mjs`); each says where the hands go (`HandHold`).

## His walk, run and jump

**His walk, run and jump** (firstPerson.ts; the user, 2026-10-05/06): the walk and the run are the thighs swung and the knees bent through `legPose`, and **they stay that way**. Asked to make them look better ("too wide", "his speed doesn't match his strides I think"), a rebuilt gait was tried (the cadence and the stride's length from the ground speed, the feet planted by two-bone IK, the city's 4.5 m/s default pace shown as the run it is, a walk on a key) and turned down: "it looks bad. He no longer walks... The old walking animation was much better, just his gait was too wide", and that he covers more ground than his strides is fine, "since he's not completely human". So: only the width changed. The model's own stance is an A-pose's (ankles 41 cm apart); the legs are now brought in under the hips (`STANCE_IN`, and `STANCE_WALK` more as he walks: ankles ~22 cm apart standing, ~19 walking), the feet kept flat. Don't match his animation to his speed by changing which gait he's in; the default pace is a walk.

**A jump** (Space, as before; `FirstPersonRig.air`/`airV` from `FirstPerson.airHeight`/`airSpeed`, `JUMP`): the legs all but straight and the toes pointed as he leaves the ground, drawn up at the top (the leading leg ahead), straight again coming down; the arms up and out a little; and as he lands the knees give on a spring (the body comes down ~7 cm on them and is back up in a third of a second, the view dipping a little). `node debug-shots/restpose.mjs <dir> "" gait|jump` photographs eight points of the walk and the run from the side and the front, and a jump (the showroom's `__fp.pace(v)`, `stridePhase(rad)`, `air(height, rise)` hold them still). Free mocap wasn't used; if it's ever wanted, Quaternius' Universal Animation Library (CC0) is the candidate and would need retargeting onto this skeleton.

## His body in the city

**In the city** (district/main.ts) the rig is your body on foot: loaded hands free (`armed` false: both arms swinging, pumping running, hands relaxed; standing, they hang at his sides all but straight, the hand beside the thigh with its palm to it and turned a little back, the fingers loosely curled: `HANG` and `RELAXED` in firstPerson.ts, after the user found the first version's bent elbows, hands held out in front and clawed fingers odd, 2026-10-05; `node debug-shots/restpose.mjs [dir] [outfit]` photographs him standing, walking and running from four sides and each hand up close), scaled to the camera's 1.7 m eye height (`fitEye`), posed each frame from the view (its pitch passed in where the camera doesn't pitch: `FirstPerson.viewPitch`) and a gait mapped from the game's speeds (its walk, 4.5 m/s, his walk; its run, 9, his run), sliding back as you look steeply down (`BACK_STEEP`) so you see your legs and boots; hidden driving, flying, riding or in a scene; its muzzle-flash light is left out (a new light recompiles every city shader). Q on foot switches to third person (see below); X draws or puts away the shotgun, the right button raises it, the left fires (shooting in the city is allowed: `real/gunfire.ts`, `CityGunfire`, marches each pellet or bullet along the bore against `District.shotAt` (the ground and floors, walls to each building's height, parked cars, poles and hedges near the ground) and the traffic, leaving holes, dust and chips, sparks off metal, the blast or the pistol's crack and the impacts heard from where they are; nobody reacts yet, the mob and the traffic carry on; `?debug=1` exposes `__fire()`). `debug-shots/citybody.mjs` photographs it.

## Faceless, and the third-person camera

**Mack is faceless:** his face is never shown. Third person (`models/thirdPerson.ts`, `ThirdPersonCamera`; Q in the showroom's first person, `__fp.third(true)`, and on foot in the district, remembered in localStorage `citypop.thirdPerson`) is an over-the-shoulder camera that **turns freely round him** (the user's direction, 2026-10-05: the camera mustn't force him to turn; before that it stayed behind him, his body facing where you looked). `BodyFacing` in thirdPerson.ts keeps which way his body faces apart from the view and hands the rig a stand-in camera at his eyes turned that way: he faces the way he's going (walk toward the camera and you see his front) and stands as he was when he stops; with a gun raised, his fists or a sword out, or a shot just fired (1.5 s) he turns to the view (`modeFor`); first person is the view itself. His face stays shadowed from any angle. (Closer while aiming, `footOffset`; pulled in by walls (the showroom's raycast, the district's `blocked` at his level), aimed at `AIM_RANGE` along the view so the crosshair holds; behind the bike when riding in the showroom, the look round it limited). The page keeps the camera at his eyes for everything (walking, streaming, sound, interiors) and moves it behind him only for the render (`place` before, `restore` after); with the head shown the body stands with its eyes at the camera's eyes, and the head nods with the view. How his face is hidden is under review (`FaceStyle`, `FACE_STYLES`, kept with the wardrobe; showroom first person: N cycles it, C a review mirror in front of his face (front, three quarter, profile; never in play); the district's debug menu Face): `shadow` (below), `brim` (a soft shadow from the brow), `none` (bare, for sunglasses), `mosaic` and `blur` (on the finished image: `models/censorPass.ts`, the last composer pass in the showroom and the district, over the ellipse `FirstPersonRig.faceOnScreen` gives), `smooth` (a blank mannequin face: the face's vertices onto an ellipsoid in the skin shader and in its shadow pass (`faceDepth`, a clone loses `customDepthMaterial`), one even skin tone, the inside-out mouth and sockets discarded) and `chrome` (that face mirrored), `bar` (a black censor bar over the eyes facing whichever camera draws it) and `robo` (`models/roboHelmet.ts`, a RoboCop helmet: shell, visor band, the jaw bare). The default, `shadow`: as a backstop for any angle that comes round, `models/faceShadow.ts` (`FACELESS`, applied when a character loads) makes the front of his face one flat dark tone, his hair's colour in shadow, with no shading, so no feature can be read (a mask at rest on the skin, eyes, brows and lashes, from in front of the ears, under the chin to the hairline; a trace of rim only at the silhouette of the sideways-facing cheeks and jaw; `FACE_SHADOW`, `__fp.faceShadow(k)`). Never frame a camera on his face.

## His wardrobe

**His wardrobe** (`models/wardrobe.ts`, `OUTFITS`): an outfit is a model of its own, built from a definition that names `mack.json` as its `base` and gives only what differs (`load_definition` in build_character.py: its keys replace the base's, its garments are added less the types in `without`): `mack` the leathers (the bōsōzoku leader's jacket, tee, jeans, boots), `mack_suit_black|charcoal|navy|cream` (MakeHuman's `male_elegantsuit01` recoloured by region, `shoes04`; a texture op's `orig: true` masks by the image's brightness before any op), `mack_hapa` (a trial of his look: half Japanese, `race` half asian, the Asian skin, an eyelid fold and softer nose by targets, dark brown eyes; in the leathers), `mack_nude` (story only: the arrival; MakeHuman's body is smooth at the groin, so it's censored as built; the demo leaves it out: `DEMO_HIDDEN_CHARACTERS`); every `mack_*` keeps the shadowed face (`isFaceless`). Sunglasses (`models/sunglasses.ts`, `GLASSES_KINDS`: `wrap` the T2 nod, `aviator`, `slim`; mirrored lenses with their own small reflection map so they read on the dark face) are worn on the head bone like the helmet (`FirstPersonRig.setGlasses`, hidden under a helmet). A change of clothes loads the outfit's model and `takeOver`s from the rig he's in (gun, fight, bike, helmet, glasses, head, eye fit); `loadDressed` loads him as chosen (localStorage `citypop.wardrobe`) in the district, on the race page and in the showroom. His motorcycle helmet can be worn on foot too (`WardrobeState.helmet`, `FirstPersonRig.wearHelmet`/`wornHelmet`: kept on riding a bike without one, back on after a bike with its own). Showroom first person: O clothes, Z sunglasses, K helmet, `__fp.wear(outfit, glasses)`, `__fp.helmet(name)`; the district: the debug menu's Wardrobe (clothes change on foot). How he gets clothes in play is still to come. `debug-shots/wardrobe.mjs`, `citywardrobe.mjs`. `debug-shots/fpthird.mjs`, `fphead.mjs`, `citythird.mjs` (each writes an `index.html` gallery, `debug-shots/gallery.mjs`, so the dev server shows the folder at `/debug-shots/<dir>/`).
