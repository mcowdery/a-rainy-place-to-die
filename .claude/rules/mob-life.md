# The mob: how the crowd lives

Covers posing on the GPU, walking and routines, emotes, who is out when, crowd shadows, passengers, followers. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## Posing, walking and routines

**They're posed and animated on the GPU:** `ghostMaterial`'s vertex shader rebuilds the skeleton from a few numbers per figure (body, pose, facing, look, walk, floor: `packFigures`, `FIGURE_STRIDE`, `FIGURE_ATTRS`) and `uTime`, mirroring `poseBones` (`posedFigure` is the CPU reference the tests use).

**Most people are going somewhere** (`cellCrowd`, ~65-70% walking): walkers (`FigureSpec.walk`) walk a clear stretch of pavement, shared lane, plaza or park path (up to 90 m) alone, in couples, with a child or two friends side by side, fading in at one end and out at the other. Nobody stands in the road: `footingOf` (with the neighbouring cells' roads) calls carriageways and junction boxes 'road' (the pavements stop at junctions) and shared lanes 'lane' (walk only). People stand only where it makes sense: against a wall on the phone or waiting, talking where the pavement is wide; in plazas mostly walking through, someone alone only with their back to something or (near a stamp's landmark, `plazas()`'s `focus`) watching it, the rest in pairs and groups; nobody stands facing a wall (a final pass turns them round). You walk through people on foot (story NPCs too); cars still stop for them. Standing also and **at the kerb waiting for the walk light**: at each zebra (just outside each junction box, as `real/ground.ts` draws them) a few wait and cross; at signals (cell-edge roads on the grid lines) on the signal's clock (`FigureSpec.walk.signal`: cycle, when they appear, how long they wait; `Signals.walkPhase`; the mob's `uTime` is the traffic's clock, `TrafficSystem.clock`), so they cross on the green while the cars wait, and at scrambles diagonally too, corner to corner.

**Standing people go about a routine** (in the shader: `pickAct`, `act`): every 5-11 s (their own pace) they move on to another everyday thing, eased over a second: standing easy on one leg then the other, hands in pockets, texting with both hands, a call, arms crossed, hands clasped, looking about, a glance at the watch, talking with a hand, a brief wave. Their pose sets the temperament (a talker mostly talks, a phone user mostly texts or calls, a 'wave' waves now and then); someone holding a bag (side +-2 in the packed numbers) skips the two-handed things. Set pieces' figures posed mid-stride with nowhere to walk stand and do the same; fixed figures (`fade: false`: the showroom's grid, story NPCs) keep their pose. The forearm has a side bend (a wave) and an inward twist (crossed arms) as well as its bend (`Limb.side`, `twist`). People together share a seed (`FigureSpec.seed`) and walk together. The streets' crowds are drawn instanced by `real/crowd.ts` (`Crowd`, owned by the District: the chunk workers' crowd stage returns packed figures, not geometry; one draw per template in use for everyone in range, ~20-26 draws; buffers rebuilt only when the chunks shown change; umbrellas while it rains). Set pieces' people (`Kit.person`, the konbini, the live house...), story NPCs and the showroom bake theirs with the same attributes (`GhostBuilder`, `addFigure`, `addUmbrella`), so one material animates both. Drawn in the opaque pass with alpha to coverage (one surface per figure, no seams, no sorting) in one of `MOB_LOOKS` (the debug menu's People, `?mob=`; `setMobLook`, which also sets whether everything is the one black, `uBlack`, and the edge density): `ghost` (the default: all black and see-through), `color` (the figures in their colours, near-opaque, and lit by the scene: the define `MOB_LIT`, compiled in only for this look, `setMobSun` each frame gives the material the hemisphere light, the sun or moon and the sun's shadow map, which it samples itself, four taps, so people darken in a building's shadow and at night as the city does, with a small floor so nobody is a hole; the street lightmap still lights them at night; no lamp shadows on them; and nobody comes and goes in this look, since they aren't ghosts: the material's `uStay`, with which a walker stops at the end of its walk for its gap, turns round and walks back, someone at a crossing crosses on one green and back on the next, and those standing stay; the fade with distance and as you walk into someone remains).

## Emotes

**Emotes** (`real/emoteGlsl.ts`, `real/emotes.ts`; the debug menu's People tab's Emotes, `?emotes=0|1`): manga's effect marks at a figure's head now and then (a sweat drop, the anger mark, a blush, "!", "?", "!?", a heart, a note, zzz, a sigh, "...", a sparkle, gloom lines, fluster ticks): about one person in forty at a time, within 42 m, the mark weighted by what they're doing (`EMOTE_MIX`: a note for walkers, hearts for those holding hands, zzz for those standing about late at night). `emoteGlsl.ts` is what two shaders share (who emotes what and when: `emoteSlot`, `emoteMark`; the settings as uniforms on the mob's material, `emoteUniforms`, off until the crowd's `Emotes` turns them on). The marks over and beside a head, and the sigh (breath let out at the mouth, forward and down), are one instanced billboard draw on the crowd's own figure numbers (`Emotes`, placed by `MOB_PLACE_GLSL`, the joints texture and `headGlsl`, which mirrors how the mob's shader poses the head).

**The marks on a face are part of the figure's own draw**, so they turn, nod and bob with the head and can't be seen from behind: four small quads on the head's bone in each sculpted template (`mobShape.ts`, shade tag `FACE_MARK`: an eye each, a cheek each, their normals carrying uv), folded away unless that figure's emote is one of the face's, the mark drawn in the mob's fragment shader (`FACE_MARK_GLSL`: hearts or four-point stars for eyes, a blush's hatching); a flush tints the head's own skin red by how far to the front of the head it is. All black (the ghost look) there's no skin to redden, so a flush is its lines.

**Style** (the user's direction: the first sets read as bold, then as glossy clip-art): ink, not gel. Flat marks in one ivory with a thin dark keyline and a hard offset shadow, crimson only for anger and hearts, a slate blue-grey for sweat and gloom; strokes are filled shapes that swell and taper as a brush's do (`brush`); no gradients, highlights or glow; only breath is soft. Small: an accent, not a sign.

**Looks to compare** (the debug menu's People tab, `?blush=lines|flush|both`, `?hearts=over|eyes|rising`, `?stars=over|eyes|round`; `EMOTE_LOOKS`, the defaults both, eyes, over): heart and star eyes are the mark over the head when seen from behind.

## Cold breath

**Cold breath** (not an emote): one more instanced draw on the same figures (`Emotes.breath`), a soft puff of vapour at the mouth from the top of each breath, drifting forward and up, lit by the sky and the street's lightmap, within 25 m; `Crowd.cold` from the forecast's air each frame (`coldBreath` in district/forecast.ts: none above 7 °C, all of it at 2 °C and below, so never on a mild spring night; `airWith` makes snow set by hand freezing, for the breath and the weather app's number alike). Only the street crowd emotes (figures that come and go; set pieces' and the story's don't). In the district with `?diag=1`, `__district.crowd.emoteLayer.force = 'each' | '<mark>' | null` holds them up; the mob showroom's 'emotes' stage has every mark and look held up side by side, labelled (`Emotes.row`), and a button for the breath; `node debug-shots/emotes.mjs [dir] [show,face,city,perf] [query]` shoots them.

## Hours, more to do, shadows, the People tab

**Who is out, when** (`district/peopleHours.ts`, pure, `tests/peopleHours.test.ts`): each street figure keeps `hours` (always, day, commute, school, evening, late: the share of such people out by the hour, `outAt`, less in rain, `RAIN`), a threshold of its own (`out`) and a `manner`, packed as a fourth attribute (`aWhen`: hours, manner, threshold, the seasons its outfit is worn in; `FIGURE_STRIDE` 24); the material hides whoever isn't out (`MOB_PLACE_GLSL`'s `mobPlace`, shared with anything drawn with the figures: main.ts gives it `uHour`, `uRain`, `uSeason` each frame), so the streets thin and fill by themselves: uniforms at the school run, office workers at the rush hours, the nightlife after dark, the shady late, few in a downpour or a typhoon; where the night is alive (`NIGHT_LIFE` by district, a zone's `night:` in its file: Kaburo's zones, Omoide Lane, the port's nightlife) more ordinary people keep evening hours, so Kaburo doesn't sleep while the suburbs do; shorts and yukata are summer's, the down jacket winter's (`seasonsOf`); people together keep one threshold, and a child's hours if there's a child among them; set pieces' and story people have no hours and are always there.

**More to do** (the material's routine, `pickAct`/`act`, by manner): besides the first ten things, squatting on the heels, a bow, a stretch, leaning back with a knee up, laughing, looking up, scratching the head, hands on hips, a drunk's sway, nodding along (an `Act` now also leans the spine and lowers the hips); a `shady` manner squats and watches (and smokes, if it's a smoker: Smoking, above), a `drunk` sways and weaves along his walk; one walker in twenty-five is in a hurry and runs (a longer stride, the knees folding, by the walk's speed).

**The crowd casts shadows** (`mobDepthMaterial`: the mob's vertex shader again with `MOB_DEPTH`, as each crowd mesh's `customDepthMaterial`; only people within `uShadowReach`, 60 m, of the viewer, `uEye` from `setMobEye`, and not faded out, the rest dropped before they're posed; ~0.5 ms at the crossing; set pieces' baked people cast none).

**The debug menu's People tab** (`district/moodPanel.ts`' `crowd` and `windows` groups): the mob's look and whether it casts shadows (`?mob=`, `?mobShadows=0|1`), `solid` (near-opaque black mannequins), `ghost` (faint and see-through, the first look), `rim` (a cool light along the silhouette) or `lit` (pale), softly top-lit, denser at the silhouette, lit at night by the street lightmap (the city's `tLight` uniforms, capped so they stay dark); each fades on its own cycle, with distance (125-175 m) and as you walk into it, and story NPCs (`FigureSpec.fade: false`) stay. Skirts, coats and robes follow the thighs below the hips (`drape`), so they lie on the lap when sitting and swing with the stride.

## Passengers and followers

**Passengers** (`passengerMesh`, `PassengerSpot`, poses `sit` and `strap`): every train car (old and new, `real/rail.ts`, `real/trainCar.ts` on `carSeats`/`carStanding`) and bus (old and new, `real/traffic.ts`, `real/busModel.ts` on `busSeats`/`BUS_POLES`) carries a crowd baked into it (seats taken, never two side by side, people standing at the straps and poles), and people wait along every elevated and subway platform facing the track; the material is the mob's (`setPassengerMaterial`, set by main.ts before transit is built). ~0.2-0.6 ms of GPU for 250-600 people in range. `tests/people.test.ts`.

**Followers** (`district/followers.ts` pure, `real/followerParty.ts`, `real/liveFigure.ts`; `tests/followers.test.ts`): people who go about with Mack, while the story flag `following_<id>` is true (the debug menu's Followers toggles them; saves carry the flags). For now two mob figures standing in for the cast (`FOLLOWERS`): `koharu` (a woman, long hair, a skirt) and `detective` (the old detective: a man in a hat and a long coat). On foot each keeps to a spot back along the way he came (`Trail`: a crumb every 0.5 m; `gap` back and `side` across), straight there when the way is clear and else from crumb to crumb the way he went, so they take corners, doorways and stairs without a map; they use the walker's own collision and floors (`FollowWorld`), walk when he walks and run when he runs, close up and turn to face him when he stops, and catch up out of sight (`warps`) when left 35 m behind, held up for 2.5 s, or he goes somewhere by a fade. The traffic stops for them as for him. At the wheel of his car they sit in its seats (`passengerSeats` in models/carInterior.ts: the front passenger's, then the rear's; a two-seater's second follower waits out of sight), as children of the car so they ride with its body, sunk into the cushion as far as the roof asks and knees tucked up where there's no legroom; getting in and out isn't acted: they're seated when he takes the wheel, and by their own doors when he gets out, from where they walk round the car's nearer end to him (`FollowerWalk.via`). On a bike, in a taxi, on a train or bus they're out of sight and back behind him when he's on foot again; they wait through a scene and while he flies. A `LiveFigure` is one mob template drawn as a single instance whose numbers the game rewrites each frame, with two poses the material has for it: `gait` (`FigureSpec.pace` 0 standing, the routine; 1 walking; 2 running, a longer stride, the knee folding as the leg comes through, elbows bent; the stride at `phase`) and `ride` (a car seat: hips `RIDE_HIP` over the cushion, legs out ahead, `pace` tucking the knees); `close` keeps a figure whole right beside the camera (a passenger). `?debug=1` exposes `__followers`; `debug-shots/followers.mjs`.
