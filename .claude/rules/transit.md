# Transit: trains, subway, buses you ride

Covers vehicles you walk about in, the elevated lines, the subway and its stations, the terminal, rides. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## Vehicles you walk about in

**Transit, second generation (approved, the default; `?transit=old` brings back the first trains and bus):** you walk about inside moving vehicles. `district/cabin.ts` is a commuter car's walkable layout (benches with a seat each, poles, the gangways, the cab's partition, the doors when open; pure) and the `CabinLayout` interface any vehicle gives (floor, blocked, exit, seatNear); `district/busCabin.ts` a city bus's (the low front, the steps up to the back rows, the driver and fare box, the front and middle doors). `real/cabinRider.ts` (`CabinRider`) keeps the walker in the vehicle's own frame: each frame the camera goes into the car's frame, the ordinary controls walk it there against the layout (`FirstPerson.blocked` and `floorAt` swapped), and it's composed back into the world after the vehicles move (`compose`, again after the traffic for a bus); gangways carry you into the next car, E sits and stands (seats face any way: `SeatAt`), and walking out of an open door sets `exited` (main.ts `getOff`: onto the platform or pavement when there's floor there, else a fade to the station's `.platform` or out of the bus's middle door). `real/trainCar.ts` is the new commuter car (stainless with the line's colour bands, or white for the subway: `carLook`; door leaves sliding between the skins: `TrainSet2.setDoors`; benches, partitions, poles, straps, racks, LED strips, the taxi ad atlas's photo ads over the windows and hanging in the aisle, door-top screens with the next station and the destination on the front from a canvas per set: `setScreens`; gangways with bellows; the cab behind a partition you look through; the insides culled beyond 140 m), `district/rideTimeline.ts` a ride's timeline (runs, dwells with the doors opening at every station between, the hold at your stop, the pull-away after you get off; `dwellDoors` for every timetabled train's doors; pure). `TrainSystem.startRide2`/`SubwaySystem.startRide2` return a `Ridable` (the cars, the side the doors open on: left for the elevated lines' side platforms, right for the subway's island platforms); main.ts `rideWalk` runs a journey leg by leg (a change of line at a transfer), `aboard()` stands for "riding" everywhere the HUD, rain, audio and saving care. `real/busModel.ts` is the new bus (`buildBus2`: a Japanese non-step bus, cream with its line's colour, LED boards front, side and back, plug doors, the inside with yellow stanchions and stop buttons and the next-stop screen); in traffic (`TrafficSystem`'s `transit`) each bus opens its doors while it dwells (`busDoors`), E at its front door boards it for ¥210 (`busToBoard`, `busRide`), E inside is the stop button (`requestStop`: とまります on its screen). `real/airliner.ts` is the new airliner (`airliner2`: a lofted narrow-body on the city material, four invented airlines in `AIRLINES`), used by `buildAirport(city)`. `?debug=1` exposes `__rider`, `__trains`, `__subway` and (with the flag) `__gotoBus()`. `tests/cabin.test.ts`.

## Elevated rail

**Rail:** `content/world3d/rail.yaml` (`district/rail.ts`) is the elevated lines, each a path along L0 grid lines through its points (`pts`), its corners rounded into arcs (`RailPath`: sampled every metre, `at(s)`, `project`), of kind `train` (a viaduct deck, two tracks, catenary) or `monorail` (two beams on T-piers, white straddle cars): the Toto Line (T, Gakuenzaka to T05 霞町 Kasumi-chō), the Kawabata Line (K, 川端線: 歌舞路東 by Kaburo Crossing, 電光町, over the river to 川端; a 16 m street under it, `roads.yaml`) and the Tōto Monorail (M, 東都モノレール: 霞町 (the Toto Line), along Kasumi-dōri and down to the port (東都港), south over the water to Hanejima and the airport terminal's door). A station stamp says its line (`station: { jp, en, line }`); the network (`district/subway.ts`) numbers each line's stations along it and routes across all of them. The planner keeps the ground under the curves clear (`railReserved`, passed to `District` and the chunk workers). `real/rail.ts` builds each line's viaduct (its piers off the carriageway: one column in the road's median, or over a road without one a portal frame, a column at the back of each pavement and a beam across: `RoadUnder`/`roadUnder` and `portalOffset` in `district/rail.ts`, used by the stations too (`stationKerb`); a pier under each end of a line, and the train lines' ends an end wall with buffer stops, short of the junctions) and runs its trains (three-car sets on a timetable, stopping at every station, each car on the curve, keeping left; four sets shuttle each line, turning back at its ends over a crossover (`turnbacks`, `trackAt`: past a terminal station with a stub beyond it they cross over and stand at the stub's end; where the station is the end, they cross over before it and turn at its platform), never vanishing (the subway's the same way: both directions in step (`trainOffset`), so a run ends where and when the other way's stand begins, beyond the end station past a crossover in the tunnel (`laneAt` in `real/subway.ts`, each car on its track), the stand in view); `trainModel` is in the showroom); `tests/rail.test.ts` (smooth paths, stations on straight track 10 m off, routes across lines, clear of the expressway and of buildings); `real/station.ts` is the `station` landmark (concourse, gates, stairs, upper concourse, side platforms). Levels: `District.floorAt(x, z, current)` picks the level nearest the walker where they overlap (the platform over the pavement), and collision above 1 m uses the station's raised colliders. A `station` node takes the train to its `returnSpawn`: the camera rides in the car (`TrainSystem.startRide`), and E skips.

## The subway and the terminal

**Subway** (`content/world3d/subway.yaml`, `district/subway.ts`): lines run straight under roads, each station a placement of a `landmark: subway` stamp (`subway_<code>.yaml`) whose street face is 10 m from the road's centre; codes come from the line's letter and order (Y01...), `transfers` pairs stations where lines meet. Two lines: 夜光線 Yakō (Y01-Y05, east-west between rows 10 and 11) and 若葉線 Wakaba (W01-W06, north-south under col 28: through Kaburo, then south through Kasumi-chō (W04 霞町, a transfer to the Toto Line and the monorail), the port (W05 海岸 Kaigan) and under the bay to W06 汐見島 Shiomi-jima, where Shiomi-dōri's last block narrows to 20 m over it), meeting at 歌舞路一丁目 Kaburo 1-chōme. The Toto Line is in the same network as a line of kind `elevated` (T01 Asagiri, T02 Tōto-Chūō, T03 Kaburo; its station landmarks in order along the viaduct), so the route picker and transfers cover it and its rides go through `TrainSystem.startRide`.

**The terminal, 東都中央 Tōto-Chūō** (after Shinjuku, between Kaburo and Asagiri): the Toto station `station_totochuo`, the department store beside it (`totochuo_dept`, Asagiri kind `dept_store`: show windows, blank stone, a screen, a blade sign, a neon crown), and across the road the west exit (`west_exit`, landmark `rotary`, `real/rotary.ts`): a clockwise bus and taxi loop round an island with a clock tower, bus bays with parked buses (`parkedBus` in `real/traffic.ts`) and queues, a taxi rank, and two stair kiosks down to the underground mall (東都中央地下街, concourse level, lined with shops), which runs east under the tracks into Kaburo-nishiguchi's concourse (`subway_y03` sets `passage: u1`, opening that concourse wall at `PASSAGE_T`). Transfers: Tōto-Chūō and Kaburo-nishiguchi.

## Subway stations and rides

`real/subwayStation.ts` is one layout for floors (street, B1 concourse at -5, B2 island platform at -11, stairs between; the platform stairs only for walkers already below ground), collision per level (`subwayLevel`; `District.blocked` keeps -5 and -11 collider sets), the stairwell hole, shelters and geometry (self-lit: below ground there's no sky or street light) with canvas signs (roundel, name boards, fare map, a live departure board); its `below` group shows only underground or by the entrance. `real/subway.ts` runs the timetable (trains keep left, dwell, turn back beyond the end stations) and the tunnels, and rides: E on a platform opens the route picker (`district/routePicker.ts`, fewest stops and changes via `subwayRoute`), the ride plays in real time in the middle car along the real line under the city (E skips), stopping at the stations between with the door chime and each station's own departure melody (`CityAudio.chime/melody`); a change of line is a toast and the next leg. Below ground (`y < -2.6` or riding) the whole surface is hidden and the sun, sky and lightmap are off. After the last train (終電: story flag `world.late`, `?late=1`, L toggles) the entrances' shutters are down (from outside) and no trains run, subway or Toto. `?debug=1&ride=y01_station,w03_station` starts on a ride.
