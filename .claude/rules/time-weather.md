# The clock, seasons, sky and weather

Covers the story clock and the light through the day, the physical sky, seasons, the forecast, weather on the road, snow, wipers, rain and wet streets. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## The clock and the light

**The clock** ([src/poc3d/district/clock.ts](src/poc3d/district/clock.ts), pure, `tests/clock.test.ts`): time passes. Minutes since the story began are the flag `world.clock` (saves carry it; day 1 starts Friday 22:00); it runs half a game minute a real second (an hour every two minutes) except in scenes, menus and the benchmark, and jumps: **T** opens the wait panel (`district/waitPanel.ts`, like Skyrim's: 1, 3 or 6 hours, or until sunrise or sunset (the season's), morning 07:00, noon, evening 18:00, night 22:00, late night 01:30), a hotspot with `sleep: true` sleeps until 07:00 and autosaves (the MC's booth 17 and futon), a taxi adds its driving time, and the story asks for a time by flag (`time_morning`, `time_noon`, `time_evening`, `time_night`, `time_late`, and `time_dawn`/`time_dusk` for the season's sunrise and sunset: set true, the clock runs on to it and clears it; for scenes drawn for one time, and later choreographed scenes). From the minute: `world.time` (the look the story and nodes read: `phaseAt`, by the season's sunrise and sunset, so it moves with the seasons and is set again when the season changes), `world.late` (after the last train 00:40 until 05:00; L waits to either), the sun's and moon's places every frame from the clock to the fraction of a minute, so they and the shadows move smoothly (`placeLights` in main.ts), the light (`blendAt`: the atmosphere blended between the looks either side of the minute, `blendAtmosphere`; a 3D rule can also name a `season:`, so each season has its own sunrises and sunsets (summer hazy and warm, autumn amber, winter cold and clear); the looks are keyed to sunrise and sunset (`keyframes`): night, the blue hour, dawn, morning, day, golden hour, dusk, the blue hour, night, where morning, golden and bluehour (`LIGHT_LOOKS`) start from day's or dusk's rules and change what theirs set (`LOOK_BASE` in atmosphere.ts); the day's length follows the season (`DAYLIGHT`: Tokyo's sunrise, sunset and noon sun for April, July, October and January, so winter evenings are dark by five); the sun moving east over the south to west (`sunAt`, its disc only once risen) with the key light handing over to the moon through the twilight (`sunDirAt`, `moonnessAt` by the sun's height); the sky glowing round the sun low down at sunrise and sunset with a faint band opposite and the clouds there lit (the atmosphere's `sunGlow`, `uSunGlow` in `real/sky.ts`); the stars coming out through the twilight (`starsAt`) and the neon fading across the blend rather than switching; **the physical sky** (`real/skyModel.ts`, pure, `tests/skyModel.test.ts`): single scattering through a spherical atmosphere (air, a city's haze, the ozone layer, the Earth's shadow) plus Hillaire's multiple-scattering approximation, computed once into a table of sky colour by the sun's height and view direction (about a second, in a Web Worker: `real/skyWorker.ts`; the authored sky until it arrives), its brightness scaled to the day sky and partly adapted (`ADAPT`) so sunsets and the blue hour stay readable; each minute the sky shader gets the slice for the sun now (`Sky.setPhysical`, `uSkyLut`) and blends it with the authored gradient by the atmosphere's `phys` (the debug menu's Sky colours, `?skyColors=`, `SKY_COLOR_MODE` in moodPanel.ts: `computed`; `tinted`, the computed sky's light recoloured in the painted palette for the time of day (per-channel factors from the painted horizon and zenith over the computed ones, `uTintH`/`uTintZ`, worked out each minute, brightness kept; so sunsets stay pink but brighten round the sun), the light on the city keeping the painted colours; `mixed`, half of each in the painted palette; `painted`, the default (the computed sky then only shows when picked); `node debug-shots/dayshots.mjs <times> skyColors=<x> -cmp-<x>` for each and `python scripts/sky/compare_sheet.py` lay them side by side in `debug-shots/sky/compare.png`) (built in the worker only once a setting that shows it is picked; 1 by day and at sunrise and sunset, 0 at night so deep/noir and the city's glow stay authored, little under cloud, partial at the blue hour), the mountains' haze following it; the light takes its colours from the same slice (`SkyModel.stats`: the fill, the sun through the air, zenith and horizon for reflections and the sea) and the fog takes the horizon's colour where you're looking; sun shadows fading with the sun, never switched, so nothing recompiles at dawn and dusk), the phone's clock and the HUD (Day 3 · 金 22:07). `?clock=HH:MM&day=N` (and the old `?time=`, `?late=1`) set the start; `?debug=1` exposes `__clock`.

## Seasons

**Seasons** ([src/poc3d/district/seasons.ts](src/poc3d/district/seasons.ts)) turn with the story's chapters, not the days: the flag `world.season` (spring to begin with; `?season=`), which the story moves by setting `season_spring|summer|autumn|winter` (the game moves the season and clears the flag). No rebuilds: tree crowns are tagged by species (`FOLIAGE_TAG` + index in `models/trees.ts`) and the city shader colours them by `uSeason` (cherry and dogwood blossom and fresh green in spring, deep green in summer, ginkgo gold, zelkova rust, cherry red and dogwood crimson in autumn, deciduous trees bare in winter while pines, camphors, azaleas and box keep theirs), lawns go from fresh to deep green, gold and straw, the hills' forest follows (`Edges.setSeason`), the mountains take more snow in winter (`uWinter` in the sky), and in spring cherry petals, in autumn leaves, drift down all round (`Drift` in `real/weather.ts`: drawn as their shapes, turning and tumbling, carried downwind (the carry integrated on the CPU so a change of wind doesn't jump them), hanging longer as the wind rises, and when it's up a share skitter along the ground in hops). Fallen ones lie on whatever is flat (`fallenAt` in city.ts: two offset layers of cells each maybe holding a leaf or petal, turned and sized at random, with a midrib; a wash of colour further off): a thin scatter everywhere, drifts in streaks, piled along the ground slabs' edges (kerbs, the foot of walls, a plaza's rim: `slabEdge`, from each slab's rectangle and a road's kerbs that `addGround` writes into style and flags, `SLAB_FLAG`), and thick under the trees that dropped them (main.ts keeps the 32 nearest in stable slots, `uLitter`, from the cells' props and the landmarks' trees, which `addTree` reports through `setTreeSink`). Autumn is windier (a season's breeze under the debug menu's wind, `seasonWind`) and cloudier. Cherries stand where Japan plants them: in rows along park paths (hanami; parks are mostly cherries, `openLots.ts`), a row by every school's field (`SCHOOL_CHERRIES` in `real/north.ts`), the riverside, and here and there a sakura namiki (a stretch of street lined with them, more in residential quarters: `props.ts`, species per line and two-cell stretch).

## The forecast and the weather app

**The forecast** (`district/forecast.ts`, pure, `tests/forecast.test.ts`: `outlookAt(minute, season, seasonStart)`): the weather changes by itself a three-hour spell at a time with the season's odds (`ODDS`): a spell of rain (a drizzle to a downpour: `amount`, the flag `world.rain_amount`, which the rain eases toward over ten seconds), snow, or a dry spell that may hold a shower (20-60 minutes, most in spring); fog on some early mornings. Summer opens with the rainy season (梅雨 tsuyu, its first `TSUYU_DAYS` days from `world.season_start`, which main.ts sets when the season changes: grey skies and steady light rain, flag `world.tsuyu`), then heat waves (猛暑, runs of three days, flag `world.heat`: a blazing sun with hard shadows, a clear bright sky with a white-hot horizon, warm light off the ground, and a heat shimmer over the distance (only past ~80 m by the scene's depth, fading as you rise: `GradePass.heat` in `real/grade.ts`)) with evening downpours. Typhoons (台風, flag `world.typhoon`) pass over now and then in late summer and the first `TYPHOON_AUTUMN_DAYS` of autumn: `TYPHOON_HOURS` of rain bands and rising wind, a downpour and a storm-force wind at the height (the forecast's `wind` adds to the setting's, `turn` swings its direction; lightning follows), then 台風一過, clear. The debug menu's Season row has every stage: spring, the rainy season, summer, a heat wave, a typhoon, autumn, winter. `ForecastForce` (flags `world.typhoon_at`, `world.heat_until`) lets the story or the debug menu start one, and `world.settled_until` holds settled weather (no heat wave or typhoon of the forecast's own: the debug menu's plain summer and autumn). The sun's glare on clear days, strongest in a heat wave (`GradePass.glare`: a halo, a streak and lens ghosts, as much as the sun's disc shows in the image; a few texture reads, no extra pass). Summer's sound: a soft cicada chorus in waves by day (among trees, louder in a heat wave) and the higurashi at dawn and dusk (`CityAudio`, `insects()` in main.ts). It also gives the temperature. main.ts follows it unless the flag `world.weather_hold` is set (by `?weather=`, the benchmark, the debug menu's picks; its 'auto (forecast)' lets go); a change by hand lasts until the next spell.

**天気**, the phone's weather app (`district/weatherApp.ts`): now (sky, temperature, the rainy season, a heat wave and its heatstroke alert), the next 24 hours and the week, and the sun and moon (`moonTimes`, `tests/weatherApp.test.ts`: tonight's moon on the lunar calendar with its phase, its old name (三日月, 上弦の月, 十三夜, 満月, 十六夜, 下弦の月, 有明月...), age and the share lit, the next moonrise and moonset, the season's sunrise and sunset; at night the sky's icon is the moon's phase while it's up).

## Weather on the road, snow, wipers

**Weather on the road** (`district/roadGrip.ts`, pure, `tests/roadGrip.test.ts`), for your car and a race's rival: wet roads grip a little less; the shader's puddles, recomputed in 32-bit floats (`puddleAt`), drag a wheel in them (one side in one pulls the car toward it) and at speed skim it (aquaplaning), mildly, throwing up spray (`Splashes` in `real/weather.ts`: droplets as short streaks along their motion with a drop at the head, fanned out sideways and kicked up behind, and a faint mist; for yours and the traffic's wheels near you, with `CityAudio.splash`); snow cuts grip to about 0.56 and drags; wet autumn leaves are a little slippery; a crosswind pushes the car sideways with the gusts, hard up on the expressway or a bridge (`crosswind`); fog and darkness only hide things. `weatherBefore`/`weatherAfter` wrap the racing model's step; the rival takes bends and brakes by the grip there is. The traffic drives with care (`conditions` on the street's and the deck's traffic: slower, longer gaps, gentler braking and corners in rain, fog and snow). Weather `snow` (in `WEATHERS`; its atmosphere rules are a pale overcast, a glowing low sky at night): snow falls (`Drift`) and settles over a minute or so on what faces up (`uSnow` in the city shader; roads keep less), melting over several once it stops (`?snowcover=` to start with some).

**Wipers** (`models/wipers.ts`), in rain and snow: one layout per car model (`wiperLayout`, pure: the blades' plane up the glass from its real foot, past the scuttle; two blades in tandem as a right-hand-drive car has them, pivots at the driver's corner and the middle, lying toward the passenger's side and sweeping up to the driver's pillar; `stand` clears the glass's curve) places the blades (`wiperMatrix`; they park below the glass on the scuttle, `WIPER_PARK`, so from the driver's seat they lie along the bottom of the view, not across it: `debug-shots/wiperpark.mjs`), gives the city shader the fans they clear (`wiperFan`, `uWipers` with `uWiperGlass`/`uWiperPivots`/`uWiperBeat`, `wiped` in city.ts: snow off the fan, and the rain's drops gone behind the blade and coming back until it's round again) and the cabin's windscreen the same from inside. The beat (`wiperBeat`): continuous in heavy rain and snow, a rest between sweeps in light rain; each car has its own place in it. Your car carries its own two blades (`CarView.wipers`, `setWipers`; at rest on the race page and in the garage), on while you drive it in the rain; the nearest moving cars' (not buses, not the far models) are one instanced mesh (`WiperSet`; `TrafficSystem.movingNear` gives each one's model and body). From the driver's seat (`CarInterior.glass`, `setRain`): the windscreen from inside, drops running down it standing and up it at speed, beads clinging, each a little lens, wiped by the same fans. `debug-shots/wipers.mjs`, `trafficwipers.mjs`. Wheels press tyre tracks into it (`real/tracks.ts`, `TrackMap`: a 256 m texture wrapped round the camera at 8 px/m that each wheel's move draws into on the GPU, R the track and G its height so a deck above doesn't show the street's; strips coming into view are cleared, fresh snow fills tracks in over about a minute, and the shader draws them as pressed grey snow, between clean snow and dark slush, with tread ribs and a brighter ridge along each edge: `trackAt`). The hills' forest and the fringe's roofs (`Edges.setSnow`), the land round the city and the street bridges' decks (`Sea.setSnow`) whiten with it. Snow means winter: setting it by hand (the debug menu, the story, `?weather=snow` without `?season=`) moves the season to winter, as the forecast only snows then.

## Rain, wet streets, clouds

Weather (`real/weather.ts`): rain streaks in a box round the camera and splashes, both lit from the lightmap (rain shows where there is light); light cones under nearby street lamps scaled by the atmosphere's `haze`; none of it inside covered volumes (`landmarkShelters`: shops, canopies, the station, the viaduct; the rain shader skips them per drop, so it still falls outside a shop window).

**Wet streets:** `uWet` is continuous; the ground wets through over 20–60 s of rain and dries over a few minutes (or the debug menu's Wet streets, `?wet=`). Puddles use the same noise in `city.ts` and `ssr.ts` and act as dark mirrors. Wet buildings (`WALL_RAIN_GLSL` in `real/carRainGlsl.ts`, each part fading out by the pixel's size on the wall before it can turn into a pattern of dots, which the first version's grid of blobs on the glass did): walls darken unevenly, soaking in tall runs that fill in as the rain goes on (`soak`), with a film that catches the sky and the street's light and threads of water wandering down them (`wallRuns`); windows and shopfronts (`wetPane`) have water sheeting down the glass and gathering into threads, and within about ten metres the drops themselves, running with trails and clinging in clusters, shaded as the cars' are (`rainLens`); roofs go glossy (`node debug-shots/windowpeople.mjs <dir> "clock=21:00&weather=rain&wet=1" <spawn> <looks>` photographs facades), and cars get rain (`real/carRainGlsl.ts`, up close, in the mesh's own frame `vLPos` so it rides with the car: beads of two sizes standing on what faces up, each its own size and a little oval; on the sides and the glass drops running down with a trail and small ones clinging; each shaded as a lens, a dark rim, the light pooled at its far side and a glint; a wet film over all; `debug-shots/carrain.mjs`).

**Rain layers** (`real/weather.ts`): `RainSystem` has bending 3-segment streaks in a gusting wind field with sheets in heavy rain; `RainLayers` adds near ribbons, far rain curtains on cylinders at 30/70/140 m lit by the lightmap, and car wheel spray; `StreetWater` adds drips off nearby open canopies and debris tumbling downwind in strong wind (kept out of the cabin of a vehicle you're in, as `Drift`'s petals, leaves and snow are: each `update`'s `keepOut`, main.ts `cabinKeep`). People carry clear umbrellas in rain (`District.umbrellas`, drawn by the crowd as a template per body): each is built where the held arm (`UMBRELLA_HOLD` in `real/people.ts`) puts the fist and skinned to that forearm, and while it rains (the material's `uUmbrella`, set by `Crowd`) carriers hold it up in front of the chest, the free hand keeping to its routine (baked figures: `addFigure(gb, s, true)` with `addUmbrella`). The debug menu's weather cycle (`?cycle=1`) runs a six-minute storm that builds, peaks and clears.

The sky has a cloud layer (atmosphere `clouds`, `cloudLit`, `cloudDark`), and is drawn after the opaque city with the depth test on so the clouds only cost where sky shows.
