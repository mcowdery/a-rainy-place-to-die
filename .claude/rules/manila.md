# Manilaya (Manila): the second city, and street races

Covers how a second city runs on the engine, what Manila is, and the multi-car street race. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## A second world on the same engine

**Cities** (`district/cityConfig.ts`): `?city=manila` on `/` loads `content/manila/`; no parameter is Tōto (`content/world3d/`). `CITIES` says where a city's folder is, where you start (`startSpawn`), its seed, the atmosphere kind and the localStorage prefix for its saves (Tōto's keys are unchanged). `loadDistrictContent(city)` (`district/content.ts`) globs both folders' `.yaml`/`.txt` raw, picks one, and falls back to empty rail, subway and races files when a city has none; the expressway's text comes back in `DistrictContent.expresswayText`. The chunk workers get the city in their init message. The city's districts are the engine's eight kinds (`gen/macro.ts`, `STYLES3` in `plan.ts`), restyled by zones: a new city is new L0, zones, roads, traffic, stamps and atmosphere, not new code, until it needs a landmark builder or a model. `tests/manila.test.ts` loads Manila and Tōto.

**What a city can switch** (`CityConfig`): `ads` (false: no billboards, posters or taxi photo ads; the chunk workers read it, `ChunkBuilder`'s `ads`), `rooftopSigns` (false: no neon channel letters on roofs, `real/signs.ts` `setRooftopSigns`), `tropical` (see below), `sunYaw`, `daylight` and `mountains` (the sky), `startSpawn`, `seed`, `storage`. A city with no expressway gives `routes: []` (`Expressway.loop` is then a stub road far off the map, `buildExpressway` returns an empty group, `ExpresswayTraffic` runs no cars).

**Names** (the user's call, 2026-10-09: the real city's sound but invented names, as Tōto is Tokyo): the game's city is **Manilaya**; every district, avenue and bridge in `content/manila/`, the jeepneys' route boards and the HUD use the invented names (the full list is in `story/names.md`); ids and file names (`?city=manila`, `content/manila/zones/tondo.yaml`, `manila.yaml`) stay as they were. Money is pesos there (`CityConfig.currency`, `src/money.ts`: `money(n)`; one wallet, the city's symbol; the taxi says 'Saan po?' and 'Salamat po'). Radio: HARANA FM is Manila's own station (`cities: [manila]` in `content/radio/stations.yaml`; `loadStations(city)`; its songs also stay in the phone's music app).

**Trees per city** (`district/treeSets.ts`, `CityConfig.trees`): which species go where (boulevards, streets, the bay and river walks, plazas, parks, playgrounds, the shrubs of hedges, pots, planters and the park hub) is a `TreeSet`, read by `real/props.ts`, `openLots.ts` and `dressing.ts` through `treeSet()` (`cityConfig.ts`), set once with `setTreeSet(cityId)` on the page (`main.ts`, beside `configureSky`) and in each chunk worker's init, as `setFoliageVariant` is for the foliage. Tōto's table is the old hard-coded one, unchanged; Manila's is tropical (royal palms and coconut palms with rain trees on the boulevards, mango, rain tree, coconut and banana on the streets, coconut palms on the bay and river walks, bougainvillea for the shrubs), with no cherry, ginkgo, zelkova, pine, dogwood or azalea anywhere. The tropical species (`models/trees.ts`: coconut, royalPalm, banana, raintree, mango, bougainvillea) are appended to `TREE_SPECIES` (the shader puts them in the evergreen group); fronds are two-sided notched strips. Manila's edge forests (`real/edges.ts`, `buildEdges(..., tropical)`) are jungle: bright mixed greens, denser and taller, palm and banana-clump instances, the season pinned to summer in `main.ts`; its hills (`content/manila/terrain.yaml`) rise 4 m a cell to 28 m. **Manila's broadleaves were made unlike Tōto's** (the user liked the palms, not the round crowns): `TREE_SPECIES` has `banyan` (balete: a huge dense crown, buttress roots, aerial roots hanging to the ground; plazas and parks) and `flametree` (Delonix: a flat feathery crown under red-orange blossom and a drift of petals) appended after bougainvillea; `raintree` is now a very wide, flat umbrella on three heavy limbs and `mango` a tall, dense, dark glossy dome (cards over a core, with copper and lime new-growth tips); the tables are palm-dominated (`MANILA_TREES`: about two thirds of the streets and boulevards are coconut or royal palm, flame trees and rain trees the few). Their leaf cards have their own shapes in the shader (`city.ts` `lShape`: fine leaflets for rain and flame trees, a long blade for mango, a broad leaf for banyan), and `trees.ts` builds them with fewer, larger cards (`CARD_K`, `CARD_SZ`, `TWIGS`, `DENSE`): about 880 (rain), 980 (mango), 760 (flame) and 1120 (banyan) triangles, against 1690 and 1890 before. Tōto's table and species are untouched.

**Not yet per city** (found by the survey, still Tōto's in Manila): the Rindō-gumi story hooks in `chase.ts` (`city_garage.front`, `nishihara_danchi.front`), `playtestTasks.ts`, the airfield rectangle (`real/airport.ts`), the radio stations, VN and phone content, the windows' rooms (futon, kotatsu), and the Japanese signs on landmark builders. Traffic keeps left and cars are right-hand drive in both cities (alternate-reality Manila): a right-hand-traffic flag would touch `traffic.ts`, `autoDrive.ts`, `taxiDispatch.ts`, the expressway and every car's seats.

**Tropical** (`CityConfig.tropical`, Manila): no seasons (`season()` is always summer: no petals, no autumn leaves, no snow, and a snow weather set by hand becomes rain), the forecast is `tropicalOutlook` (`forecast.ts`: the dry season, `DRY_DAYS` 40, scorching days of 28-38 °C with an afternoon thunderstorm now and then, then the monsoon (habagat) for `WET_DAYS` 70: grey steady rain, typhoons passing often (a window every 4 days, 40%), and `Outlook.flood` after a day of heavy rain), the phone's weather app speaks Tagalog and English (`weatherApp.ts` `LABEL_TROPICAL`, no kanji), the HUD says monsoon or dry season, and the insects are crickets by night and a gecko's calls (`real/audio.ts` `tropical`, `setTropical`; no cicadas, no higurashi). The sun (`clock.ts` `configureSky`): `DAYLIGHT` is Manila's (up about 5:50, down about 18:10, near the zenith at noon) and `sunYaw` turns the path a quarter so the sun rises in the north and sets in the south over the bay (the engine's compass has the bay south). Working vehicles carry Manila's invented firms (`setWorkLiveries`, `models/vehicles.ts`; `?city` sets it in the page and the workers). The far mountains are a volcano cone to the south-east with a plume and no snow, over low hills (`real/sky.ts` `uSector`, `uCone`, `uPlume`; Tōto's is Fuji). Manila's sunsets are in `atmosphere.yaml` (the summer rules at its end: violet to magenta to blazing orange, big lit clouds, a humid haze; they sit before the `skies:` block, and the `phys` key blends the physical sky). Flooding is drawn: see **Floods** below.

**Boats** (`real/boats.ts`, `CityConfig.boats`: Manila only): bangkas (outrigger boats, six paintings, some with canopies, long-tail engines or sails, lanterns), a passenger launch, lighterage barges, two container ships and two tankers anchored out in the bay (lit at night), all instanced (about a dozen meshes, about 70 craft; only those within 700 m of the camera are placed every frame), moving along loops in the bay and up the Pasig, bobbing on `SEA_LEVEL` with a soft wake, and moored along the banks and the quay; `tests/boats.test.ts` checks every path stays on water and clear of the bridges. They are in the showroom (`models.html`, a Boats block at (1500, 0, 1500)) for review.

**Filipino streets**: the zones' sign words are English and Tagalog (SARI-SARI STORE, KAINAN, CARINDERIA, LOAD, VIDEOKE, BOTIKA...; `real/shops.ts` `WORDS` maps them to trades, first in the list); `court` is an open-lot kind (`plan.ts` `LOT_OPEN`, `openLots.ts` `court`: a painted basketball court with a goal at each end (`hoop` prop), a fence, a bench, lamps and players; the barangay zones leave 9% of their lots as one); the barangay zones' `X` letter is an informal settlement (`*_shanties`: 3-5 m lots of one or two storeys, 3 m lanes, rust, grey, tarp-blue and plywood walls, every home a roof), the shanty's patchwork walls and scrap roofs are the `informal` look (**Floods and shacks**, below); jeepneys are in every zone's `cars:` mix (`models/vehicles.ts` `jeepney`, four liveries, built in the showroom: `models.html`, and **awaiting the user's review there**; take them out of the zones' `cars:` if rejected), and the tricycle is in the mix beside them (**Tricycles and the Tafto Line**, below). There is no expressway in Manila (the Skyway opened in 1999; the 80s-90s city has flyovers and the elevated railway, the Tafto Line).

**Filipino looks** (`CityConfig.filipino`, `filipino()` in `cityConfig.ts`, set with `setTreeSet` on the page and in each chunk worker; Tōto's output is unchanged): driven by zone `look` keys (`zones.ts`, `ZoneLook`): `block` (share of buildings up to 16 m that are unplastered hollow-block houses: grey block-joint walls (`BLOCK_FLAG` in `city.ts`), and a flat roof left unfinished with rebar standing out of column stubs, a half-built wall, a corrugated lean-to weighed with tyres, a drum tank, washing: `real/manilaBuilding.ts` `unfinishedTop`), `roofs` (pitched roofs are corrugated iron in Manila: `ROOFS_IRON`, ribs, tyres and stones), `arch` (arched punched windows, `ARCH_FLAG`), `church` (share of lots 9 m wide or more with a bell-tower, pediment and pilasters: `churchMass`; Intramuros), `vending` (chance of a vending machine per building, 0.14 when absent), and `street` (0 to 1, how much clutter, below). Every Manila building also gets window grilles, caged balconies with washing, iron canopies over shops and roof gear (`manilaBuilding.ts` `front`, `roofGear`). `real/manilaStreet.ts` emits and draws the new prop kinds from `cellDetail`: sari-sari kiosks (hatch, hanging snack packets, bench, umbrella), push carts and barbecue grills, jeepney stops and tricycle-terminal signs, parols (on frontages and street lamps, a lit star), tarpaulins on walls and strung across streets (coloured sheet with bars of print standing for the lettering: no text atlas yet), Santo Niño shrines, garbage piles, a barangay hall porch (flag, notice board) and the covered court's steel trusses (`openLots.ts` `court`). The poles (`props.ts` `poleRun`) are every 20 m, on pavemented streets too, with 13 to 15 wires a span, service drops to the nearest wall, extra transformer cans and cable boxes. Checked in the barangays and Intramuros by `debug-shots/manila.mjs` (with `fly=1` for the roofs); the shop interiors' Japanese copy is unchanged.

**Tricycles and the Tafto Line** (not yet reviewed by the user: the tricycle is in the showroom, six paints, parked and driving, and low model; **take `tricycle` out of the zones' `cars:` if rejected**): `tricycle` is a `CarType` (`vehicles.ts`: `DESIGNS.tricycle` is only its envelope, 2.2 x 1.9 m, for sizes, `vehicleLength`, the low model and the wheels; `PAINTS.tricycle`, `CABIN_STYLE`, `LABELS` and the showroom's records have it; `tests/carInterior.test.ts` skips it, nobody sits in it), its shape is `addTricycle` (`models/tricycle.ts`): a 125 cc motorcycle (`addBike` with `wheels: false`, and `detail` from 0.1 coarsens the bikes' blobs and tubes) on the right, a steel sidecar on the left (the kerb side, traffic keeps left): tub with a rounded nose, bench, chrome poles and bumper, an arched canopy in the paint's pair (`TRICYCLE_ACCENT`) with a valance, a slanted windscreen, a louvred rear curtain, the operator's two-figure number in seven segments on the flank (from `marks`), a plate and tail lamps, and a faceless driver in a cap (not when `lamps: false`, parked). Three wheels: `wheelLayout('tricycle')` gives the bike's two (each drawn from both sides for the hubs) and the sidecar's, so traffic's `Wheels` turns and steers them like a car's; in traffic they have their own driver (about 27-40 km/h, slow to pull away). Zones give them `tricycle: 60%` of the jeepney's weight in the `cars:` lists. **The Tafto Line** (`content/manila/rail.yaml`, one line `tafto`, T01-T08, yellow, a viaduct up Bonifasyo Street, col 15, rows 2.2-21.8 on the west bank, which `roads.yaml` narrows to 20 m as Tōto's roads under its stations; `content/manila/stamps/station_m_*.yaml`, one stamp per station copied from Tōto's (`station_asagiri` east-facing, `station_kaburo` west-facing, alternating) and placed at the end of `placements.yaml`: Balintagak, Monumeno, Blumentrato, Tayumin, Karyedo, Pedro Gal, Vito Cruzan, Libertag; `jp` is the Latin name again, there is no kanji). Riding, trains, platforms and the route picker are Tōto's, unchanged. `tests/manila.test.ts` checks the stations; `debug-shots/trike/trikeshots.mjs` (the model) and `railshots.mjs` (views by `?cam=` or `?spawn=`) look at them.

**Jeepney lines** (`buses:` in `content/manila/traffic.yaml`; the mechanism, hailing and the ride are in [transit.md](transit.md) **Jeepney lines you ride**): four lines on the avenues and radials, each a loop of one bank (the river is never crossed) with three jeepneys in one livery, whose route board is the line's name: KUBAO - DIVISORA (`[26,8,32,13]`), PASAG - KIYAPO (west bank, `[10,13,15,22]`), DIVISORA (`[26,13,32,17]`) and SAMPALUAN - KIYAPO (`[26,2,32,8]`). Their stops (one a side, named in the file) are the jeepney stop sign and bench, and the dressing's random stops keep clear of them (`stopClear`). `tests/jeepneys.test.ts` checks each loop is built on both sides, none overlap, the stops stand clear. **Not done**: the jeepneys in the zones' `cars:` mix can't be ridden; more lines, a line over a bridge (a loop can't cross the river; it needs a path along the bridge road); the stops' signs show names in Latin letters only; the cabin has no steps to the ceiling rail or standing passengers hanging off the back.

**Floods, shacks and shop words** (`real/flood.ts`, `real/shopText.ts`; `tests/flood.test.ts`): `Outlook.flood` (or `?flood=0..1`, or the debug menu's Weather tab: flood auto / 0 / 0.5 / 1; Manila only) raises one murky, rippling water mesh over the built cells (the sea's shader family, `waterSurface` exported from `real/sea.ts`, so the reflection pass treats it as water; the street lamps' lightmap added to it; a 32 m grid whose vertex heights are `MAX_DEPTH` 0.36 m x flood x `FloodField.low`, the lowness by distance to water cells, terrain height and a per-vertex hash, so the streets by the river and the bay flood first and deepest and the ground under 0.025 m of water is simply not drawn; hidden below flood 0.05, indoors and underground; it rises at 0.02 a second (0.5 forced)). `FloodField.depthAt` also feeds `RoadWeather.floodAt`: wheels wade (`wading`, full at 25 cm; `roadGrip.ts` `wheelWater` takes grip and drags hard, about 5 m/s^2 at 20 m/s), `updateSplashes` throws spray in it, and the traffic's `conditions` slow and leave longer gaps. **Informal settlement shacks**: a zone look `informal` (0-1; the four `*_shanties` zones have 0.92) makes a building a home with `INFORMAL_FLAG` (4194304; `buildings.ts`): the wall shader (`city.ts`, the `informal` branch) draws patches of about 0.8 x 1.2 m, uneven, each hashed to corrugated sheet with ribs (galvanised or rusted), plywood, painted planks, blue tarpaulin or rusted flat sheet, with dark seams, rust streaks and stains, a plank door and a lit window; the roof is a near-flat scrap sheet (`ROOFS_INFORMAL`, pitch under 0.7 m) with the iron roofs' tyres and stones. **Shop words**: `ShopAtlas(renderer, text)` takes a `ShopText` (`shopText.ts`: `TOTO_TEXT` the default, `MANILA_TEXT` English and Tagalog: carinderia dishes, SALE MURA, BOTIKA, FRONT DESK, LOAD AGAD...), picked by `CityConfig.filipino` in `main.ts`; a new string in a room goes in the table, not in `shopAtlas.ts`. The windows' upper-floor rooms (`windowScenes.ts`) still have Tōto's.

**Footbridges, brownouts, banners and rooms** (the third pass): **Footbridges** (`content/manila/footbridges.yaml`: `row|col: n, at: cells along`; `district/footbridges.ts` pure, set by `loadDistrictContent` as a registry the page and the workers share; drawn as the prop kind `footbridge` by `manilaStreet.ts`): a covered deck 5.9 m up (5.5 m clear for a bus) over an avenue of 20 m or more, a landing and a straight 34-step concrete stair each way along both pavements (the stairs are solid concrete), parapet and handrails, a gable roof of corrugated sheet on posts (sloped over the stairs), sodium lamps, columns in the median. Walkable: `District.floorAt` ramps the stairs and holds the deck for a walker already up, `blocked` has the rails above one metre, the towers' and the median columns' street-level rects are `extraColliders` (cars and the walker), the deck is a rain `Shelter`; lamps, poles, trees and hedges keep out of the stairs (`underFootbridge`). Sixteen sites by the markets, towers and nightlife; `tests/manila.test.ts` checks no cross street or set piece is in a stair's reach and walks each one. **Brownouts** (`district/brownout.ts`, pure; Manila only): the city is cut into feeders (3 x 2 cells), each may be dark for 40-110 minutes in each 120-minute block by a chance the forecast sets (`outageChance`: dry 1.5%, heat 9%, heavy rain 10-30%, a typhoon 22-82%, flood more); the dark cells go to the shaders as a bit field in one uniform array (`uOut[64]`, `uOutMap`; 24 cells to a float, no texture unit): `blackoutAt` dims the lightmap (`lightAt`), the lamp heads, the shop fronts' lamps, the window lights (a few windows keep a candle's 5%), the shops' interiors (18% run a generator and stay lit), and the sign material's neon; nothing recompiles when it changes. Junctions in the dark have dead signals (`Signals.dead`: the lamps are dark and the cars take turns, 6 s a road). `?brownout=1` (or the debug menu's Weather tab: brownout auto / here / none) darkens the feeders round where you stand; `?brownout=0` has none. Not done: the generators' hum (no sound yet), the rooms behind a dark window don't change, the street-lamp shadow lights are unchanged. **Banners**: wall tarpaulins (candidates: KAPITAN BEN SANTOS - SERBISYONG TAPAT, MAYOR LITO REYES - PARA SA LAHAT...) and banners strung across streets (WELCOME TO BARANGAY PASKO, MALIGAYANG PISTA, ENROLLMENT NOW OPEN, SALAMAT PO...) have real lettering as decals from the sign atlas (`manilaTexts()` is added to `signTexts` when `filipino()`; `tarp` and `jeepstop` are 'swap' props so they're built with the sign builder; in the middle distance the old bars of print), the jeepney stop plates say JEEPNEY / SAKAYAN and TRICYCLE / TERMINAL; parols (star lanterns) are also strung on cables across the narrow streets (`parolline`, two strings with a star every 1.7 m, glow in the lightmap) and hang smaller in the windows. **Rooms and fittings**: `windowScenes.ts` `setSceneCity(true)` (from `main.ts`) puts `MANILA_SWAP` in the place of Tōto's kotatsu, futon, morning exercises, izakaya kanpai and mahjong rooms (the plastic table with an electric fan and laundry on a line, a mat under a mosquito net, the Santo Niño's altar, a drinking session on stools with the videoke machine behind, tong-its) and dresses the yukata, kimono and yakuza figures as Manila's own (`MANILA_WHO`); no noren in the wall shader (`uFil`); station signs have no 駅 or 行き. `node debug-shots/manilawindows.mjs <dir> [kinds]` is the contact sheet.

## Manila

42 x 28 cells of 128 m, about Tōto's size (`content/manila/l0.txt`; the bay is to the south because the engine's sea opens past the map's bottom). The Pasig river runs down col 20 and splits it; the bridges are at rows 3, 8, 13 and 17 (`roads.yaml` `bridges`). West bank: Bagumbayan (the business district, `T`), Ermita-Malate (nightlife, `N`), Intramuros (the walled old town, `O`), South Harbor (`H`). East bank: the Divisoria bazaar (`E`), Binondo (`O`), Sampaloc (`U`), Fort Centre (towers, `T`), Poblacion (`N`), the Pasig docks (`H`). Round them the barangays (`R`, `onlyZoned`, so every residential cell is painted by a zone file: `zones/tondo|paco|santamesa|pandacan.yaml`, with condo belts by the centre, gated villages in the corners and two parks). The zone files are generated from the L0 (the generator is not kept: edit the YAML). Place names are real, brands are not.

**Made for driving** (the user's brief, 2026-10-09: more drivable than Tōto, designed for racing and car gun fights): `roads.yaml` has the ring road (Circumferential Road, cols 4 and 38, row 2), the Baywalk Boulevard (row 22), three cross-town avenues (rows 8, 13, 17) and four radials (cols 10, 15, 26, 32), all 24-32 m with medians, so no part of the city is more than two blocks from a wide road and the grid has no dead ends; zones widen the local streets to 6-10 m (drivable: `roadClass` calls under 3.5 m an alley). The Skyway (`expressway.yaml`) is one elevated loop on the west bank so far. Traffic is generated loops over the whole city (`traffic.yaml`, `auto`), thin (spacing 72) so a race isn't one long queue.

## Street races

**Street races** (`content/<city>/streetraces.yaml`, `district/streetRace.ts` pure, `streetRaceField.ts`, `streetRaceHud.ts`): a field of six or more (the parser refuses fewer than five rivals) through gates over the city's streets. The def: `points` (L0 grid points `[col, row]`, the cell corners where the avenues cross; the first is the start, the last the finish), `laps` (the gates after the start repeat), `pay` by place, `host` (an npc node: E on it opens the challenge, `StreetRaceHud.challenge`), `field` (name, type, paint, `pace`, `power`, `grip`, `gunman`, `aim`, `health`: a `ChaseCarDef`). `StreetRaceState` is the book-keeping: gates count only in order and within `GATE` (26 m); standings are the finishers, then gates passed and nearness to the next, the retired last; the race ends when you finish or are out. `tests/streetRace.test.ts`.

**The field:** each rival is a `ChaseCar` ([car-guns.md](car-guns.md)) in its `'race'` role: `race: { goals, pace, base }`; it lays a route through its next three gates (`planVia`: the GPS's street router joined end to end), lays it again part-way along a street when fewer than 200 m remain (a route laid close to a junction has no room to round its corner and takes it slowly), and drives it with the auto drive's `'race'` style (`autoDrive.ts`: the pursuit's, quicker, corner pull 8, and `lanePath`'s `turnScale` 2.2 so corners are taken wide). `pace` is the rival's own, a little more when behind you and less when ahead (`RUBBER`, up to 10%). A racer shoves through a queue of traffic once it is under 9 m/s (0.2 s), rather than waiting. The grid is two abreast on the road out of the start on the left carriageway (an avenue's median is solid), you in last place. Armed rivals' gunmen shoot at you through the chase's own rules; rival-against-rival shooting does not exist (their gunmen aim only at you). Damage is `ChaseState` used as a ledger (`shoot`, `ram`, `shot`); a rival out is retired from the race and rolls to a stop; you are out if your car is totalled or your health is gone. The next gate has a beacon (`streetrace:beacon`, additive columns) and the GPS route to it (`ChaseHost.gpsAt`). Pay is `pay[place - 1]`. The debug menu's Races tab starts them; `__streetRace.start(id)` / `.race` with `?debug=1`.

**Checks:** `node debug-shots/manila.mjs <dir> [query] [x,y,z,yaw,pitch ...]` looks at Manila; `node debug-shots/manilarace.mjs <dir> [race] [seconds]` runs a race with your car parked on the grid and prints the standings (`RETIRE=1` ends it for a picture of the results); `node debug-shots/manilatrace.mjs [race] [seconds] [car]` samples one rival's speed.

**Known gaps:** rivals still slow to a crawl at some junctions where traffic has queued (it plays as traffic, but costs them seconds); the old circuit on the race page (`content/race/courses/manila.yaml`, [racing.md](racing.md)) is untouched and is not the city.
