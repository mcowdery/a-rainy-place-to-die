# Roads, traffic, the expressway, taxis

Covers traffic and signals, avenues and streets, the Tōto Expressway, taxis, headlights. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## Traffic

**Traffic** (`content/world3d/traffic.yaml`, `district/traffic.ts`, `real/traffic.ts`): cars and taxis drive clockwise loops of cell-edge roads in the kerb lane (keep left) and city buses anticlockwise loops, dwelling at a stop mid-block on each edge, clear of crossing streets (shelter and pole sign on the kerb, at the kerb where it stands: an edge that widens into an avenue has it further out; street lamps, hedges, vending machines, potted plants and parked bicycles keep out of the mid-block window a stop can use). Besides the two core loops, five feeder lines run out of the core on proper streets (traffic.yaml: 学園坂・水道町循環, the Dome line, the port loop to the fish market, 霞町循環, 鷹ノ台循環); the old town and the western suburbs have their own loops (川端循環, 西原・桜ヶ丘循環) on bus streets: everyday streets widened to 12 m with pavements in roads.yaml for them. Besides the authored loops, `auto` generates car loops over the rest of the city (`carLoops`: squares of `size` cells on a fixed lattice where no loop is, cells built on both sides of every edge, every edge a proper street by `roadClass`), so traffic comes with each new district. Only traffic within `SIM` (900 m) of the camera is simulated (the rest waits where it is and drives on when you come back), and the vehicle ahead is found through a 40 m spatial grid, so the cost stays with the cars round you. Driving is the Intelligent Driver Model per vehicle (own top speed, acceleration, braking, time gap): following the vehicle ahead in the lane on any loop, slowing for corners, stopping at red (and at amber when it can), not blocking the box, right-turners yielding to oncoming traffic. Signals (`Signals` in `district/traffic.ts`) run at every grid-corner junction (`Route.junctions`): green, amber and all-red for each road, plus a pedestrian scramble phase at scramble crossings. Signal poles stand only at those junctions, and their lamps are drawn live (`SignalLamps`).

**Wheels** turn: moving vehicles' wheels are drawn apart from their bodies (`Wheels` in `real/traffic.ts`: instanced, one mesh per rim style and side, scaled per vehicle from `carWheels` in `real/cars.ts` or `BUS_WHEELS`), rolling with the speed, the front ones steering with the curve (tan(steer) = wheelbase × curvature); the old bodies have no arches, so their wheels stand out far enough for the rims to show. Parked cars keep baked wheels. Brake lights and body pitch follow each vehicle's acceleration, and the nearest cars have engine sound (revs, gears, throttle, Doppler). Parked cars stay off the cell-edge roads. The walker collides with vehicles.

**Turning:** each route corner is driven as a driver steers it (`TURN` in `district/traffic.ts`: the wheel wound in, held, wound out, a clothoid-arc-clothoid; `TURN_R` 5.2 m for left turns round the near kerb, 8.5 m for right turns across the junction), the path sampled (`Route.path`, `along()` gives position, heading and curvature); a vehicle follows the lane by its rear axle and faces the way it moves (no sliding), slows to each turn's speed (`TURN_PULL`), speeds up as it unwinds and leans out of the turn. Stop lines sit before the turn starts.

**People in the road:** each vehicle looks along its lane ahead of the bumper for anyone there now or by the time it arrives (`TrafficSystem.update(dt, camera, walkers)`: the walker, with their velocity), stops short of them (following them if they're going its way) and sounds the horn after a hard stop or a wait (`honks`, `CityAudio.horn`). Plaza crowds don't stand in carriageways.

## Streets and avenues

**Streets for driving:** three kinds.

**Avenues** (`content/world3d/roads.yaml`, `district/roads.ts`: runs of cell-edge roads with their own width and an optional raised `median`, fed to `planCell3` as a map of edge keys, with the scramble crossing's approaches merged in at 18 m or more): the four round the expressway loop are 32 m (two lanes each way either side of a planted median, 3 m pavements) and run a block past its corners, and Yogiri-dōri, the crossing's avenue a block inside the loop's south side, is 32 m too; Asagiri's own edges are 16-18 m (West Shinjuku's grid). A road with a subway under it is 20 m at most (stations stand 10 m from the road's centre), so Tōto-ōdōri narrows over the Seikō Line's Tōto-Chūō station. Medians (`CellPlan3.medians`, broken at junctions) are drawn kerbed and planted, are solid to collision (`District.blocked`) and to the GPS's driving grid; lane paint follows (a lane line down each side). Street traffic on two-lane carriageways keeps to the kerb lane (`routeFor`).

**Side streets** inside the blocks stay narrow; **alleys** stay walking only. Landmarks along the avenues were moved back from them (the mega-sign, Yoru Mart, the live house, Yasuichi, The Peak, Stella Production; along Yasuhara-dōri, the loop's south side: the police HQ, the law firm, Hotel Toto Inn, Club Shirasagi and your garage, with Kaburo Station 4 m north to make room); `tests/district.test.ts` checks no stamp stands on a road with the avenues at full width.

## The Tōto Expressway

**The Tōto Expressway** (東都高速, `content/world3d/expressway.yaml`; layout `district/expressway.ts`, pure, tested; look and traffic `real/expressway.ts`): a network of routes (`routes`: each a list of L0 grid points, its corners rounded at `radius`, one-way in point order; `loop: true` closes it; open routes join others where their decks overlap; ramps name a route, a `leg` and a `block`; exits run straight on from a route's point into a tunnel; a route's `trim: [start, end]` leaves metres of deck off its ends where nothing runs on past its last ramp or junction, so no deck runs on to its point as a dead stub: `Road.trim`, the cut end closed by an end wall, and at the route's end yellow-and-black boards, amber flashers and the lanes hatched off; the airport and Shiomi branches end at their bridges' island ends with the ramps running on down, the Wangan's west end at the airport junction; `debug-shots/exends.mjs`). A two-way route is two routes, one each way, each with an `offset` to its own left, so their decks stand side by side over one avenue and share piers on the line (in the median); ramps' feet stand in the avenue's inner lane like the loop's, and generated car loops keep off streets with a ramp's walled foot (`Expressway.rampColliders`, `carLoops(..., avoid)`). The plan (docs/city-plan.md) grows it with the city, revisited with each new section: route 1 (1号線, two-way down Kōnan-dōri from the loop's south side) and the Wangan (湾岸線, two-way along the port a block in from the seawall, over Wangan-dōro; ramps into the port on route 1 and at each end, where its stubs will run on to the airport and over the river) are in; route 4 to Kurokami is to come. First built: an elevated one-way inner loop (C1), Shuto-style, 15 m up over the avenues round Asagiri and Kaburo (the grid lines of columns 21 and 31 and rows 10 and 13, all inside the city; its south side runs along Kaburo's southern edge, a block south of Kaburo Crossing, so the crossing and the mega-sign have open sky), its piers in the medians, driven clockwise on the map (keep left), its corners 60 m fillets (`radius`, gradual enough to hold speed; they cut across the junction, so no pier stands in the street there: `piers()` skips them and the deck spans the corner). Four entrances and four exits (`ramps`, by `leg` and `block`: each works within one 128 m block; 240 m long, under 10%, one 6 m lane (`RAMP_HALF`); its foot and walled part in the avenue's inner lane beside the median (`RAMP_FOOT`), so keep to the inner lane for the expressway while the kerb lane runs on past; once high enough it swings out beside the deck, carried on arms from the deck's median piers (no ramp piers in the lanes), and the last 60 m angle into the deck's outside lane, so you merge by driving straight on; arrows painted up an entrance's foot): two pairs on each long side, serving Asagiri and Kaburo. Where a ramp is below 5.2 m it's a walled embankment (a wall to the street: `streetColliders`), with a yellow-and-black crash cushion where an exit ramp's embankment faces the traffic under it; above, piers. Green 東都高速 入口 signs stand on the median's nose by each entrance's foot, with a green ENTRANCE gantry (`entranceGantry`, a cantilever from the median) over it; each exit's foot faces the street with a 進入禁止 no-entry disc and a 逆走 WRONG WAY panel on a post in the median (`noEntry`), and every ramp has arrows painted the way its traffic goes (on an exit they point at anyone driving up it); gantry signs over the deck before each exit. The entrances are places on the map, in the phone's Maps app and in the taxi list (group `Expressway`), and the map draws the expressway in green. **Junctions** (`links` in expressway.yaml: `from`, `to`, `radius`, `cutFrom`, `cutTo`): where two routes cross, a connector deck runs on a circular arc tangent to both at their crossing (`Road.joins` marks the ends that run into another deck: no end wall), and the cuts take off the stubs that would run on across the other deck, so a car turns from one to the other without braking for a right angle. Decks at one height can't cross, so only the turns that don't are linked: loop south side to route 1 south and route 1 north back onto it; route 1 south onto the eastbound Wangan and the eastbound Wangan off to route 1 north; the westbound Wangan into the airport branch and the branch out onto the eastbound (concentric arcs, the outer wider by twice the decks' offset); the Shiomi branch off the westbound Wangan and back onto it. The rest (westbound Wangan to route 1, the Shiomi branch to the east) would need a flyover. Where a gradual corner or a connector cuts across a block, the lots it passes over are open ground (`expresswayReserved`: rects on the arcs' samples more than 17 m from a grid line, passed as reserved ground to the page's `District` and the chunk worker's model alike, like `railReserved`); `tests/expressway.test.ts` checks nothing stands under the deck there. **Slip lane** (`slipLane` in expressway.yaml, Tōto's 16 m; `rampEdgeKeys`/`rampEdges`, applied to the avenues map in `content.ts`): the cell-edge road under each ramp is that much wider (half each side of its line) and carries `EdgeSpec.slip` / `Road3.slip`, which means the traffic keeps its lanes inside the road less the slip lane (`traffic.ts` `half`, `autoDrive.ts` `laneOf`, `real/ground.ts` lane paint, which sets the slip lane off with a solid line along each kerb), so the extra width is a lane of its own outside the kerb lane. A ramp's foot stands in the middle of it, on the kerb side (`Road.foot`: `Expressway` takes the road's width from the city as `roadWidth(edgeKey)`; without a slip lane the foot is the inner lane beside the median, `RAMP_FOOT`, as Manila's), walled below 5.2 m, and then swings in over the through lanes (from 75 m to 125 m out, once it clears them) to the deck's outside lane; the slip lane runs to the junction beside its block. Every cell edge a ramp runs along is widened (`rampEdges`: the foot's block and the ones it climbs over), so no lot stands where it swings in, except where a set piece stands beside the road once widened (the subway station, the garage: `content.ts` leaves that stretch as it was and the ramp's foot there is the inner lane); a ramp climbs over all but its last 50 m and is level with the deck for those (`RAMP_MERGE`), so the two are one open surface there. The lots beside the wider road are planned back from it (the law firm, Hotel Toto Inn and Stella Production moved 10 m north to clear it). The ramp lane is 7.2 m between its parapets (`RAMP_HALF` 3.6), an entrance's mouth has a hatched gore painted in front of the wall's nose on the through-lane side (`ramp()`), the posts of its gantry, side sign and no-entry panel stand on the pavement, the gantry is 8.4 m wide on a post 8.6 m high, an exit is signed twice, 320 m and 120 m before the fork (`gantry`, standing on the deck the ramp leaves), and at the nose where the wall between ramp and deck starts a green 出口 EXIT board with an up-left arrow and the ramp's name stands on the deck's edge (`noseSign`). A terminal deck (the Shiomi and airport branches) can't end sooner than where its ramp's arms stand on it (`RAMP_ARMS`, checked at load): the ramps are carried by the deck's piers, which is why a stub runs on past the ramp's merge to its end wall. **Parking areas** (`plazas` in expressway.yaml: `at` a grid point, `dir`, `from`, `length`, `width`; `Road.plaza`): a raised apron the Shiomi branch's decks end on, 汐見島PA (Tatsumi PA-like), in place of the stub and its end wall. It's a road of kind 'deck' (driven anywhere on it, the decks merging into it, its edge a wall), drawn by `real/expressway.ts` (asphalt, a wall round it, bays with parked cars, an island with the rest building, vending machines and lit windows, floodlights with pools of light, a green sign over the throat, columns in the avenue's median and beyond its pavements), and the lots under it are open ground (`expresswayReserved`). Cars come in down its east side and go out up the west. It stands on columns in the avenue's median and on two thin buildings, one each side of the road under it (`Road.plaza.wings`, solid to the street: `streetColliders`): glazed ground floors, bands of lit windows, a canopied door and a lift core at the middle of each, and a stair tower on the apron over each one's south end, for people to go down to the street (visual only so far: you can't walk up). Nothing is planted under it: the street trees and hedges are left out and the median there is bare paving (`expresswayCovered`, `DistrictModel.covered`, `cellDetail`/`addGround`'s `covered`). The Shiomi ramps stay beside the stub above it. A first design, for review. At the NW and NE corners spurs run straight on into tunnels signed 黒神峠 KUROKAMI and 夕凪峠 YŪNAGI. Each road is a centreline sampled every metre with a height and half-width; `Expressway.at(x, z, y)` is the road under a point at about that height (ramps merge with the deck where they overlap at the same height; every other edge is a wall: `pushBack`, except at street level, a ramp's foot). Drawn as one merged mesh on the city material (deck, lane lines, parapets with rails and reflectors where edges are walls, piers with crossbeams, sodium lamps with additive pools of light at night, the signs, tunnel portals). `ExpresswayTraffic`: cars in both lanes on the IDM, slowing for the bends and for your car in their lane; your car collides with them. Your car (ownCar.ts) drives it on the racing model (its Ground switches to the network once you're up a ramp: `onExpressway`; up there it's `aloft` to the street's traffic; a car left where the city has since changed goes back to its bay). Driving into a tunnel fades to that pass (`race.html?venue=<venue>&mode=free&from=<exit>`), whose menu offers 'Back to the city': `./?from=<exit>` puts you on the loop past that corner, driving. `?debug=1` exposes `__ex` and `__onExpressway(i, road?)`. `tests/expressway.test.ts` (a lap in the outside lane without leaving it, the on-ramp from the street onto the deck, every ramp's foot reachable, off the bottom of an exit, the street's traffic clear of piers, medians and ramps, walls, portals).

## Taxis

**Taxis** (`district/taxi.ts`): H at the kerb of a street with traffic waves one down (`TrafficSystem.hailTaxi`: the nearest cruising taxi coming your way on its loop, within 150 m and able to stop, pulls in at the point of its route nearest you, within 14 m, and waits; it gives up if you walk 60 m off); E gets in, and the list says where to (`destinations`: places and districts, each with its distance and fare: `fare`, ¥500 flag fall for 1.1 km then ¥100 a 255 m, +20% after the last train). The ride runs along the GPS's driving route (`NavGrid('drive').route`) seen from the back seat, sped up (5 s a km, at most 24 s) with the meter ticking; E skips. You pay from the race profile's yen (short, the driver takes what you have). Where no cruising taxi is coming your way (streets without a traffic loop: the suburbs, the old town, the islands) one is sent (`district/taxiDispatch.ts`, 配車): a taxi cruising out of sight is taken off its loop (`TrafficSystem.spareTaxi`, `take`), set down on the road network where you can't see it (`trimToUnseen`: the eight approaches round you, the shortest starting out of view, preferring one that ends with you on its left) and driven along the GPS's driving route keeping left, slowing for corners and the traffic ahead (`Approach`, `gapAhead`), pulling in at the kerb by you; it then waits like a hailed one, and once you've ridden or walked off it goes back to its loop when out of sight (`restore`). `tests/taxiDispatch.test.ts`. `?debug=1` exposes `__taxi`.
