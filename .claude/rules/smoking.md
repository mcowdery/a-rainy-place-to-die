# Smoking

Covers who smokes in the crowd, the smoke, Mack's cigarette and lighter, his idle squat. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

**Smoking** (the user's direction, 2026-10-05: the city is late-Showa Japan, where most men smoked; it's to show, and the smoke is to look real and not like the cold's breath; bosses and politicians smoke cigars; Mack can smoke too).

**The mob** (`district/peopleMix.ts` `smokeOf`, pure, tested): who has something lit is a share by who they are and what they're doing (`SMOKE_SHARE`: about 4 men in 10 standing about and 1 in 7 walking, more of the suits, the labourers and the shady, few women except the nightlife's; never a child, a school's clothes, a uniform at work, or anyone running), a `cigar` for the `boss` outfit (fat cat, politician) and the old yakuza boss under his hat, else a `cigarette`; `cellCrowd` gives it to each figure from where they stand (`FigureSpec.smokes`; no draw from the cell's random numbers), packed in the carry number (+8 a cigarette, +16 a cigar, over the bag, umbrella and straps). The mob's posing is now a shared GLSL chunk, `MOB_POSE_GLSL` in people.ts (the limbs, `pickAct`/`act`, `mobPose`, which sets a figure's skeleton, and `bone`): the mob's material uses it and so does anything that must find a part of a posed figure. A smoker's free hand (the right when the left has a bag) carries it low and brings it to the mouth on its own round (`SMOKE_ROUND`: up, a drag, down, a hold, the breath out; every 8-15 s, a cigar 15-24), whatever else the routine has them doing (smokers have their own, of things that leave the hand free; nobody mimes a cigarette any more: act 10 is gone); not with an umbrella up, a strap held or someone's hand. The arm with the fingers at the lips is solved per body (`smokerJoints`: the hand's grip point, the mouth, and the swing, bend and twist that bring one to the other, in the joints texture's extra texels, `JOINT_EXTRA`, now 20 columns). `real/smoke.ts` `CrowdSmoke` (owned by `Crowd`, filled with the shown chunks' smokers) draws in one instanced draw: the stick itself in the fingers (six-sided: a cigarette's filter, paper and ash, a cigar's foot, band, body and ash), the ember (flaring on the drag, seen across a street at night, hardly by day), **the thread off the tip** (blue-grey, hair-thin and straight for a hand's breadth, then wavering, torn into strands and gone a metre or two up; it is the path the tip has moved along, each bit staying where it was let go, so it follows the hand to the mouth and trails, shorter and wider, behind someone walking; it leans with the wind, `Crowd.wind` from main.ts `windVec`) and **the breath of smoke** after each drag (paler and greyer: a stream of soft puffs out of the mouth that slows, billows and hangs three or four seconds). `SMOKE_GLSL` (`smokeDrift`, widths, alphas) and `SMOKE_FRAG_GLSL` (shapes over `smokeNoise`, a small tiling noise made without a canvas) are shared by everything that draws smoke. Lit by the sky and the street's lightmap as the breath is, so in the dark you see only the ember. The debug menu's People tab: Smoking (`?smoking=0..1`, the mob material's `uSmoking`: how many of those who smoke have one lit). The mob showroom's 'smoking' stage (thirteen smokers and two walking past; the panel has a wind for the smoke); `node debug-shots/smoking.mjs [dir] [show,city]`. 21 smokers among ~450 people at Kaburo Crossing, no change in frame time. Only the street's crowd smokes (set pieces' baked figures could be given `smokes` and would hold it, but have no smoke yet; the people in the windows and the shops don't).

**Mack** (`models/smoking.ts` `Smoking`, the rig's `smoking`; `models/cigarette.ts` the stick, which burns down, and his lighter; `real/smoke.ts` `MackSmoke`; `tests/smoking.test.ts`): **J** on foot lights one up, J again flicks it away, Shift+J changes between cigarettes and cigars (localStorage `citypop.smoke`; the debug menu's Player tab, Smoking; the showroom's first person: J, Shift+J a cigar). Acted, not conjured: lighting up needs both hands free (the gun away): the left goes into his jacket and the right to his pocket, the left puts the cigarette to his lips, the right brings the lighter's flame to its end, it catches as he draws (not lit before), the lighter goes back and the left hand takes it down as he breathes the first out. Lit, it's held low in the left hand between the first two fingers, up for a drag every 8-13 s (a cigar 15-22); when that hand is wanted (a gun drawn, fists up, a bike's bars, a wheel) it goes up to his lips and stays there, puffed at now and then without hands, and the hand takes it back at the next drag once it's free; it burns down (5 minutes, a cigar 15) and is flicked away at the stub (it flies, lands, glows out). The act has the arms last each frame (from the rig's `poseShadow`, through `handTools`: reset, reach, orient, relax), eased in and out of the rig's own pose, the hand moved so the cigarette's mouth end meets his mouth exactly; `smoking.out` (`SmokeOut`: the lit end, its heat, his mouth and the way his breath goes, the flame) feeds `MackSmoke`, which draws the same thread from a history of where the lit end really was (a sample every 60 ms: fainter where the hand moved fast, soft and wide right under his eyes so it's never a hard line up the view), the breath, the ember's glow and the lighter's flame. In first person you see the hand come up at the bottom of the view, the ember's glow and the breath blown out ahead; no smoke in a car's cabin. `node debug-shots/macksmoke.mjs [dir] [cigar]` (the showroom: `__fp.smoke(kind)`, `flick()`, `watch(angle, dist)` a mirror further off, `wind(x, z)`), `macksmokecity.mjs`.

**By himself** (the user, 2026-10-05: he should smoke often without the hotkey, especially idle): `Smoking.auto` lights one after ~6 s of standing about with his hands free (walking counts a quarter), and the next 35-110 s after one is finished; breaking into a run he throws away what he's smoking (the same flick; one he was still lighting goes back unlit) and lights nothing while he runs (the user, 2026-10-06); main.ts sets it from what he smokes.

**The hold** (after the user found the hand odd): held low, the arm hangs at his side as it does anyway (it goes with its swing), only a little more bent, the fingers down and the palm back and in, the cigarette between the first two fingers' middle bones with its lit end out ahead; the elbow goes back when the hand is down and out to the side when it's at his mouth.

**Squatting** (`FirstPersonRig.squatting`, `SQUAT`: the Japanese squat, as the mob's shady: feet flat, knees apart, the thighs about level and the seat clear of the heels, the back leant a little over the knees, the forearms across them and the hands hanging (a first version right down on the heels with the back bent over looked uncomfortable to the user and was raised ~10 cm, 2026-10-06); a cigarette then rests across the left knee): **C** squats and stands; left standing 22 s with his hands free he goes down by himself, and any step or a drawn gun has him up. The rig lets the body down until the feet are on the ground and reports how far his eyes dropped (`eyeDrop`, ~0.8 m), which the page takes off the camera's height (`FirstPerson.drop`). Both idle habits are switches in the debug menu's Player tab, Smoking (localStorage `citypop.mack.idle`). `node debug-shots/restpose.mjs <dir> "" smoke|squat|free` photographs the cigarette hold, the squat and the free camera. Not done: the lighter's flame lights nothing round it, no sound (the lighter's click, the drag), no ash dropped, nobody else's cigarette is ever lit or stubbed out (the crowd's are always burning), and he can't smoke in a car.
