# Racing: the race page, venues, the garage

Covers the car model and its stunts, venues, time trials, drift attack, circuits, shooting and battles on the race page, owned cars, the garage, tuning. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## The car model

`race.html` + `src/race/`: **racing**, on its own venue maps (the city stays the place to explore and travel; racing is street and drift racing on mountain passes, like Initial D, and later Shuto-style expressways). This is the handling prototype. `vehicle.ts` is the car model, arcade-leaning over a real tyre model:

- a planar bicycle model with Pacejka-style lateral force, a friction ellipse, load transfer, rear-wheel drive, a power limit, a handbrake that cuts rear grip, gravity along the slope, and a fixed 120 Hz step;

- `CarSpec` (`COUPE`) and `Assists` (`DRIFT_ASSISTS` and `ROAD_ASSISTS`: countersteer, a slide-angle clamp, a yaw cap, traction help that stops full throttle alone spinning the car unless it's already sideways or you've just used the handbrake);

- a speed-sensitive steering lock, with extra lock only for countersteering;

- **stunts**, each asked for on purpose (the assists stand down for them and come back once the car's straight; `Car.stunt` says one's under way): a **burnout** (throttle and brake *held* together, `BURNOUT_HOLD` 0.25 s, with the car all but stopped, `BURNOUT_FROM`: the fronts hold, the rears spin, `spin` 1; steering walks the tail round the held nose), a **donut** (out of a burnout only: the rears stay lit, with little sideways grip, while the throttle's down under `LIT_SPEED` *and the wheel's kept hard over*, `DONUT_GRACE`, and the car goes round its nose; straighten the wheel or lift and they bite again. It must never come on by accident: the first version lit the rears on any overlap of the two pedals at low speed, and on the handbrake with the throttle down, and left them lit while the throttle stayed down, so pulling away from a junction or a handbrake corner in town spun the car right round; `tests/vehicle.test.ts` 'none of it happens by accident in ordinary driving'), a **handbrake turn** (the handbrake *held* over `HANDBRAKE_HOLD`, 0.3 s, with the wheel over, and no automatic countersteer while it is: the slide the car's allowed, `open`, grows from the assists' cap at `SPIN_OPEN` a second, so the tail comes round further the longer it's held; let go and the turning dies at once, `RELEASE`, and the allowed slide closes again from where it is, `SPIN_CLOSE`, so the car grips up the way it points: a pull of about 0.55-0.85 s is a quarter turn round a street corner; brought past `FLIP_COMMIT`, 120 degrees, it goes on to exactly a half turn from where it pointed when the handbrake went on and is brought to rest there, `flipTo`, however long it's held and however little speed is left, one turn a pull; it turns no faster than `SPIN_TOP` whatever the assists; a tap is still only a drift's flick; never on a bike, `CarSpec.twoWheels`, whose handbrake is its rear brake. The user's word, 2026-10-05: the first version spun too readily and made a 90-degree handbrake corner impossible) and a **J-turn** (backing at speed, over `J_SPEED` 5.5 m/s so a three-point turn isn't one, the wheel over and the throttle down: the nose swings round to face the way the car's going and it drives on);

- reversing that goes where it's pointed: less lock the faster it backs (`REVERSE_PULL`: the steered wheels trail, and full lock used to whip the nose round into a 30° skid), the kinematic blend up to 6 m/s, and the engine slowing it with your feet off (`REVERSE_DRAG`).

## Venues, drift attack, circuits

`course.ts` loads a venue (`content/race/courses/*.yaml`, via `courses.ts`):

- a practice `lot`, a road as a centripetal Catmull-Rom through control points sampled every metre, and a level viewpoint `summit`;

- heights come from a hill function, smoothed along the road (the lot and summit ease in);

- the ground has cut and fill, and grip for tarmac, shoulder and grass;

- collision is the road's guardrails, the lot's tyre walls and the summit rim.

A course file also has a `blurb`, its `atmosphere` (time of day: sky gradient, fog, the one light in the sky with its colour and direction, the fill, stars, whether the sun's disc shows, exposure; the scene and the page draw it), an optional `sea` (level and shoreline: the land beyond falls away under it, a glossy sheet out to the horizon, the shore and the viewpoint's seaward side kept clear of trees) and `trial` medal times (`[bronze, silver, gold]` seconds, up and down; the course tests check that gold beats and bronze trails a careful autopilot). Venues: 黒神峠 Kurokami Pass (`kurokami.yaml`: night, 1.1 km, two hairpins and an S, the shooting range in its lot) and 夕凪峠 Yūnagi Pass (`yunagi.yaml`: dusk on the coast, 1.7 km, five hairpins up the seaward face, the sun setting over the sea). A course's `kind` is `pass` (the default) or `wharf`: 東都埠頭 Tōto Wharf (`wharf.yaml`: night, a container terminal's back lot of 320 x 240 m on the quay, sodium floodlights, gantry cranes, the black water; no pass, only a stub of service road) has `blocks`, container stacks standing on the lot (x, z, w, h, `tiers`; solid: `Course.blockAt`, `inside()` is false in one, `collide` pushes out the shortest way), and the scene skips the forest, drift ring and slalom for concrete, containers (instanced), cranes and bollards.

**Drift attack** (`?mode=drift`, a course with `drift`: `time`, `zones` (`at`, `r`, `mult`) and `ranks` [C, B, A, S]; `driftAttack.ts`, pure): after a 3-2-1 with the car held, drift points (as free drive scores them) count only inside the zone that's up, times its multiplier; leaving a zone you've been in moves on to the next (reaching a later one skips those between, for nothing); done at the last zone or when the time runs out. The zones are rings on the ground (the current one pulses pink, done ones green); each zone flashes its points, the finish shows the score, rank, best (localStorage `rainyplace.race.v1.<venue>.drift`) and each zone's points, and pays `ATTACK_PAY` by rank; Enter goes again. The menu's wharf card offers Drift attack and Free drive; pass-only tests filter on `kind`. `tests/driftAttack.test.ts`.

**Circuits** (`kind: circuit`, `road.loop: true`: the road a closed Catmull-Rom loop (`Course.loop`, `wrap`), flat ground, no viewpoint (`summit` optional), walls at the rail): マニラ湾 Manila Bay Street Circuit (`manila.yaml`: sunset over the bay, 2.75 km; the bayside boulevard as the start straight, then the city's corners; the paddock's pit garages joined to it). Its scenery is `race/circuitScene.ts` (`buildCircuit`, which `buildVenue` hands a circuit to; the sky, sun and sea from `addSky`): tarmac with red-and-white kerbs on the bends, the chequered line and grid boxes, the start gantry's five red lights (`VenueScene.startLights`), jersey barriers with invented sponsors' boards and catch fencing, city blocks round it (instanced, windows lit by the shader from where they are, towers inland), palms, lamps and the sea wall on the promenade, a grandstand facing the pits, jeepneys parked in the side streets.

**The race** (`?mode=race`; `race/circuit.ts`, pure: `gridSlots`, `CircuitRace`: a standing start, laps by distance round the loop (the nearest sample unwrapped; jumps ignored), places, fastest lap, finish), the course's `circuit` (`laps`, `start`, `rivals` (name, car type, paint, skill), `pay` by place): you at the back of the grid, the rivals (RivalDriver on a loop: `laneMax`, a racing line to the inside of each bend, passing whoever is ahead, braking in time for bends at speed (`LAT`, scaled by the car's steering lock), lifting when running wide at a wall; `top` speed, `push` from the race: a little more behind you, a little less far ahead); the countdown under the lights, lap flashes, P/laps/lap time on the HUD, a results card (places with times or gaps, your fastest lap, best kept in localStorage `rainyplace.race.v1.<venue>.gp`) and pay by place. No shooting on circuits. `tests/circuit.test.ts` (the loop, lap counting, a rival lapping cleanly, a field of five finishing three laps).

## Time trials, the scene, the page

**Time trials** (`trial.ts`, pure): `trialPlan` (the grid on the road behind the start, three checkpoints, the finish: into the viewpoint going up, back to the lot's edge going down), `Trial` (a 3-2-1 countdown with the car held, then the clock on real time so slow motion doesn't help; checkpoints count only in order and from close by, so the finish can't be reached by skipping any), `medalFor`, `GhostTrack` (the run at 20 Hz, interpolated) and `loadBest`/`saveBest` (the best time, its splits and ghost per venue and direction in localStorage, `rainyplace.race.v1.<venue>.<dir>`). In a trial the ghost of your best drives alongside (a translucent blue car), each checkpoint flashes its split against your best's, and the finish shows a results card (time, medal, new best or the gap, splits, drift scored); Enter starts again. Trials have no shooting. The venue menu (M; shown when the page opens with no `?venue`) lists the venues with their time of day, length and climb, your bests and medals, and links to each mode. `tests/trial.test.ts`. `scene.ts` builds it: terrain, the road with its lines, guardrails with reflectors, lot lamps, cones and a drift ring, the viewpoint with a vending machine, hairpin lamps, instanced cedars, and a moonlit sky. `main.ts` is the page:

- the showroom's sports coupe (`addVehicle` on the city material, `wheels: false`, its wheels apart from `wheelLayout`/`addWheel` so they roll, steer, spin up with wheelspin and lock under the handbrake) with headlight spot lights;

- a chase camera that trails the direction of travel, so drifts show, and the other driving views (Q, `race/driveCam.ts`: far chase, the cockpit in the car's cabin, the bonnet, the bumper);

- tyre smoke and the HUD (speed, gear, revs);

- drift scoring (angle × speed, a multiplier that builds, banked 1.2 s after you straighten up, lost on a knock);

- timed runs: leaving the lot up the road starts an uphill run, leaving the top starts a downhill one;

- `sound.ts` synthesises the engine, tyres and wind.

## Shooting from the car and battles

**Shooting from the car** (a test in the practice lot only; not in the city, which is undecided). The course's `targets` (`TargetDef` in `course.ts`) are built by `targets.ts`: paper bullseye boards (points by ring, 10/5/2/1; a bullet knocks one back on its hinge and it stands again after 3 s), orange steel plates hanging from a post (5 points; they swing and ring) and boards sliding on rails; each face has its own canvas, so hits are painted into it (splats, holes). `shooting.ts` has the weapons: the pistol (黒星 Type 54: 8 rounds, instant hits with a tracer and muzzle flash, a little aim assist) and the paintball marker (60 balls, automatic; the balls fly at 88 m/s plus the car's velocity, drop and slow, and splat on targets and the ground, as instanced decals). Right-hand drive: the driver's arm goes out of the right window, a wide arc from just ahead (8°) round to 165° behind; the passenger window is across the car, so the driver stays in the seat and shoots through it: aiming to the left moves the camera into the driver's eye (`EYE`), with the cabin round you (the car's cabin, models/carInterior.ts, with a dim fill light that is always in the scene and only dark outside the view) and the weapon held low and right toward the crosshair; in that view the body's lean (roll and pitch from the load) is damped to a third, as a driver holds their head level, so the window keeps the horizon mid-drift (the chase and shoulder views keep all of it); you can shoot only through the opening (tested in the car's leaning frame) (`WINDOW`, a rectangle on the door's plane in the car's frame, so what it frames is exactly what you can hit; `WINDOW_ARC` its angles), with 2.5x spread and a slower rate; the windscreen and the back are no line of fire (the crosshair says so and nothing fires). `ARC`, `sideFor(rel, pitch)` (from the driver's eye to the aim point), `nearestShot` (where the weapon waits while the aim is off every window), `spreadOf`. Spread opens with the slide and the speed. Hold the right mouse button to aim (camera over the driver's shoulder, the crosshair's circle is the spread; the aim is free and stays put in the world while the car turns; a little more countersteer assist while aiming). Raising the gun brings **slow motion** (the world at 30%: car, targets, shots, smoke; the camera, mouse and time-trial clock stay real time; muffled sound, a lower engine, a longer echo, a vignette) while the focus lasts (2.5 s, refilling over 6 s with the gun down; G turns it off); never from the hip. Left mouse fires; without aiming it fires **from the hip** where the camera looks (2x spread, the shotgun's cone 0.05 against 0.04 aimed; no assist, the dotted crosshair for a moment), F switches weapon, E reloads (automatic when empty), T cleans the targets and resets the score. Hits score double while drifting and 1.5x across the car. `gunSound.ts` synthesises the shots (with a slap-back echo), clicks, reloads and hits. Checks: `window.__race.aimAt(x, y, z)` keeps the crosshair on a point, `__race.trigger(true|false)`, `__race.shooting`. `tests/shooting.test.ts`.

**Battles** (`?mode=battle&arms=gun|paint`, downhill unless `&dir=up`; `&skill=` scales the rival's pace and aim): you against a rival, a red coupe on the same handling model driven by `RivalDriver` (`battle.ts`: follows the road in a lane of its choosing, slows for the bends ahead, pulls out to pass you, pushes when behind), with `separateCars` for knocks (each car three circles; pushed apart, momentum traded, spun by where it was struck). Both are armed: you as in free drive (a pistol or the marker, fixed per mode), the rival through a gunman in its passenger seat (`Shooting.seat = 'left'`: the left window is his wide arc, the same window rules mirrored), who shoots when he has a line on you within range after a moment to aim, leads paintballs, and misses by more with distance, both cars' speed and across his car. Shots strike cars through invisible hit volumes (`carView.ts` `hitVolumes`, tagged by `part`: the lower body, the glasshouse, the four tyres standing just proud of the sides, and the heads inside, the driver's and the gunman's; `Shooting.castBodies` lets a round through a window, not the roof, reach a head behind it, and one into the body at a wheel find the tyre; `Shooting.bodies`, `onBody`; the pistol's assist only pulls a shot that would miss, toward a car's body, never a tyre or head) and leave marks on them (`CarMarks`: bullet holes, paint splats).

**Real guns:** `HEALTH` 100, `DAMAGE` 6 a body hit, 12 through the glass; a tyre shot out (the car spins) or a round in the driver's head is lethal; a round in the rival gunman's head silences his gun. A car out loses at once (after 1.5 s to see it go; shot dead, your view darkens red); otherwise the first down wins. The gunman aims at your cabin, so his rare tyre hits are strays.

**Paintball:** each hit you take adds `PAINT_PENALTY` (0.5 s); the lower total wins, settled as soon as the other can't catch up. HUD: both cars' health or paint, 1ST / 2ND, the marker over the rival, a flash at the screen's edge when you're hit (red, or the paint's colour) and a shake for a round; checkpoint flashes say LEADING or the gap; a results card (win or lose, why, both times and penalties, the cars, your hit rate). `carView.ts` also builds and poses the coupes (`buildCar`, `poseCar`, `turnWheels`, `addHeadlights`). `tests/battle.test.ts` (the rival drives both venues both ways cleanly; knocks; two AI cars race side by side to the bottom).

## Owned cars, the garage, tuning, keys

**Owned cars and the garage** (`garage.html`, `src/race/garage.ts`): `catalog.ts` is the five cars you can own (invented makers: Tōkai Kestrel 86 GT the 80s hatchback, Seika Mame 660 roadster, Ōmi Swallow Type-S the coupe, Kaiun Tatsumaki RS rotary, Ōmi Raikō GT-4 turbo AWD), each a `CarSpec` (the handling model has `awd` (the drive's front share), `turbo` (lag; off boost 55% power; `Car.boost`) and `lsd`), a price, a blurb and an engine `SoundProfile` (redline, firing pulses per revolution, buzz; `CarSound.configure`, with a turbo whistle and blow-off); `PARTS` (engine, turbo, weight reduction, tyres, suspension, brakes, LSD: levels with prices and what each does to the spec; `tunedSpec`), `stats` for the stat bars, and the cosmetics (paint palette, `NEON` colours, banners, `PRICES`). `profile.ts` (`DEBUG_RICH`: while true, the wallet is topped up to ¥99,999,999 on every load, for debugging; turn it off for the real economy) is your yen and cars (each with paint, a two-tone's lower colour, parts, `Livery` and neon), kept in localStorage `rainyplace.race.v1.profile` (a new profile: the hatchback and ¥60,000), `buyCar`, `spend`, `earn`, and what things pay (`PAY`: trial medals or a finish, a new best, battles won or lost, banked drift points). The race page drives your current car (its tuned spec, look and engine sound; the battle rival drives the same model, stock, in red), pays into the wallet with a line under the clock, and its menu links the garage. The garage (`garageScene.ts`): a rented shutter unit in a brick railway arch at night (fluorescent tubes that flicker when a train passes overhead, a workbench, pegboard, tyres, roll cabinet, oil drum, a poster, the street and a vending machine outside); orbit round the car; a panel with tabs: Cars (yours to drive, the dealer's to view with stat bars against yours, buy), Tuning (one level up at a time; hovering a part shows its effect on the bars), Paint (and the hatchback's lower colour), Livery (racing stripes and a side stripe from the body builder: `VehicleSpec.livery`; a door number and a windscreen banner as canvas text at `liveryAnchors`), Neon (`addUnderglow` in `carView.ts`: tubes under the sills and an additive pool of light on the ground that rides with the body). Changes preview on the car and are paid for when applied. `?yen=` sets your money on either page. `carView.ts` builds any model with its `Look`. `tests/garage.test.ts` (prices and character, parts, the wallet, every model fully tuned driving down Yūnagi cleanly).

**Driving tuning** (`race/tuning.ts`, for testing: ` on the race page, the district's debug menu Car: tune driving): a panel of sliders over your car's handling (engine, grip and tyres, weight, brakes and steering) as multipliers on its own spec (so each model keeps its character; `Car.spec` is replaceable), and the assists (countersteer, slide cap, yaw cap, traction help) set outright for the race venues and the city apart; the value each gives your car, a live readout (speed, gear, slide, yaw, lateral and longitudinal g, wheelspin), double-click a slider to reset it, reset all, copy settings (JSON, to bake a setup into `catalog.ts`). Kept in localStorage `rainyplace.tuning`, applied to your car on both pages; rivals and traffic stay stock.

Keys: W/S, A/D, Space handbrake (a tap: a flick; held half a second or so with the wheel over: a quarter turn; held a second and more: a 180), W+S a burnout (then steer: a donut), backing up W with the wheel over a J-turn, R back on the road, M venues, Enter restart a trial, free drive: 1 lot, 2 top; H help. A click captures the mouse: it orbits the camera round the car and looks up or down (easing back behind after 1.2 s); I inverts Y, shared with the district (`rainyplace.invertY` in localStorage). `?venue=kurokami|yunagi&mode=free|up|down|battle`, `?at=lot|top|road` (free drive), `?cam=chase|far|cockpit|hood|bumper`, `?paint=0xc01818`, `?invertY=1|0`. Tests: `tests/vehicle.test.ts` covers acceleration, braking, turning, drifting, recovery, slopes and walls; `tests/course.test.ts` covers the grade, continuity, an autopilot driving up and down, and the rails.
