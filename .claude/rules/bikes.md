# Mack's bikes

Covers the three motorcycles, the helmet, riding in the showroom, on the race page and in the city. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## The bikes

`models/bosozoku.ts` is Mack's first bike, the one he takes from the bōsōzoku leader (under review on the showroom's cast pad; `debug-shots/bikeshots.mjs` photographs it alone): an invented late-70s 400 four, the Seika Shiden 400F (紫電), as a gang leader's kaizōsha: a rocket cowl with the headlight in its nose and a tall three-tier smoked screen, oni-han bars, a three-step seat with a tall backrest, takeyari pipes rising behind it, a raised tail with an invented Tōto plate, gold six-spoke wheels, candy purple with white stripes and gold pinstripes (`BikeLook`; painted on the lofted shells' UVs); its own meshes and materials like the shotgun; `steer` turns about the steering axis (forks, front wheel, bars, gauges; the cowl is frame-mounted), the wheels spin about x, and `rider` gives the seat, grips and pegs for a rider. `models/cruiser.ts` is the second, the T2 nod (a bike he buys or builds later): an invented Japanese V-twin cruiser on American lines, the Kaiun Raijin 1600 (雷神): solid disc wheels on fat 16-inch tyres, a wide fork with chrome shrouds under a nacelle and a big round headlight, a fat tank with a chrome console and speedometer and 雷 badges, a 45-degree V-twin with a big round air cleaner, staggered shotgun pipes down the right, deep fenders, a low scooped seat, pullback bars, floorboards; silver (`CruiserLook`). Black is the default and his bike; first person only for now (no helmet). `models/sportbike.ts` is the third, the sports bike: the Ōmi Hayate 900RR (疾風), a late-90s Japanese superbike (fairing shells, twin headlamps under a double-bubble screen, clip-ons, rear-set pegs, an underslung exhaust; black, white, or red and black, `SPORT_LOOKS`: a look's `lower` makes it two-tone, the paint under the shells' middle line and the whole lower fairing in it, `badge` the 疾風 graphic's colour); `rider.tuck` folds his back and neck down over the tank (and lowers the eye), `rider.helmet` puts on his helmet, or a look's own (`SportLook.helmet`) (`models/helmet.ts`, `buildHelmet`, `HELMET_LOOKS` black or red and black: one shell surface in angles round the head's centre (`at(phi, theta)`, an ellipsoid with the chin bar pushed forward), open at the eye port and the neck, vertex-coloured for the stripe over the crown and a two-tone chin bar, a smoked visor proud of the port on its pivots, rubber gaskets round the port and the neck, vents, a spoiler, a dark liner; `FirstPersonRig.setHelmet(look)` fits it to the head bone, seen in third person and in his shadow), and its gun is the Type 54 rather than the shotgun (`BIKES[id].gun`).

## Riding

Riding (showroom first person: E by a bike gets on or off; W throttle, S brake, A/D steer; `debug-shots/fpride.mjs`): `FirstPersonRig.ride(bike)` seats him (the pelvis on the seat, the legs to the pegs or floorboards by the same two-bone IK as the arms, the hands closing round the grips in the steering's frame so they turn the bars, sitting upright) and returns his eye point; X draws the shotgun while riding (`mounted`: `update` seats him and runs the gun one-handed in the right hand, the left staying on its grip: upright beside the head at rest, the arm straight out to aim, the chest turning toward where you look, the flip-cock after each shot; raised, the gun points at what's under the crosshair (`AIM_RANGE` along the view: one-handed the arm goes straight from the shoulder toward it, two-handed the gun turns about the grip; `models/crosshair.ts`, the overlay in the showroom and the city whenever the gun is out); the hands close overhand round each grip's own axis (`Bike.rider.gripAxisL/R`: out on straight bars, back on pullbacks); `seatBody` gives the eye point to put the camera at first; `debug-shots/fpridegun.mjs`); the showroom's ride is a kinematic sketch.

**On the race page** (`race.html?ride=cruiser|cruiser-silver|bosozoku|hayate|hayate-white|hayate-red`, any venue and mode; `race/bikeRide.ts`): the bike runs on the racing model itself (vehicle.ts is a bicycle model: a motorcycle is the real thing; `BIKES`' specs, `CarSpec.size` for its walls), so it grips, slides and drifts like the cars (the handbrake, power on; the tuning panel tunes it); `BikeRide.pose` lays it over into the lateral acceleration (up to ~49 degrees), pitches it with the ground, turns the bars with the steering (countersteer shows) and rolls the wheels; Mack sits on it with the bike's gun (`BikeRide.mount`: its gun to hand and its helmet on; `unmount` gives back the gun he had on foot) (X draws it, the right button raises it with slow motion as the cars' guns do, the left fires: `Shooting.blast`, the gun's `pellets` and `spread` from his muzzle at what's under the crosshair, scoring the lot's targets); Q switches first person (his eyes; the default) and third (behind the bike, his head shown: `FirstPersonRig.setHeadless`; over his right shoulder with the gun up); the car's headlights move onto the bike's lamp, its meshes hidden. `debug-shots/racebike.mjs`.

**In the city** (district/main.ts): your black cruiser and the black Hayate stand in the garage's middle bay either side of your car (`cityBikes`; `?bike=home` puts them back), each an `OwnCar` with `bike` set (the same racing model, district collisions, expressway deck, traffic seeing it and saved spot, `citypop.city.bike` and `citypop.city.bike.hayate`; no damage yet), drawn by `BikeRide.from` with the city's own Mack (`onPose`); E by one gets on (first person: his eyes; Q third), X draws its gun, the right button raises it and the left fires; a tunnel on one goes to the pass on it (`&ride=<id>`). `?debug=1` exposes `__ride(id)` and `__bike`; `debug-shots/citybike.mjs <dir> "" <id>`.

The bikes are all built on `models/bikeKit.ts` (the `Bike` they return, plate and gauge textures, rods, pipes and lofted shells).
