# Editions: standard, uncensored, demo

Covers the three builds, what each may contain, overlays from adult/, the demo's hidden art. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

**Editions** (`src/edition/`, `types.ts`): three separate builds. **Standard** (the story, censored, still adult leaning; `vite`, `vite build`, tests, the vn:keys script), **uncensored** (`--mode uncensored`: the standard edition plus explicit images and sex scenes laid over it from `adult/`) and **demo** (`--mode demo`: the gameplay only, for audiences who want none of it: no VN scenes, only KAIWA's welcome on the phone, no story nodes to use, and Kaburo's revealing ads left out). The mode points the `@edition` alias (vite.config.ts; tsconfig maps it to standard.ts for typechecking) at `standard.ts`, `uncensored.ts` or `demo.ts`, and each provides the build's story content (`story.ts`'s globs of content/vn and content/phone; the demo imports only kaiwa.yaml), its overlays and its Kaburo ad art (`art.ts`'s glob, or the demo's own glob with the files in `demoArt.ts`'s `DEMO_HIDDEN_ART` excluded; the demo must never import `art.ts` or `story.ts`, whose globs would bundle everything), so a build only contains its own edition's files (checked by building: dist-demo has no story and none of those images). Code that only needs the name uses the `__EDITION__` define (`src/edition/globals.d.ts`): the chunk workers' ad list is `districtAdsFor(__EDITION__)` in models/ads.ts, so workers and the atlas agree on indexes. In the demo, E works only on what the game handles itself (`gameplayNode` in main.ts: your garage, sleep hotspots, stations and elevators, walk-through doors, race hosts); story npcs, doors and hotspots offer nothing. The uncensored edition's files are in `adult/` (git-ignored here; its own git repository, backed up to the user's NAS, never pushed to this repo's GitHub), mirroring the main tree: `adult/content/vn/<story>/scene.json` overlays a VN story (`vnOverlay.ts`: a base frame's image, video, size, title, bubbles, choice text and hotspot geometry, and new frames, fr900 and up, in runs that lead back to where the base frame went), `adult/content/vn/<story>/assets/` and `adult/content/phone/media/` come before the base's files of the same name, `adult/content/phone/<contact>.yaml` overlays a contact's beats (`phoneOverlay.ts`: messages, and replies' text and then). An overlay can never change targets, flags, conditions or entry points (it's an error, and that story or contact plays as standard), so both story editions play one story and share saves. The uncensored district opens with an age check (`ageGate.ts`, remembered in localStorage `rainyplace.ageConfirmed`). `tests/edition.test.ts` checks the rules, the demo's art and story, and whatever overlays `adult/` holds. Claude doesn't write the explicit scenes (the user writes them in Cursor with Grok, from Claude's brief); see `story/style.md`. **Revealing art and models are not in this repository** (it is public): the 17 ads of `DEMO_HIDDEN_ART` and `mack_nude` live in `adult/assets/ads/kaburo/` (sources in `adult/assets/ads/source/`) and `adult/assets/characters/`, and `uncensored.ts` adds them to what standard has; standard and the demo have no file for them, their ad list drops them (`districtAdsFor`, models/ads.ts: so the Love District shows ordinary ads outside the uncensored build) and the wardrobe offers only outfits whose model exists. **The bare woman's detail** (nipples and their colour, pubic hair, the marks at the foot of the cleft) is built by hooks (`real/mobBare.ts`) that `adult/src/mobBare.ts` registers; `src/edition/bare/uncensored.ts` loads it, the `@bare` alias (vite.config.ts, tsconfig `paths`) picks that or the empty `none.ts`, and `build:all` checks the standard and demo builds hold none of it (the look tables' names). The ad briefs' revealing items are in `adult/assets/ads/briefs/`. New art that shows nudity or is strongly suggestive goes in `adult/assets/ads/kaburo/`, in `DEMO_HIDDEN_ART` and in the demo's glob; a cast model with nudity (Mack nude) in `adult/assets/characters/`, `DEMO_HIDDEN_CHARACTERS` and the demo's model glob (`Edition.characters`: the cast's models come from the edition, models/characters.ts reads them there).
