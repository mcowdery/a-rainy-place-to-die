# The mob: bodies, outfits, colours

Covers the passers-by as figures: the sculpted bodies, outfits and footwear, the palette, the mob showroom, modelled figures. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## The mob

`people.ts`: **the mob**, the city's passers-by as faint, dark, see-through figures (after COM3D2's "transparent man"; shades of black, charcoal and dark navy, wine, olive and brown in `GHOST_COLORS`, brighter colours from old content darkened by `mobShade`). Each body type (man, woman, child, elder) with each hair (short, long, bun, fedora, cap, none) and **outfit** is a template (`TEMPLATE_COUNT`, `templateIndex`; the umbrellas follow, one per body). Outfits (`district/peopleMix.ts`, `FigureSpec.outfit`, `outfitOf`): `plain`, `long` (a long coat or skirt), `suit` (a jacket, the shirt's V and a tie, a briefcase; for women a pencil skirt and a shoulder bag), `maid` (women only: a rounded bell skirt with lace under the hem, the apron wrapping the skirt's front with a frill, its waistband, bib and straps, the bow and tails behind, a white collar, puffed sleeves with cuffs, the katyusha arching over the head), `school` (a pleated skirt and sailor collar for girls, a school bag; a child's randoseru), `kimono` (robe, obi, hanging sleeves; a woman's obi bow), `yukata` (light cotton, a dark obi, a bow for women and children), `work` (a hard hat and a hi-vis vest with reflective bands), `police` (navy, a peaked cap, the duty belt) and `otaku` (a rucksack; it was `backpack`), and eleven more that only the sculpted generation draws (below; the classic templates draw each as the nearest of the first ten, `CLASSIC_OUTFIT`, and no mix has them yet). Garments that wrap part of the body are open arcs (`TemplateBuilder.loft`'s `arc`). They're mixed by place like the cars: `DISTRICT_PEOPLE`, a zone's `people:` in its file (Asagiri's offices in suits, Host Street, Denkō-chō's maid café lanes and otaku, the campus and student town in uniforms, the temple quarter in kimono, yukata by the river and at Shiomi-jima's events, hard hats at the port, a few police about), `CITY_PEOPLE` elsewhere; a body that can't wear an outfit falls back to everyday clothes, and a parent with a child wears their own clothes. They're built once from lofted cross-sections (torso, legs, feet, arms, mitten hands, an egg head with a jaw) bound to a skeleton of eleven bones plus a root and two feet (held nearly level through the stride, so toes and heels stay out of the ground), in its bind pose; the skeleton, the loft builder and each body's proportions are `real/mobRig.ts`.

## The showroom and the sculpted bodies

**The mob has a showroom of its own, `mob.html`** (`showroom/mob.ts`; it loads in a couple of seconds, the model showroom no longer has People rows): each row a stage with a backdrop (each body turned round, women's hair, the others' hair, poses, outfits front and back, a street of people walking and standing), close-ups, M the generation (new, current, or both with the current a row behind), 1-3 the backdrop, K the look, N skin, T standing still or going about the routine (the material's `uStill`); `?gen=&light=&look=&skin=&view=&t=` (and `?lift=4`, which brightens the figures without making them see-through: for judging shapes in shots), `__focus(name)`, `__view(...)`; `node debug-shots/mobshots.mjs [dir] [query] [views]` photographs every view of both generations.

**The sculpted generation of bodies is the city's now (2026-10-04: `mobShape` defaults to 'shaped', on the main thread and in the chunk workers alike; 'classic' stays for the showroom's comparison and its tests). In the city they are all black and see-through to start (the debug menu's People: `ghost`, the default; `color` shows them in their colours, near-opaque; `?mob=ghost|color|solid|rim|lit`), and the new outfits are in the districts' mixes (district/peopleMix.ts; shorts, the down jacket, nurses and doctors in none yet: they want a season or a place). At Kaburo Crossing the crowd went from ~410k to ~800k triangles with no change in GPU time (16.1 to 16.4 ms headless at 1600x900; `node debug-shots/mobcity.mjs [dir] [query] [spawns]` shoots a spawn and prints the crowd's figures, triangles and frame times). A teen has an umbrella of its own (a row an umbrella, as the joints). The same faceless mob, with the body anime draws** (the user's direction, 2026-10-04: style and body shape "like COM3D2's models", "more sexy than chibi cute", still faceless, and not colour; a first pass with realistic proportions, and a second with colour, cel shading, outlines and eyes, were both turned down). `real/mobShape.ts`, `buildShaped`; `setMobShape('classic' | 'shaped')` picks which the templates are built as, before anything is built, the caches keyed by it; the skeleton is the same, so one material poses both. Its shapes: a woman is an hourglass (wide hips set back, a small high waist, the ribs and chest carried forward, narrow sloping shoulders; torsos through more sections, smoothed, then pushed out where a body has form, `Bulge`: the bust, soft buttocks, the hollow of the back; the same field is applied to whatever is worn over the torso and to the tops of the legs. The bust is shaped in the torso itself, not added forms (the user turned down separate domes: "balls"): each breast one bulge with a longer reach above its centre than below (`up`: a long upper slope into a round underside) and a domed top (`round`), on a torso with its points close together across the chest and few round the back (`loftAt`, `CHEST_ANGLES`) and sections every 1.5 cm through it, so it doesn't come out pointed), her legs (`SHAPELY`) full thighs tapering to slim knees and fine ankles, standing close together (the legs run in from the hip joints: the knee and foot only turn about x, so their pivots' x doesn't matter), in heels (`PUMP_ROWS`, `HEEL`), thin arms hanging a little inside the shoulder joints (narrower shoulders), small hands without fingers (a palm widest at the knuckles, the thumb's base a bulge), a slim neck, the trousers from the waist; a man wide shoulders tapering to narrow hips, his top worn out (its own loft from its hem up over trousers that stop under it); heads a little over life size (`HEAD_K`), faceless, anime in outline: a wide round skull, full cheeks running down to a small pointed chin, a hint of a nose in profile, ears where the hair leaves them out; hair in anime's chunks: a thick cap over the skull from a hairline (`shell`, `HairStyle`, its nape cut ragged by `spike`), and locks (`lock`: a four-sided blade along a path in head units, tapering to a point; `onHead` gives a point on the skull by angle and height): bangs of uneven length to the brows, longer at the temples (`fringe`), layers lying on the cap whose ends flick out round the sides and back (`layer`: a crop, a bob, a man's hair), a long lock in front of each ear, long hair down the back as seven broad locks ending in points, a high ponytail and twin tails each a bundle of pointed locks, a bun lofted, three more hair kinds (`bob`, `ponytail`, `twin`; the classic generation draws them short or long); a shorter skirt and bare legs under it. Skin is tagged in the shade (+100) so the material can lift it apart from the clothes (`uSkin`; 1, the default everywhere, leaves it as dark as the rest). About 1.8-2.3x the classic triangles a figure (~2,700 a man, ~3,500 a woman against ~1,500), which cost nothing measurable at the crossing when they went into the city; a far LOD is still worth having if a busier place shows it. Longer legs would need the skeleton's joints moved (the hip and knee heights are `mobRig.ts`'s, shared with the classic templates and the material's `uPivot`).

## A woman's body

**A woman's hips and legs are one surface** (2026-10-05; the user: in trousers she looked as if she wore a diaper, and asked for it smoother and for a more shapely bottom with the cleft's line). They were three shapes, a pelvis closed off in a bowl with a leg's tube pushed up into it either side, which left a crease from each hip to the crotch like the leg of a pair of briefs, each shaded apart. Now (`buildShaped`, the woman's branch): her chest is lofted as before down to the waist (`CHEST_ANGLES`), and below it rings of `HIP_ANGLES` (a point on the middle in front and behind, close together either side of the middle behind; `HIP_ROWS`) carry on down to where the legs part (`CROTCH`); from `HIPS_PART` down each ring draws in from the hips' section to the outline of the two thighs there (`rayEllipse` along the same ray, with what joins them between: `PART_*`, broad in front so the front of her trousers is smooth, narrow behind); her legs (`LEG_SEG` 12 round, the thigh through more sections) stop at the crotch, the inner points of each one's top ring moved to the middle and shared by both; and `zip` joins rows of different counts through shared vertices (waist to chest, each leg to its half of the lowest hip ring), so it all shades as one. Her thighs don't meet: from mid thigh up to the crotch each is slimmer on the inside, its outside where it was, leaving `THIGH_GAP` 2 cm between them (the user, 2026-10-06: 'usually there is a little bit of space in between the legs on female characters'; they used to touch there). The crotch is at `CROTCH` 0.825 of the man's heights (the user found 0.8 low), with `HIPS_PART` left at 0.875 so the seat stays as deep as it was (drawn in from higher up it looked smaller and pointier: the user's choice of the two).

**How the hips bend with the legs** (`thighShare`, `THIGH_LINE`, `THIGH_BAND`; the user asked for less deformity with the legs apart, in a split, running): a point on the hips or at the top of a leg goes with its thigh by where it is, half at a line from the crotch up and out to a little under the hip joint's height at the side, through a band that's short at the crotch and tall at the side and over the buttock (so a raised leg stretches the buttock instead of folding under it); the points on the middle go with the pelvis alone, and the line under her between the legs starts well behind the hips' smooth front (`PART_TUCK`: further forward it stood out as a flap when a thigh rose). It is two bones blended linearly, so a leg turned far out still pinches the top of the thigh a little: there's no twist bone to spread it. `node debug-shots/bodysheet.mjs <dir> hips,hipsclose` poses the bare body through a run, a high step, the legs apart, a split, a squat and a chair to judge it.

**Her bottom**: over the plain seat every body has, ordinary clothes (trousers, shorts, skirts) show it round: `ROUND_SEAT` 0.6 of `CHEEK` with the two sides run together, no cleft and no line (the user's choices, 2026-10-05: first, after seeing the cleft on everything, 'bloomers and track pants are perfect', the others to be like them; then, from the showroom's row of fullnesses, 'round' for size); teens keep the plain seat alone. In full it is `CHEEK`, a bulge each side over the seat, taken by the body with `cleft` (where the two sides overlap a vertex takes the nearer one's push alone, so they meet in a crease: `bodyField`; what's worn over it takes `field`, run together and never less than the body's, so nothing comes through) and the middle column shaded a little darker (`CLEFT_SHADE`): only for `FORM_FITTING` outfits, which for now is nothing on (`nude`, `nude_heels`).


## Teens, footwear, outfits and colours

**The sculpted generation is the one the user is keeping** (2026-10-04: "I want to stick with these and not the make human models"), refined a request at a time: a head-size control in the showroom (`setShapedHeadScale`, `?head=`, 92% to start), men's and boys' hair one ordinary short cut with no locks (`H_SHORT`), a little girl's twin tails short bunches with a bobble.

**Teens** (`isTeen`, `TEEN_SCALE`): a woman or a man in the school uniform is built as the adult, slighter (a girl's bust less, the head a little larger), then scaled down whole with its joints (about 8-9% shorter), and the material has a row of joints for each after the four bodies' (`TEEN_ROWS`, `teenRow` in people.ts), so they pose as their own size; the schoolgirl wears knee socks.

**Footwear** (`footwearOf`): heels only with a woman's suit (`SHAPELY`'s raised ankle; every other woman's leg is `FLAT_LEG`); else a woman's flats, everyone else's shoes, work boots with the hard hat, and sandals with a kimono (white tabi) or a yukata (bare feet); heels also go with office clothes and the evening dress; the showroom's feet close-ups show them.

**More outfits** (2026-10-04, sculpted only): `dress` (a one-piece to the knee, a sash, short sleeves), `mini` (a short tight skirt, knee boots), `gown` (an evening dress off the shoulders: the torso's skin above the bust), `shorts` (a T-shirt; `BARE_LEG`, the shorts lofted over the thighs), `hoodie` (the hood down behind the neck, a pocket), `office` (a white shirt and tie or a blouse and pencil skirt, no jacket), `track` (the school's track suit, red: a stand collar, a pale stripe down arms and legs) and `gym` (school gym clothes: a white short-sleeved shirt with a red collar over a girl's red bloomers or a boy's short shorts, white socks and shoes), both teen-sized on a woman's or man's body like the school uniform (`isTeen`) and worn by children too, `nurse` (whites that stay pale in the one-colour look: a woman's dress to the knee, a man's tunic to the thigh over trousers, short sleeves, a red cross on the chest and a woman's cap with its cross: the user's choice, 2026-10-04; the hospital's own sign keeps its green cross), `doctor` (a white coat worn open, white in every look like the nurse's whites, a dark blue stethoscope round the neck), `apron` (shop staff: a red bib apron and a cloth over the hair, red whatever the figure's colour: the shade's tag 5; tag 6 is the nurse's cross, red; 7 the otaku's dark green rucksack and straps (each strap goes all the way round: chest, shoulder, pack, under the arm), 8 the school's red (track suit, bloomers and gym shorts), 9 the evening dress's dark red, 10 a yukata's golden sash and bow) and `puffer` (a quilted down jacket); `TROUSERED` and `BARE_ARMS` say how each dresses the body's own legs and arms; the showroom has them as a second outfits stage ('new outfits', with waist-up and feet close-ups).

**The otaku** has glasses (a frame, pale lenses, arms to the ears) and the rucksack's straps down the chest, and holds them: both hands on the straps standing or walking (`STRAP_HOLD`, `holdsStraps`: only when the generation set is the sculpted one; +4 in a figure's carry number, the material's `straps`). Lenses, the nurse's whites and the doctor's coat are the shade's tag 4: pale whatever the figure's colour, so they show in the one-colour look (the stethoscope is tag 2's dark blue against the coat).

**Holding hands** (`holdHands(a, b)`, `handAt`, `FigureSpec.reach`): two 'hold' figures placed so their hands coincide (whoever's hand hangs lower raises it to the other's: a child's up to a parent's; worked out on the templates of the generation set, teens too; the reach rides in the walk's first number, `aWalk.x`; a holder's body keeps still); the showroom's 'holding hands' stage.

**Every outfit has colours of its own** (the user's pattern, 2026-10-04: parts tagged in the shade, `TAG_COLORS` in people.ts, tag x 100 + shade, which keep their colour whatever the figure's tint and through the one-colour look, so an outfit reads at a glance on a black figure; and all clothing is coloured: everyday tops, bottoms and suits are tags 16-18, which take a colour from `CLOTH_TOPS`, `CLOTH_BOTTOMS` and `CLOTH_SUITS` by the figure (a hash of its tint and fade seed), so plain clothes differ from person to person; and the body is coloured too: skin (tag 1, a muted tone a little lighter or darker by the figure), hair (19: black and dark brown, an elder's grey) and shoes (20).

**The palette is a noir city's in late Showa Japan** (the user, 2026-10-04: "a lot of your coloring is very bright colors"): muted, deep and dusty, given as sRGB hex in people.ts (`hex`), no bright or fluorescent colours. The mob showroom now opens coloured and solid; **the old look is a toggle**: B everything black and see-through as the mob first was (the material's `uBlack`: clothes and all take the one colour; `?black=1`), O see-through only (the ghost look), C the body (skin, hair, shoes) all one black): a tan trench coat or long skirt, a suit's white shirt, red tie and brown case, a maid's whites, a sailor uniform (white top, navy skirt, collar and socks, a red scarf; a boy's white shirt; a child's red randoseru and yellow cap), a violet or navy kimono with a pale or brown obi, a pale yukata with a golden sash, a hi-vis vest with pale bands under the orange hard hat, police blue, a sky-blue dress, a pink mini skirt, a white tee and tan shorts, a grey hoodie, office whites with a red tie or a navy skirt, a blue down jacket.

**Together** (`GhostBuilder.seeds`): baked figures that fade and stand within `TOGETHER` (1.25 m) of one another, up to four, take one fade seed, so pairs and little groups in set pieces and the showroom come and go as one (the city's crowd already gives each group one seed and one walk). Walking parents and children in the city walk side by side; they don't hold hands yet.

## Modelled figures (MakeHuman), set aside

**Modelled mob figures: MakeHuman (tried the same day, then set aside; they stay in the showroom).** After two lofted passes were turned down, the user chose figures made from real models, and of those MakeHuman ("let's go with the makehuman route"; "as many unique mobs as possible while staying Japanese looking"), drawn in **the ghost look in one black-like colour** (the mob showroom's default: `look` ghost; the material's `uFlat`: rgb and how much of it replaces every figure's tint and shade; and `uEdge` 0: how much denser a figure is toward its silhouette, 0.2 in the district, which on a modelled figure draws every fold and crease as a dark line, so the showroom's is even all over; G cycles it). `scripts/blender/mob_figures.json` lists each figure (a body by `mpfb_base.py`'s arguments, Japanese by default: the Asian body and skins; a hair asset; clothes); `python scripts/blender/mob_figures_generate.py [--women 34 --men 28 --students 8 --children 6 --seed 7]` writes the generated crowd into it (seeded: ages, builds, hair and outfits drawn from pools, no two alike; assets that come through the triangle cut in shards are left out of the pools: a qipao, `cortu_cargo_pants`, `toigo_tiered_mini_skirt`; prefixes `mw` women, `mm` men, `ms` students, `mk` children; the hand-written `mhbody_*` nude body types are kept), and `python scripts/blender/mob_figures.py [names] [--previews]` builds them: `mpfb_base.py` to a GLB in `debug-shots/mobmodel/tmp`, then `mob_from_model.py` to `assets/mob/<name>.json` (~15-20 s a figure; a failure is reported and skipped, a figure with far more triangles than asked listed to look at). `scripts/blender/mob_from_model.py -- <in.vrm|.glb|.fbx> <out.json> [--body woman|man|child|elder] [--tris 3200] [--hair 0.4] [--height 1.68] [--preview <absolute .png>]` is the converter (three skeletons, `RIGS`: this project's cast and `mpfb_base.py`'s output on the Unreal mannequin's bones, VRoid's VRM, Rocketbox's Biped FBX; a model bone's part is read from its name, `role`): it drops brows, lashes, eyeballs, teeth and tongue, deletes transparent faces and skin a garment covers, brings the arms down to hang (`ARM_OUT`) and the legs straight under the hips and bakes that, **blanks the face** (`blank_face`: the mob is see-through, so every layer of a face drew its outline: faces of the face hidden from in front are cut, the openings that leaves (the eyes, the lips) are filled, and the region is smoothed so the lids and lips go and the brow, nose and chin stay as shapes), carries the weights onto the mob's fourteen bones (the two strongest kept; hair below the neck goes with the spine, a skirt below the hips with the thighs), tags hair, skin, bottoms, shoes and white garments for the shade, cuts hair and the rest down on separate budgets, and writes the mob's frame scaled to the height asked with the model's own fourteen joints. `real/mobModels.ts` reads the JSON; `registerMobModel(doc)` in people.ts gives it a template index after the umbrellas' and a body of its own in the material; `FigureSpec.model` draws a figure with it.

**Every body's joints are a texture now** (`mobJoints`, `tJoints`: a row a body, the four lofted ones then the models, a texel a bone, the arms' bind angle after the bones; the shader's `pv(i)`; it replaced uniform arrays, which ran out: `MAX_MOB_MODELS` 252), one texture for every mob material, rewritten when a model is registered. The mob showroom opens on a street of all the models walking and standing as the mob does, then a line-up per family in rows (orbit round for their backs); C switches one colour for everyone, `?one=0&look=solid` the old look. MPFB's data folder (`%APPDATA%/Blender Foundation/Blender/5.2/extensions/.user/blender_org/mpfb/data`) holds the CC0 MakeHuman packs (hair, dresses, skirts, shoes, hats, suits, shirts, pants; one kimono, which also stands for a yukata; no school uniform: students wear a shirt or sweater over a short skirt and knee socks, or trousers); `assets/mob/CREDITS.md` records the packs and what was tried and turned down (Rocketbox: Western-looking; VRoid: set aside, licence unconfirmed; **a realistic figure has to look Japanese**). Not done: the district doesn't use models yet (the crowd picks lofted templates by body, hair and outfit; models have no umbrellas and no far LOD, a model's body slot doesn't get the elder's stoop or the child's stride, and the JSON is ~150 KB a figure: a binary format and loading on demand before a hundred of them ship); Japan-specific garments (a sailor collar, a school blazer, a narrower yukata sash) would be modelled in `garments.py` or made into MakeHuman assets. Figures stand 0.15 m up on the raised pavements and plazas (`PAVEMENT`; `FigureSpec.y` is above that), and on open ground at its own surface.

## The shady ones

**The shady ones** (2026-10-05, to make the city feel more dangerous; `real/mobShape.ts`, the showroom's 'shady' stage): `yakuza` (a suit open over a loud shirt, a chain, sunglasses; by hair a punch perm, a crop, slicked back, or the old boss in white under a hat), `chinpira` (an aloha shirt, sunglasses, sandals), `irezumi` (tattooed arms and shoulders, an undershirt and belly band; summer), `bosozoku` (the long embroidered coat, headband, mask, a regent), `boss` (a heavy body in a double-breasted suit, bald or a comb-over; with a cap or hat a politician in a sash and white gloves), `hood` (hood up and a mask, or dark glasses, or a cap), `yankee` (teen-sized: a sukeban in a long sailor skirt with a mask and a wooden sword, or a school gang leader in a long uniform coat) and `drunk` (a suit undone, the tie round his head, a box of sushi). An outfit's hair picks its variant (`VARIANT`, `cutOf`). In the city they're in the districts' mixes (Kaburo's zones most, the port, the old town), and alleys have their own people (`ALLEY_PEOPLE` in people.ts: one, two or three against an alley's wall, more where the night is alive, keeping late hours and a shady manner).
