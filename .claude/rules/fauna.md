# Animals: birds, cats, dogs, hens, bats, insects

**The animals are in both cities by default (the user approved it 2026-10-10; `?fauna=0` turns them off)**; they were first behind `?fauna=1` (default off: the user's say first; step 4 of the plan, 2026-10-10: see **In the city**). Older note, kept: **the animals were NOT in the city** (the user, 2026-10-10: they were added without the showroom review, and were taken out: `real/fauna.ts` is not imported by `district/main.ts`; the user's rule is that models are approved in a showroom first). They live in the two showrooms until approved, and the bats were rejected and removed. Covers the animals as built, to wire into both cities once approved. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

**What and how many** (`district/faunaMix.ts`, pure, `tests/faunaMix.test.ts`): `faunaTargets(ctx)` gives a count for each of 13 kinds from the city (`tropical`), the hour, the season, rain, wind, snow, a typhoon, how near open water and trees and lamps are, and `shut` (indoors, underground, in a ride, in a VN: nothing). Tōto: crows (scavenging by day, gathering at dusk, more in autumn and winter), pigeons, sparrows, gulls (by the sea), cats (more at night), bats at dusk (not in winter), butterflies (spring, summer), dragonflies (summer, autumn), fireflies (summer nights, a few in spring), moths round the lamps. Manila (always summer): pigeons, sparrows, egrets and terns (gulls) by the bay and river, cats, stray dogs, hens and roosters (most in the lanes at dawn), many bats at dusk, butterflies, dragonflies, fireflies, moths; no crows. Insects need warm, still, dry air (none in rain, a gale or snow); a typhoon clears everything.

**The simulation** (`real/fauna.ts`, `Fauna`, made in `district/main.ts` beside `ownCar.smoke`; `?fauna=0` has none, `?diag=1` exposes `__fauna`): a few dozen animals on the CPU, kept within `FAUNA_REACH` 72 m of the viewer, one instanced draw a kind (`ShaderMaterial`, vertex-coloured boxes and blobs, posed in the vertex shader from `iAnim` and `iFold`: wings flap, legs swing, the head dips to peck, wings fold when it stands). They appear out of sight at 9-58 m, by a bench, bin, cart, lamp or tree where there is one (`world.props`), and fade in and out. Ground animals stand only where `standY` allows (main.ts: pavement, lawn, gravel or earth at street level, clear of walls and props), plan each step once (the end and the midpoint checked; never probed per frame), and startle when you come near: birds take off (and the others by it), a cat, dog or hen runs off. Birds overhead circle or glide (crows, gulls) or flap (sparrows); bats are erratic; butterflies, dragonflies, fireflies and moths flit about a tree or a lamp (`flit`). Lit by one number (`Fauna.light`: the hour's, dimmer in rain), not the city's lights, so they are dark shapes at night; fireflies are additive and blink. `__fauna.startleScale = 0` lets you stand beside one.

**The models** (`real/faunaModels.ts`): tapered limbs (`seg`), blobs and boxes, with the gait weights per vertex (a leg's swing grows hip to paw, the head's dip grows along the neck). The cat and the dog stand, walk and run, and **rest in their own poses** (`catSit`, `catLoaf`, `dogSit`: separate models, picked in `Fauna.rest` when one stops for a while; the dog sniffs by dipping its head); birds are one `bird()` shape tuned per species (breast and rump, neck, beak, tail, jointed legs, folding wings). They are smooth-shaded from real vertex normals (the shader lights them with `Fauna.sunDir`, the sun's or moon's direction, set by main.ts, over a floor and the sky from above; baked top-light was what made the first set look like faceted clay), have eyes, thighs and shoulders, and tails and necks as smooth chains (`chain`); a bird's wing is a cambered strip with feathered trailing edge whose tip lags the root as it beats, and folds along the flank towards the tail. `node debug-shots/faunaclose.mjs <dir>` looks at them from the side in the rooms.

**Second pass (2026-10-10)**: everything the user rejected in the showrooms was removed from the room lists and its file deleted (57 candidate files left, all approved or undecided; the verdicts are in `debug-shots/review/<room>.json`, per room: the same animal can be approved in one city and rejected in the other, e.g. the mallard). The files are static meshes with no skeleton (two Shibas have a one-clip trot), so none of them animates yet.

**The user's verdicts so far (2026-10-10)**: the animals built in code for the sparrow, pigeon, crow, gull, cat and dog, the Quaternius cat and dog, the J-Toastie cat, the Poly dove and raven and the Quaternius Shiba were rejected and removed; the bats before that. Approved: Poly's sparrow, mourning dove, crow, seagulls and gulls (still and flying), cat and kitten, rooster, and the Pat Siefring and elkiotbear Black Shiba. Left undecided: the built chicken, egret and insects, and Manila's dogs and egret. The code-built shapes of the rejected kinds are still in `real/faunaModels.ts` (nothing shows them); `real/fauna.ts` can only move those built shapes, since the model files are static or have their own rigs: the way to put approved files into the city is a new loader, once the user settles the list. The Blender scripts that posed the Quaternius cat and dog are kept in `scripts/animals/`.

**The showrooms compare candidates and take the user's verdict** (`showroom/rooms/faunaRoom.ts`, the two pages' lists, `Row` and `Cand`): an animal a row, its candidates side by side along it, lettered A, B...: a model built in code where one is undecided, and other people's models from poly.pizza (Tōto: ~25 rows, Manila: ~30, with stand-ins labelled: a raccoon for the tanuki, a cow for the carabao, a goldfish for koi, a lizard for the monitor) in `assets/animals/candidates/` (many CC-BY 3.0, Poly by Google's; credits in `assets/animals/CREDITS.md`; `real/faunaStatic.ts` loads any file, sizes it to a length, stands it on the ground and lights it like the animals). **The panel's Review section** (`showroom/review.ts`, usable in any room: `makeReview(room, redraw)`, `add(group, id, name)`, `panel(api)`): a button for each candidate goes round undecided, approved, rejected; kept in localStorage and posted to the dev server's `/__review` (`scripts/devEndpoints.mjs`), which writes `debug-shots/review/<room>.json` (ids like `cat:6dM1J6f6pm9`): **read it to know what the user chose**. `?files=0` or `?files=id,id` loads none or only some candidate files. `scripts/animals/polypizza.py search|get` finds and fetches more (it waits between fetches: the site answers 429).

**Animating the static candidates** (the user, 2026-10-10, 'yes let's go with your recommendation': rig the quadrupeds and birds in Blender, bend the small things in the vertex shader; pilots first). `scripts/animals/rig.py -- <rig> <abs out dir> [preview] [export <abs out.glb>]` (`scripts/animals/rigsheet.sh` also joins a contact sheet with `sheet.py`, which needs PIL: system Python, not Blender's) reads `scripts/animals/rigs/<rig>.py` (`SRC`, `BONES`, `CLIPS`, `PREVIEW`: see the header), builds an armature, weights each vertex by distance to the bones (rough, for low-poly bodies), keys the clips and exports a skinned .glb with them to `assets/animals/rigged/`. A pose turns a bone about a **world** axis, so swings down a chain add; `("move", (dx, dy, dz))` slides one. Poly's models face -Y, Z up (the flying seagull faces +X: use `yaw: -Math.PI / 2`). Pilots in the showroom, each clip its own candidate to approve: `rigs/cat.py` (Idle, Walk, a diagonal gait; Run, a bound; Sit; Loaf) and `rigs/gull_fly.py` (Flap, Glide). Known roughness: the sit's back is long, the tail lies through the floor a little, the loaf's tail and shins stick out. A rigged candidate in a room list is `from: 'rig:<name>', clip: '<Clip>'`; its review id is `<key>:rig:<name>#<Clip>`.

**The rest of the approved ones, rigged by fitting** (`scripts/animals/autorig.py`, specs `scripts/animals/rigs/auto_<name>.py` written by `rigs/_make.py`, which lists id, kind, facing and options for each: `rigsheet.sh auto_<name> <abs dir> export <abs rigged/<name>.glb>`): a spec names a `KIND` and which way the model faces (its `FORWARD`, read from `debug-shots/animals/all/` sheets made with `snap_many.py`; Poly's mostly face -Y, a few +X, the dragonflies +Y) and the fit reads the mesh: the lowest vertices are the paws or feet (front and hind split at the widest gap between them), the extremes the head and tail's tip, the rest of the skeleton between them (`bones_dump.py <rig>` prints the fitted bones). `quad`: pelvis, chest, neck, head, a three-bone tail and four three-bone legs; clips Idle, Walk (diagonal), Run (a bound), Sniff (head down), Sit. `bird`: body, neck, head, two legs and feet; Idle (looks about), Walk, Run, Peck. `flyer`: body, head, tail, two bones a wing; Flap, Glide, Hover. `fish`: five bones along the length; Swim, Idle. Thirty were made (shiba, fox, raccoon, squirrel, kitten, a cat, a dog, two goats; sparrow, dove, crow, three gulls, two ducks, four herons and egrets, rooster, hen, chick; a flying gull, three dragonflies and the moth; one koi) and are in the showrooms as `animated: <Clip>` candidates after their static ones. Seen: the walk and run swing the legs, the head dips to sniff or peck, wings beat. Not seen close: the tears the weights make at the joints. **`crawler`** (the second batch): sprawled legs: a five-bone spine that wiggles and a bone a leg from the body to each foot (feet are the low vertices well out to a side, split into `legs` a side along the length; `lat` how far out; legs swing about the vertical axis in alternating pairs, the sign worked out from where the foot is): both geckos, the lizard, two turtles, the beetle, the cockroach and two crabs (their scuttle is a forward swing: a crab would look better sideways), clips Idle, Walk, Run; and the butterfly 814C as a `flyer`. In the sheets the legs and body move right on all of them. **Not rigged**: the frogs (a hop needs more than a crawl), the snail, the other butterfly (2ZwY, two wings that fold as a pair), the parrot, the rat, the two Shibas posed mid-trot, the dogs facing sideways (baWJ, 83Ha, eDmS, 24Ws).

**Fixes after the user's first look at the animated ones** (2026-10-10): the raccoon's sniff made a lump (the neck and head pulled a block of back: now a slimmer neck and head, and the sniff is scaled by how far the head rises above the back, `sk`); the sit of the raccoon, fox, kitten, Shiba and cat was broken because the 'paws' found were the tail's tip (a paw search now skips the midline, `paws()`) and the pelvis drop was a guess (`rig.py` now slides it so the lowest of the body just touches the floor: a spec lists `GROUNDED` clips; `tilt` option per model); the madtrollstudio cat (`cat2`) stretches because its model is a thin tube whose weights blend badly: its sit got a smaller tilt, the rest is not fixed ('don't change if not fixable'); Poly's Dog 24Ws lay on its side (exported Z-up): `reorient.py` stood it up (it is a sitting dog, not rigged); the parrot is now 0.1 m (0.3 m tall) and animated (Idle, Peck, Walk, Run: its feet are tiny so a walk swings its tail more than legs) and a capuchin (`29hG1_J1Uiq`, rig `monkey`: Idle, Walk, Run, Sniff) stands in for both macaques (Japan's, `saru`) and Manila's monkeys.

**Sit, second pass** (the user: raccoon, fox, Shiba, madtroll cat and Poly's kitten sits still bad): those five have `simplesit` (a spec option): the pelvis tilts 40 degrees, the forelegs straighten, the thighs go forward and the shins back so the haunch stays one piece instead of the hind leg tearing off it. They now read as sitting in the sheets (kitten, fox and raccoon best), but a thin shin and foot still dangle under the hock, and the madtroll cat's tube body is still the weakest; a hand rig per animal is the next step if these don't pass.

**Decision (the user, after four tries): the Sit clip is dropped for the raccoon, fox, Shiba, madtroll cat and kitten (`"sit": False` in `rigs/_make.py`); their hind legs are fused to the body and tear when folded. Dog5 (5ttS) keeps its sit, and the hand rig `rigs/cat.py` keeps Sit and Loaf.**

**Third batch of rigs** (dogs baWJ, 83Ha and eDmS as `quad` facing -x, +x and -y: a model facing -x needs `yaw: Math.PI` in the room list; the rat; a frog and a snail with the new `chain` kind: a row of bones, `hop` makes a frog crouch, spring and land, otherwise a glide with a wave): in the sheets they all move right. **Not rigged, final**: the second butterfly (2ZwY, a pair of flat petal wings with no body), the two Shibas that already carry a trot clip of their own, and Poly dog 24Ws (a seated dog).

**In the city** (`?fauna=1`, `main.ts` loads `real/fauna.ts` and `real/faunaRows.ts` only then; `?diag=1`/debug exposes `__fauna`): `real/faunaRows.ts` holds each city's rows (`TOTO_ROWS`, `MANILA_ROWS`: every candidate not rejected, with its clip, size and turn) and the showrooms read the same lists; `Fauna.useModels(rows)` makes a `ModelSet` (`real/faunaCity.ts`): per simulated kind the ground and air variants (a static file with a rigged version in its row is dropped for the rigged), each file fetched the first time one is wanted (lazy `import.meta.glob`), cloned into a pool of 5 (`loadTemplate` in `faunaStatic.ts`, skinned clones with their own mixers). The sim (`Fauna`) is the same as before with 16 more kinds (fox, tanuki, squirrel, rat, duck, turtle, frog, beetle, snail, goat, gecko, lizard, parrot, cockroach, crab, monkey: `faunaMix.ts` says which city and hour; `WATERSIDE` ones stay by open water); `writeModel` plays the clip the animal's mode calls for (Idle, Walk, Run, Peck or Sniff in bursts, Sit, Flap, Glide, Hover) with fall-backs, and a kind with a model is drawn by it alone (the shapes built in code stand in only for the chicken, egret, butterfly, dragonfly, moth and firefly, whose built shapes were never rejected). A bird that is startled takes off only if the city has an air model for it (the flying gull); otherwise it runs. Measured on the pod at Kaburo Crossing at night (`debug-shots/faunaperf.mjs`): 12 animals cost ~0.6 ms CPU and ~0.1 ms GPU, +39 draw calls. Seen in both cities (`debug-shots/faunashots.mjs "<query>&fauna=1"`): cats, dogs, pigeons, rats, a squirrel, geckos, crabs, a goat at the right places and hours. Not checked: every kind's look in motion in the street, the fireflies' size, a second city's turn at dawn.

**Where they live** (the user, 2026-10-10, after walking the city: ducks were in the middle of town, cats and dogs were everywhere, too many ran away): `faunaMix.ts` `habitatWeight(kind, Habitat)` (pure, tested) says how well a spot suits a kind from how built-up it is (`FaunaWorld.density`: the macro cell's kind, downtown 0.95 to suburb 0.2), whether it is in a park, by a pond (a park's pond: ground of the open lot in `KIND.water`; `FaunaWorld.areas` gives parks and ponds) and by the coast. Downtown (towers, neon, the electric town): pigeons, sparrows, crows (not in Manila), rats and cockroaches in the alleys, a few cats and dogs at 10% and 4%; the suburbs: cats, dogs, hens and goats (Manila); the wild ones (foxes, tanuki, squirrels, monkeys, beetles, snails, lizards, parrots, frogs) in parks, ducks and turtles on ponds only (placed on the pond's water, paddling inside its rect); gulls, crabs and egrets at the coast. A spawn is accepted by chance of its weight, and the number kept of a kind is its mix count cut by the camera's weight (`limit`). A kind that makes sense nowhere has weight 0. **Startling** is rare now: only a share of each kind is shy at all (`SHY`: dogs 3%, goats 5%, cats 8%, pigeons 20%, wild ones all), and those only when you are within 0.35 (tame) or 0.7 (wild) of the old distance. Checked on the pod at dusk: downtown Kaburo kept pigeons, a sparrow, a rat and two crows; a residential cell had cats, sparrows, pigeons and a crow.
