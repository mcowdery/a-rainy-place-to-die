# The modelled cast (Blender + MPFB)

Covers how story characters are defined, built in Blender and loaded; Mack's model. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

The cast (story characters you meet and talk to, later animated) are skinned glTF models, separate from the mob (`real/people.ts`). They're built headless in Blender (5.2, `C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2lender.exe`) with the MPFB extension (MakeHuman for Blender, installed in Blender's user extensions with the CC0 MakeHuman asset packs in its user data dir):

- `scripts/blender/characters/<name>.json` defines a character: body macros (gender, age in years, height in cm, muscle, weight, proportions, race), face and body shape targets (`lr-` sets both sides), skin, eyes, brows, lashes, hair and clothes (MakeHuman assets, each with roughness, decimation, texture size and texture edits such as `colorize`, optionally masked, e.g. `uvmask: under_hair` darkens the scalp so a hair asset's ragged edge doesn't show skin), and `garments` modelled by `scripts/blender/garments.py` (the maid's apron and katyusha headband, fitted by ray-casting onto what they go over; `haircut`, a short cut in place of a hair asset: a shell from a copy of the scalp above a `hairline` (elevation by azimuth round the head's centre), as thick as the `volume` there, with a strand texture and normal map combed back along a flow from the forehead to the nape, and short alpha cards along the same flow to break up its edges; Mack's).

- `"<blender.exe>" -b --python scripts/blender/build_character.py -- scripts/blender/characters/<name>.json` builds it (`--preview-only`, `--head-only`, `--view x,y,z,tx,ty,tz out.png`, repeatable), exports `assets/characters/<name>.glb` (the `game_engine` rig: 53 bones with Unreal mannequin names, +Y up, metres, facing +Z, feet at y 0, A-pose rest, up to 4 weights a vertex; budget ≤ 30k triangles, textures ≤ 1024² except the skin's 2048²) and renders `assets/characters/previews/<name>.png` (front, 3/4, side, back, two head close-ups) in EEVEE. `scripts/blender/mpfb_base.py` is the MPFB layer (also a CLI for quick base bodies).

- **The face lab** (`scripts/blender/face_lab.py`, `npm run face:lab [-- <definition>]`, Mack by default): opens Blender with its window on the character as its definition builds it (no clothes or hair), to shape by hand with MPFB's own Model panel; the sidebar's City Popper tab saves the body's shape targets back into the definition's `body.targets` (the base's, for an outfit; matching left and right become `lr-`; macros stay as the file says) and can rebuild the character and every outfit made from it in the background. Trial faces in `scripts/blender/characters/trials/` (`mack_face_hero|leading|rugged|lean`: an outfit's `body.targets` merge key by key over its base's, 0 takes one out), rendered with `--preview-only --head-only` into `debug-shots/faces_american/`.

- Only CC0 assets are used, each credited in `assets/characters/CREDITS.md` (with a note on two MakeClothes files whose headers still say AGPL3 though their pages say CC0).

## Loading them, and Mack's model

`models/characters.ts` loads them (`Character.load(name)`: a clone with its own skeleton; `CHARACTERS` lists the built ones; `setCharacterEnvironment` gives them a soft environment light of their own; hair and lash cards alpha to coverage; shadows cast from back faces against acne on thin cloth) and, until there are authored animations, poses them relaxed from the A-pose and runs a procedural idle (`update(dt)`: breathing, a weight shift, the head looking about; offsets about the figure's own axes so the bones' rolls don't matter). They're in the showroom's Characters row (`models.html`) for review; none are in the district yet. So far: `salaryman`, `maid` (the maid with flyers outside the Stella Theater, `idol_theatre.flyer`) and `mack`, the MC (192 cm, built like the T-800: the muscle macro at full, chest, lats, V-taper, shoulders, arms and legs built up by targets, his definition a crease shading baked into the skin from the body's own shape (`cavity_weights`, `"uvmask": "cavity"`), a slight tan; black tee, dark jeans, black boots, and a black leather single-rider jacket worn open: garment `jacket` in `garments.py`, made by `garments.prepare()` from a copy of the skin before the clothes mask it, cut at the hips, neck and wrists, opened down the front, pushed out over the outermost clothes (cast from outside in) round the torso's axis, smoothed, then draped (`drape`: below the armpits it may only taper a little going down, so it hangs over the small of the back instead of following it), so it keeps the skin's own weights; a stand collar all round the neckline, zips, a turned-in lip wound like the face it hangs from, a grain normal map, `debug_colors` to see which is which; the skin under the sleeves is masked off, and the tee's faces hidden under the jacket are dropped (its `delete_where`) so they can't poke through).
