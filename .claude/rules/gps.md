# The map, the GPS and auto drive

Covers the M map, routing, the phone's Maps app, the markers in the world, the car that drives itself. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## The map and the GPS

The map (M, `district/travel.ts`: full screen, wheel zoom, drag pan, the railway lines and stations) is the player's too, for the **GPS**: a click marks a destination (a place's dot or a place in the list names it), a right click or 'Clear GPS destination' clears it; with `?debug=1` a place's dot or list entry teleports instead. The route (`district/gps.ts`, `Router` per `NavMode`): driving, and walks over `WALK_GRID` (800 m), go over `RoadNet`, a graph of the road network (each road's centreline cut at its junctions, nodes merged by position so roads run on through cell corners; built a cell at a time as a search reaches it, so it grows with the city; `roadClass`: proper streets with raised pavements and avenues cost 1, shared lanes 12x for driving (`LANE`, only to reach a place on one), alleys closed to cars; A*, the ends snapped to the nearest usable road, jogs under 1.5 m dropped); shorter walks go over `NavGrid`, a 3 m grid built only over the area round the trip (roads and plazas cheapest, open ground dearer, gaps between buildings dearest, buildings and set pieces blocked; A* with no corner cutting, straightened so its corners are real turns), which crosses plazas and parks; `nextTurn` calls the next turn. Driving, the chevrons lie along the lane to keep, the same line the auto drive follows (`laneAhead` over `lanePath` in autoDrive.ts, cached per route in main.ts `laneFor`), so they run where the car does. The route is drawn on the map; the shared state is `district/guide.ts` (`Guide`: the destination, the route for how you're getting about, rerouting when you stray or get in or out of a car, arrival), used by the M map, the phone's **Maps app** (`district/phoneMaps.ts`, a `PhoneApp` on the phone's home screen: a map that follows you, the route, the next turn at the top with distance and time left, tap to set the destination or pick a place, Clear; walking or driving) and the markers; in the world `real/gpsMarks.ts` lays glowing chevrons along the next stretch (on foot every 4.5 m for 60 m, driving every 7 m for 90 m) with a pulse running toward the destination, and a thin line of light (a hairline at any distance: widened with distance just enough to stay a couple of pixels) and a small ring stand on the destination; a compass at the top points along the route and gives the distance left. It reroutes when you stray 15 m off it, routes from the car when you're driving, and clears on arrival (12 m). `?debug=1` exposes `window.__gps('<node id>')`. Driving routes keep to what a car can do (`RoadNet.driveNodes`: the search is over a stretch of road and a direction, so there's no doubling back, and where an avenue's median runs unbroken through a junction the only ways on are straight along it or a left turn; from a spot on a median avenue you set out along the carriageway you're on; a street inside a block costs `INNER` a metre, so routes keep to the cell-edge roads, which carry the traffic and have nothing parked along them), and they set out the way the car faces where going round the block isn't much further (`GuideFrom.heading`, `ABOUT`).

## Auto drive

**Auto drive** (`district/autoDrive.ts`, pure, `tests/autoDrive.test.ts`): your car or bike drives itself to the GPS's destination along that route, on the streets (not the expressway). N at the wheel cycles it (off, with the traffic, slow and no rules, fast) and the Maps app has the same row (`MapsAuto` in phoneMaps.ts); any driving key takes the wheel back (`Driving.pilot`, `onOverride`). It works the car's own controls (race/vehicle.ts `Controls`), so handling, sound, lights and damage are the car's. `lanePath` turns the route into the line to drive: if the car stands off the road (the garage's bay), straight out to it first; the lane to keep on each road from the plan's roads (`roadFinder`, `laneOf`: an avenue's kerb lane, the left half of a street, the middle of a narrow lane or of a street inside a block (cars are parked along its kerbs); eased where a road widens), the corners rounded at the traffic's radii, pulling in at the kerb at the end; and the junctions, found by walking each leg and asking what runs across it (`AutoJunction`: a `signal` where two cell-edge roads of 8 m or more cross, `giveway` entering or crossing one from a side street, `minor` between side streets). `AutoDrive.step` follows it: steering at a point ahead on the lane, a speed for the road's class, the bends ahead (braking in time), the vehicle ahead (`AutoWorld.vehicles`: `TrafficSystem.around`), the lights (`Signals.state` on the traffic's clock) and what stands in the way (`AutoWorld.solid`: `District.obstacle` and story npcs; it steers round, squeezes through a tight gap at a crawl, backs off for room, or stops). The three ways (`STYLE`): `traffic` keeps the traffic's speeds and rules (red and amber, right turns giving way to oncoming traffic); `careful` is slow (23 km/h) and treats a red light as a give-way line, and goes round one stopped vehicle on the other side of the road when that's clear (never a queue); `fast` is quick on the straights (to ~90 km/h on avenues) but takes street corners at the traffic's pace, treats red as give-way, and overtakes only into an avenue's inner lane. Its controls are eased as a driver's are (one foot moving between throttle and brake, following the braking it planned; the wheel smoothed; its place along the path taken between the samples: stamping the pedals on and off rocked the car). It doesn't see the crowd. It backs up to turn round or out of a tight spot (`backing`), parks short and says so when the last stretch is blocked (cars parked both sides of a narrow street), and hands the wheel back with a message if the way is blocked mid-route. `?debug=1` exposes `__auto.set(mode)`, `__auto.state()` and `__auto.around(r)` (the vehicles round you, as the driver sees them). `debug-shots/autodrive.mjs <dir> <mode> <node>` drives a trip in the game and logs it; `autodrivemaps.mjs` the keys and the Maps row.
