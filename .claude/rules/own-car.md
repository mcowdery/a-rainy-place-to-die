# Your car in the city

Covers your car and garage, driving, crashing and damage, races in the city, the cabin and driving cameras, mirrors, lights, Mack at the wheel. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## Your car and its garage

**Your car in the city** (`district/ownCar.ts`): the car you're driving in the garage (race/profile.ts: its paint, livery, neon and tuning) lives in the open middle bay of your garage, 夜鷹ガレージ Yotaka Garage (stamp `city_garage`, kit kind `garage` in `real/asagiri.ts`: a brick railway-arch workshop facing the Toto Line south of Kaburo Station, three bays, the middle one lit, `GARAGE` its size), or wherever you last left it (localStorage `rainyplace.city.car`; `?car=home` puts it back in its bay). It drives on the racing model (race/vehicle.ts, `CITY_ASSISTS`: road assists with room to slide), with a Ground made from the district (height, and walls from `District.blocked`, other vehicles and people: a corner inside something is pushed out the shortest way), its own wheels, pose and engine sound (race/sound.ts); it joins the traffic as a parked vehicle (`TrafficSystem.addOwn`, `own`: traffic stops behind it and honks, never drives it). E at the garage door (`city_garage.door`) opens the garage screen (`garage.html?from=city`, which offers 'Back to the street'). Cars in traffic are no longer yours to take (only your own car is `takeable`). `?debug=1` exposes `window.__drive()` (your car, brought to you if it's far) and `__own`.

**The engine stays running, and the gas** (`OwnCar.engineOn`, `setEngine`, `idle`; the user, 2026-10-11): getting in turns the key, and getting out leaves the engine running, ticking over (`CarSound` at idle, heard to `IDLE_HEARD` 40 m by distance) until K at the wheel stops it (a stopped car stays silent when you leave; K starts it again). A totalled car or an empty tank won't start. The same for the bikes. **Gas** (`fuel` 0-1, `fuelOn`; per vehicle in localStorage `<its save key>.fuel`) only runs out when the debug menu's Car section has 'gas runs out on' (`rainyplace.carFuel`, **off by default**): about 150 km of driving or 100 minutes of idling a tank; run dry, the engine dies (`onStall`, a toast) and won't start until 'refuel' (same section). There are no petrol stations yet. The cabin's fuel dial is not wired to it. **The lights follow the engine** (`updateOwnLights` in main.ts): a car left running keeps its lamps and beams on (brake lights only while driven); off, they go dark. **Starting and stopping is acted** (`setEngine`, `engineRpm`, `shudder`, `START_T` 1.5 s, `STOP_T` 1 s): the key cranks it slow and lumpy (the lamps dip, the tach barely moves, no throttle), it catches with a flare of revs and settles to idle, the body shaking through it; stopping, the revs fall away with a last shudder. The sound, the cabin's tach and the lamps all read the same state. **Unheard and unseen by Claude**: timings and the shake are set on paper. **His hand on the key** (`OwnCar.keyHand`/`keyTurn`/`pending`, `CabinMotion.key`/`keyTurn`, `CarInterior.keyHead`, `CarDriver.limbs`): K (or getting into a stopped car) sends his **right** hand (the key is on the column's right side, so the left would reach across the wheel: the user's correction) off the rim to the ignition key (0.4 s; `CarDriver.keyGrip` bends the right hand's rim grip toward the key's head, palm to the column, fingers ahead, the grip axis turning with the key, the wheel's own turn taken out) and only then does the engine act: the key turns to start while it cranks and springs back to on when it catches, the hand stays a beat and goes back; to stop, the hand goes, the key turns off and the engine dies. A bike has no hand to show and acts at once. The hand wraps the key's head as a short rod (no pinch pose exists yet); the key's turn is its own mesh (`ignition`). Never looked at: check it with a shot of the cockpit and the far chase.

## Crashing

**Crashing** (`district/crash.ts`, in `ownCar.ts`), forgiving, so you keep driving fast but mistakes cost: what's in the way is a `wall` (buildings and set pieces, open-lot solids, the expressway's piers, ramp walls and parapets, the district's edge: `District.obstacle`), a `car` (traffic, parked cars, deck traffic: `CAR_PROPS`, `deckObstacles`), a `pole` (lamps, poles, signals, trees, vending machines), something `soft` (hedges, pots, bikes, fences, medians: `SOFT_PROPS`) or a person (story npcs). Walls stop you, pushed out along their normal with only the speed into them lost (`friction` in the racing model's collide), and met under ~45° they turn the car along them (`glance`), so you slide down a wall at speed; people stop you without damage. A car or a pole stops you only when the nose meets it fresh, at speed (`NUDGE`, ~15 km/h) and head-on (`DIRECT`: the cosine between travel and its surface normal); anything else you plough through (`passThrough`: one knock on the way in, damage and a cut in speed, and drag while inside; at a crawl no cut and little drag, so you never get wedged). Damage goes to parts (`Parts`: front, rear, left, right and the four tyres; `sectionsAt` shares a contact between them by where it is on the car): a real hit costs `impactDamage` = 0.09 × (speed into it − 5.5 m/s)² plus a little by speed (nothing under ~20 km/h; 100 km/h head-on is about half the front; ~140 km/h head-on totals it), scrapes and drive-throughs a little. The front costs power (`powerLeft`) and smokes, tyres grip (`tyreGrip`, the racing model's `gripMul`) and pull (`tyrePull`); the car is totalled when the front or the whole body is gone (`overall`, `TOTALED`). It shows (`race/dents.ts`: the body's vertices crumple in at the damaged end or side, unevenly, and the paint scrapes to dark metal; a flat tyre drops its corner, a damaged one wobbles), and there is no damage or speed readout on the HUD (the user, 2026-10-11: damage is felt and seen on the body, speed on the cockpit speedo; only the auto-drive status shows, bottom left). Kept in the profile (`OwnedCar.sections`, and `damage` the overall; `partsOf` spreads an old save's single number); a totalled car won't go: get out and it's towed to its bay (from the expressway, you too), and won't start until the garage repairs it (`repairCost`, the tow included; the garage shows the damage on the car and each part's bar).

## Races in the city

**Races in the city** (`content/world3d/races.yaml`, `district/cityRace.ts` pure: `RaceDef`, `RacePath` (a race's line along one expressway road from a start for a length or laps of a loop; `progress` searched near the last place; `safeSpeed`: the fastest a car can go and still make the bends ahead), `RaceState` (countdown, both cars' progress, splits at the quarters, first to the line; `finish` for a wreck, falling 700 m behind, leaving the route or getting out); `district/raceRival.ts` (`RaceRival`: a car on the racing model with the network as its ground and the parapets as walls, keeping to a lane, changing lanes past traffic and you, following when both are blocked, braking for bends by its skill, pushing when behind and easing off far ahead, its engine heard by distance); `district/raceHud.ts`): a race's `host` npc (the racer at Ebisu-jima PA) offers them (E: the challenge card, 1-9 picks), you're put in your car on the grid in the left lane with the rival beside you, the loop's traffic cleared from round it (`ExpresswayTraffic.clearAround`; the traffic also minds the rival), a 3-2-1 with both cars held (`Driving.hold`), the race line at the top, a flash at each checkpoint, and the result card; it pays win or lose (`pay`) into the race profile. Two so far: C1 one lap (through the traffic) and the Wangan sprint (Ebisu-jima to the fish market). `?debug=1` exposes `__race.start(id)` and `__race.state()`.

## Mirrors, looking back, the car picker

**The rear-view mirror** (the debug menu's Graphics tab, Rear-view mirror; `?mirror=off|cockpit|all`, the default `all`): at the wheel of your car the cabin's mirror shows the road behind (`RearMirror.render`, as on the race page; it was off unless `?mirror=1`), and from the cameras outside the cabin a mirror is drawn at the top of the view (`hudMirror` in main.ts: a framed quad placed in front of the camera each frame, its picture `RearMirror.renderBack` from the car's roof straight back with your car left out; hidden while aiming).

**Sharp, and rationed by how many are drawn, not by their size** (measured 2026-10-05 in the cab at Kaburo Crossing at night, `debug-shots/_lean.mjs <dir> mirrorperf|mirrorprof|mirror2`; the user found the first ones, 256 x 68 every third frame and the door mirrors 128 x 100 every sixth, too coarse and jerky): the picture's size costs next to nothing (three times the pixels each way measured within the noise: the city's cost is its geometry), a picture costs about the same however big, and half of that was the scene working out every object's place a second time, which `RearMirror.draw` now skips (`scene.matrixWorldAutoUpdate` off for the mirror's render: 4.7 ms a picture to 2.4; the view's own render does it each frame, so a mirror is a frame behind). So: the cab's mirror and the one over the view are 640 x 170 with 4x MSAA every other frame (~30 a second; `mirrorAge`, `MIRROR_EVERY` in main.ts), the cab's only while its glass is on the screen and facing you (`glassSeen`), to 160 m, without the crowd (`district.crowd.group` left out: at a busy crossing the passers-by were most of a picture's triangles). Cost with the mirrors on against off, same place: about +3 ms of GPU and +1 of CPU from the cab or with the mirror over the view (it was +6 and +7 from the cab before, for the coarse ones). All three (the door mirrors had pictures then) every frame measured +15 ms of GPU and +13 of CPU: don't.

**The cars after you are marked in the mirrors** (the markers over the view are the HUD's, which a mirror can't show): each chase car carries a red diamond over its roof (`ChaseCar.tag`, a sprite on `MIRROR_LAYER`, which only the mirrors' cameras draw; no depth test, so it shows through what's between; sized by its distance from you, so it's a few pixels of red however far; gone when the car's out).

## Driving, the cabin and the cameras

**Driving** (`district/driving.ts`): E by your car takes the wheel (`TrafficSystem.takeable/take/leave`; the vehicle's `mode` is traffic, driven or parked, and the traffic system still draws it, lights it and stops behind it or honks at it). A kinematic bicycle model about the rear axle (no sliding; less steering lock at speed; the wheel turns at a finite rate): W/S (brake, then reverse), A/D, Space handbrake, Q the cameras (below), the mouse looks round, E gets out by the driver's door (the right: right-hand drive), the car stays parked. It collides with the district, other vehicles and npcs (a knock: `CityAudio.bump`); the engine sound comes with it (it's the vehicle nearest the camera). `?debug=1` exposes `window.__drive()` (take the nearest vehicle anywhere).

**Car interiors and driving cameras** (both pages): `models/carInterior.ts` fits a cabin inside any car's body (`bodyShape` in models/vehicles.ts: the design's glass, pillars and shape as functions of x, arch-free): the inside of the body's skin moved in and facing in, open wherever the body has glass, so you look out at the bonnet, its mirrors and the road; the dash with a gauge hood over a backlit cluster (canvas faces, moving needles: tach with the engine's redline, a 180 km/h speedo (140 on kei cars), fuel or boost), vents, the radio, the column and its stalks, the steering wheel turning with the steering, seats (buckets, sports seats, plain cloth, white lace covers in taxis and the luxury saloon), rear seats, a parcel shelf, the rear tubs, pedals that press (a clutch that dips through a change), a lever that moves through the gate, the handbrake, visors, the rear-view mirror and an omamori that swings with the accelerations. A look per `CabinStyle` (`CABIN_STYLE` by car type: sport, eighties, rotary, turbo, roadster, saloon, luxury, taxi (a meter, a column shift), kei, van, truck, police). It's one merged mesh on the city material (plain, `style.x` = `INDOOR`, which the city shader keeps leaves, snow, beads, car lights and most of the street light off), the moving parts their own meshes. The controls move at the pace of the hands and feet that work them, not the car's (`CabinMotion`, the cabin's `motion`, shared with `CarDriver` so he stays on what he works; the car itself answers the keys at once): the wheel geared `STEER_RATIO` near the middle, easing off toward `WHEEL_MAX` either way and no faster than `WHEEL_SPEED` (his hands keep to the rim and his arms don't cross); the right foot over the throttle, across to the brake in about a third of a second (`FOOT_ACROSS`), a pedal going down only once the foot is on it; and a gear change (`shifterOf`: a manual's every change, a floor automatic's drive and reverse, nothing on a column): the left hand off the rim to the lever's knob (`CarInterior.knob`) while the left foot puts the clutch down, then the lever moves (`motion.gear` is the gear it shows), the clutch comes up and the hand goes back. The handbrake (a manual's lever between the seats): the left hand goes to it and pulls (`pull`, `CarInterior.brakeGrip`). His posture follows the driving: in ordinary driving (`DriverInput.calm`: the city; never racing) with nothing asking much of him, the left hand rests on the gear lever for `LINGER` seconds of driving after a change (and while stopped), so through town it mostly lives there, and the right hand slides up the rim toward one o'clock (`high`, `HIGH_SPOT`, a little different each time); the wheel well over, speed, a slide or hard braking (`alert`) brings both hands back to quarter to three. **The radio** (`CabinMotion.touchRadio`, `reach`, `RADIO_HOLD`; `CarDriver.touchRadio`, `CarInterior.radioKnob`): a press of the dial (, .), the volume (+ -) or the tape deck (/) at the wheel sends his left hand across to the radio's middle knob, held there 0.9 s after the last press (a key's repeat keeps it there), then back to the wheel or the lever; it uses the gear lever's hand path (`rider.shift`, `dial`: the hand reaches palm down with the index pointing at the radio and the other fingers folded (`HandHold.point`, `POKE_REACH`, `POKE_LIFT`, `POINT_CLOSE` the folded fingers drawn in so the little finger does not stick out, `THUMB_TUCK` the thumb swung toward them), as pressing a button; the first tries, turning the knob with the hand open or closed round it, the handbrake's hold, looked limp or odd, the user said), and waits while a gear change or the handbrake has the hand. `debug-shots/radiohand.mjs <dir>` photographs it on the race page. The cabin and the driver share one update a frame (counted off requestAnimationFrame; the first to ask moves it on). `cockpitLayout(type)` is the same fitting as numbers (pure, `tests/carInterior.test.ts`): the driver's eyes (40 cm behind the windscreen's top, 85 cm behind its foot, under the roof; the coupe's match shooting's `EYE`), seat, wheel (low enough that the dials show through its top half), pedals, dash, mirror, the bonnet camera. `race/driveCam.ts` is the cameras for both pages: Q cycles chase, far chase, cockpit, bonnet, bumper (on a bike: his eyes or behind it), remembered in localStorage `rainyplace.driveView`; Z holds a look back; in the cockpit the driver's head (`Head`) is thrown out of bends and forward under braking on a spring and jolted by knocks, the view looks into the bend and along a slide (`lookInto`) and holds half the body's roll level (`placeInCar`); looking about, his eyes swing round his neck rather than spinning in place (`turnedEye`, `NECK`), so looking back over a shoulder never shows down his own collar, where the head is folded away (`debug-shots/lookback.mjs`; `?debug=1` exposes `__look(yaw, pitch)` in the city); `RearMirror` renders the mirror's picture (the race page always; the city only with `?mirror=1`, ~1.5 ms of GPU and ~2 of CPU, else the glass is chrome reflecting the sky). In the city your car's cabin is built the first time you take the wheel (`OwnCar.interior`), Mack sits at the wheel in the cockpit view (`CarDriver.seatIn`), rain stays out of the cabin, and `Driving.placeCamera` runs once the car's posed (cars without a cabin, the debug-taken traffic, fall back to the bumper; a bus's cockpit is its driver's seat). `debug-shots/cockpit.mjs` (race page, each sports car, every view and round the cabin), `citycockpit.mjs`, `cockpitperf.mjs` (frame times by view).

## The siren

**The glovebox siren** (`district/siren.ts`, `OwnCar.siren`; the user, 2026-10-11, from the story: Mack keeps a police beacon from the old Kirishima detective's licence, [story/cast/mc.md](../../story/cast/mc.md), so a chase with it on is lawful): B at the wheel of your car (not a bike) steps through **siren and lights**, **lights only** and off (`SirenMode`, `nextMode`), and it **stays on when you get out** (the car is left parked with its beacon flashing; B at the wheel again changes it). The beacon is a magnetic red lens on the roof, between the windscreen and the rear glass on the driver's side (`fit`, from `bodyShape`: set again by `OwnCar.change`; it's a child of `view.obj`, not of what the body builder made, so a car swap keeps it), its two lens halves flashing in turn (`FLASH_HZ`, `litHalf`; plain emissive materials bright enough to bloom, as the lamps, never a light). **It lights the street**: `uBeacon` (x, y, z and how bright now: `Siren.light`, `pulse`) is a red wash in the city shader on what lies within ~26 m, on the faces that look toward it and on the wet road (`beaconLight` in `real/city.ts`; one uniform, so switching it recompiles nothing). **The street bridges' decks are on the city material now** (real/sea.ts `bridgeDeck`; they were plain `MeshStandardMaterial` boxes outside the city shader, so the beacon's red (the user's snapshot, 'the siren red wash isn't working on this bridge'), the headlights, the wet road, the lightmap and the seasons all missed them, and their asphalt, darker than the streets', looked wrong): see [places.md](places.md). `debug-shots/sirenbridge.mjs <dir> [night|day]` photographs it. **No tint over the cab's view** (a pulsing red screen overlay was tried and taken out at the user's word, 2026-10-11: the glow of the street is enough). **Traffic gives way** (`TrafficSystem.beacon`, set each frame while you drive with it on: [roads-traffic.md](roads-traffic.md) **Traffic gives way to a beacon**). **The wail is a sound in the world** (`AudioFrame.siren` is the roof's place and its car's velocity while siren mode is on; `CityAudio` builds it as a sawtooth swept 560-1160 Hz every four seconds through a band-pass and a low-pass into a Web Audio `PannerNode` at the car: HRTF, inverse-distance fall (`refDistance` 7 m, `rolloffFactor` 1.2: full within 7 m, a half at 13, a tenth at 60), the listener's place and facing set from the frame each update (the only thing using the audio listener), then into the cover so the cab's glass muffles it). The air takes its top off with distance (the low-pass closes, `3200/(1+d/90)` Hz) and Doppler bends it (`detune` from the closing speed, none when you're in the car it's on); off past 700 m. It carries on when you walk away, panned to where the car is. (The first tries were a hand-made level by distance, a square and then a steeper curve; the user asked for the sound to be really local instead.) **Putting it up and taking it down is acted** (`Siren.press(acted)`, `plans`, `acted`; a stage of about four seconds; the user asked for it, 2026-10-11): his left hand reaches across to the glovebox's handle (`Spots.glove`, the dash's face on the passenger side) and takes the beacon out (0.9 s), brings it to the middle under the wheel's hub, where his right hand takes it (the beacon blends from palm to palm), the window winds down, and the right hand carries it out of it (`window`, a hand's width past the door's skin), up over the roof's rail (`over`) and sets it on the roof by the window (`roofHand`), then comes back to the wheel; the lights, the street's wash and the wail start when it's set down (`dropAt`) and stop when it's lifted off in the reverse (`stow`: from lights only, B again), which ends with the left hand putting it back in the glovebox.
