# Your car in the city

Covers your car and garage, driving, crashing and damage, races in the city, the cabin and driving cameras, mirrors, lights, Mack at the wheel. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## Your car and its garage

**Your car in the city** (`district/ownCar.ts`): the car you're driving in the garage (race/profile.ts: its paint, livery, neon and tuning) lives in the open middle bay of your garage, 夜鷹ガレージ Yotaka Garage (stamp `city_garage`, kit kind `garage` in `real/asagiri.ts`: a brick railway-arch workshop facing the Toto Line south of Kaburo Station, three bays, the middle one lit, `GARAGE` its size), or wherever you last left it (localStorage `rainyplace.city.car`; `?car=home` puts it back in its bay). It drives on the racing model (race/vehicle.ts, `CITY_ASSISTS`: road assists with room to slide), with a Ground made from the district (height, and walls from `District.blocked`, other vehicles and people: a corner inside something is pushed out the shortest way), its own wheels, pose and engine sound (race/sound.ts); it joins the traffic as a parked vehicle (`TrafficSystem.addOwn`, `own`: traffic stops behind it and honks, never drives it). E at the garage door (`city_garage.door`) opens the garage screen (`garage.html?from=city`, which offers 'Back to the street'). Cars in traffic are no longer yours to take (only your own car is `takeable`). `?debug=1` exposes `window.__drive()` (your car, brought to you if it's far) and `__own`.

## Crashing

**Crashing** (`district/crash.ts`, in `ownCar.ts`), forgiving, so you keep driving fast but mistakes cost: what's in the way is a `wall` (buildings and set pieces, open-lot solids, the expressway's piers, ramp walls and parapets, the district's edge: `District.obstacle`), a `car` (traffic, parked cars, deck traffic: `CAR_PROPS`, `deckObstacles`), a `pole` (lamps, poles, signals, trees, vending machines), something `soft` (hedges, pots, bikes, fences, medians: `SOFT_PROPS`) or a person (story npcs). Walls stop you, pushed out along their normal with only the speed into them lost (`friction` in the racing model's collide), and met under ~45° they turn the car along them (`glance`), so you slide down a wall at speed; people stop you without damage. A car or a pole stops you only when the nose meets it fresh, at speed (`NUDGE`, ~15 km/h) and head-on (`DIRECT`: the cosine between travel and its surface normal); anything else you plough through (`passThrough`: one knock on the way in, damage and a cut in speed, and drag while inside; at a crawl no cut and little drag, so you never get wedged). Damage goes to parts (`Parts`: front, rear, left, right and the four tyres; `sectionsAt` shares a contact between them by where it is on the car): a real hit costs `impactDamage` = 0.09 × (speed into it − 5.5 m/s)² plus a little by speed (nothing under ~20 km/h; 100 km/h head-on is about half the front; ~140 km/h head-on totals it), scrapes and drive-throughs a little. The front costs power (`powerLeft`) and smokes, tyres grip (`tyreGrip`, the racing model's `gripMul`) and pull (`tyrePull`); the car is totalled when the front or the whole body is gone (`overall`, `TOTALED`). It shows (`race/dents.ts`: the body's vertices crumple in at the damaged end or side, unevenly, and the paint scrapes to dark metal; a flat tyre drops its corner, a damaged one wobbles), and the HUD (`district/damageHud.ts`, over the speedo) has the car from above coloured by each part's health and flashing when hit, a bar per part, the overall bar and the damage just taken. Kept in the profile (`OwnedCar.sections`, and `damage` the overall; `partsOf` spreads an old save's single number); a totalled car won't go: get out and it's towed to its bay (from the expressway, you too), and won't start until the garage repairs it (`repairCost`, the tow included; the garage shows the damage on the car and each part's bar).

## Races in the city

**Races in the city** (`content/world3d/races.yaml`, `district/cityRace.ts` pure: `RaceDef`, `RacePath` (a race's line along one expressway road from a start for a length or laps of a loop; `progress` searched near the last place; `safeSpeed`: the fastest a car can go and still make the bends ahead), `RaceState` (countdown, both cars' progress, splits at the quarters, first to the line; `finish` for a wreck, falling 700 m behind, leaving the route or getting out); `district/raceRival.ts` (`RaceRival`: a car on the racing model with the network as its ground and the parapets as walls, keeping to a lane, changing lanes past traffic and you, following when both are blocked, braking for bends by its skill, pushing when behind and easing off far ahead, its engine heard by distance); `district/raceHud.ts`): a race's `host` npc (the racer at Ebisu-jima PA) offers them (E: the challenge card, 1-9 picks), you're put in your car on the grid in the left lane with the rival beside you, the loop's traffic cleared from round it (`ExpresswayTraffic.clearAround`; the traffic also minds the rival), a 3-2-1 with both cars held (`Driving.hold`), the race line at the top, a flash at each checkpoint, and the result card; it pays win or lose (`pay`) into the race profile. Two so far: C1 one lap (through the traffic) and the Wangan sprint (Ebisu-jima to the fish market). `?debug=1` exposes `__race.start(id)` and `__race.state()`.

## Mirrors, looking back, the car picker

**The rear-view mirror** (the debug menu's Graphics tab, Rear-view mirror; `?mirror=off|cockpit|all`, the default `all`): at the wheel of your car the cabin's mirror shows the road behind (`RearMirror.render`, as on the race page; it was off unless `?mirror=1`), and from the cameras outside the cabin a mirror is drawn at the top of the view (`hudMirror` in main.ts: a framed quad placed in front of the camera each frame, its picture `RearMirror.renderBack` from the car's roof straight back with your car left out; hidden while aiming).

**Sharp, and rationed by how many are drawn, not by their size** (measured 2026-10-05 in the cab at Kaburo Crossing at night, `debug-shots/_lean.mjs <dir> mirrorperf|mirrorprof|mirror2`; the user found the first ones, 256 x 68 every third frame and the door mirrors 128 x 100 every sixth, too coarse and jerky): the picture's size costs next to nothing (three times the pixels each way measured within the noise: the city's cost is its geometry), a picture costs about the same however big, and half of that was the scene working out every object's place a second time, which `RearMirror.draw` now skips (`scene.matrixWorldAutoUpdate` off for the mirror's render: 4.7 ms a picture to 2.4; the view's own render does it each frame, so a mirror is a frame behind). So: the cab's mirror and the one over the view are 640 x 170 with 4x MSAA every other frame (~30 a second; `mirrorAge`, `MIRROR_EVERY` in main.ts), the cab's only while its glass is on the screen and facing you (`glassSeen`), to 160 m, without the crowd (`district.crowd.group` left out: at a busy crossing the passers-by were most of a picture's triangles). Cost with the mirrors on against off, same place: about +3 ms of GPU and +1 of CPU from the cab or with the mirror over the view (it was +6 and +7 from the cab before, for the coarse ones). All three (the door mirrors had pictures then) every frame measured +15 ms of GPU and +13 of CPU: don't.

**The cars after you are marked in the mirrors** (the markers over the view are the HUD's, which a mirror can't show): each chase car carries a red diamond over its roof (`ChaseCar.tag`, a sprite on `MIRROR_LAYER`, which only the mirrors' cameras draw; no depth test, so it shows through what's between; sized by its distance from you, so it's a few pixels of red however far; gone when the car's out).

**V** at the wheel turns the mirror over the outside views on and off (the cab keeps its own). The mirror over the view is never drawn in the far chase (`driving.view !== 'far'`).

**In the city Q cycles only far chase and cockpit** (`CITY_VIEWS` in race/driveCam.ts; `Driving.views`): the debug menu's Player tab, 'all camera views' (`Driving.setAllViews`, localStorage `rainyplace.allCameraViews`), brings back chase, bonnet and bumper. A remembered view no longer offered falls to the far chase (`within`). The race page keeps all five.

**The mirrors themselves** (models/vehicles.ts `mirrorPlan`, in `addVehicle`, so every car has them: the traffic's, parked ones, yours; they were two boxes with a pane on the back, which the user called bad once he saw them from the cab): each is a head that tapers forward from its rim to a blunt nose, swept in toward the car and toed in 17 degrees, eight-sided on cars built fine (`detail` to 0.13: yours, the chase cars, the traffic) and four-sided on parked ones, in the body's paint on an arm out of the door at the foot of the screen pillar, or (`Design.fenderMirrors`, the classic taxi) a smaller head on a stalk out on the front wing, chrome on the 70s saloon; in the rim a dark bezel, a well behind it and the glass at its bottom.

**The door mirrors show no picture** (the user's direction, 2026-10-06: they should look like mirrors, not show what's behind you): your car's are the body's own chrome glass at the bottom of each head's well, as every car's, reflecting the sky's colours. (Until then your car had a pane of its own in each, `OwnCar.sideMirrors` at `mirrorSpots(type)`, with a `RearMirror` picture apiece from the cab: removed, with the two extra renders.) Only the cab's rear-view mirror and the one over the view show the road behind.

**What's at his face follows the view from the cab** (`DriveView.view`, race/carDriver.ts): `CarDriver` poses the rig from a camera of its own at the seat's eyes looking straight ahead (or at what he aims at), and whatever the rig places from its camera in first person (a cigarette between his lips and the hand that brings it there, models/smoking.ts: his mouth is worked out from the camera when the head is folded away) stayed there while you looked about, hanging in the cab (the user's report). With the view from his eyes and the gun away for 0.6 s, that camera now takes the page's own place and turn.

**Looking back** is Z or C, held (`Driving.lookingBack`): from the cameras outside the cab the view swings round in front of the car (the mirror over the view goes while it does); in the cab he turns in his seat and leans to the middle of the car (`between` in driving.ts), so he looks between the front seats' headrests and out of the rear screen (from his own seat the seat's back filled the view). `debug-shots/_lean.mjs <dir> lookback` photographs each.

## Driving, the cabin and the cameras

**Driving** (`district/driving.ts`): E by your car takes the wheel (`TrafficSystem.takeable/take/leave`; the vehicle's `mode` is traffic, driven or parked, and the traffic system still draws it, lights it and stops behind it or honks at it). A kinematic bicycle model about the rear axle (no sliding; less steering lock at speed; the wheel turns at a finite rate): W/S (brake, then reverse), A/D, Space handbrake, Q the cameras (below), the mouse looks round, E gets out by the driver's door (the right: right-hand drive), the car stays parked. It collides with the district, other vehicles and npcs (a knock: `CityAudio.bump`); the engine sound comes with it (it's the vehicle nearest the camera). `?debug=1` exposes `window.__drive()` (take the nearest vehicle anywhere).

**Car interiors and driving cameras** (both pages): `models/carInterior.ts` fits a cabin inside any car's body (`bodyShape` in models/vehicles.ts: the design's glass, pillars and shape as functions of x, arch-free): the inside of the body's skin moved in and facing in, open wherever the body has glass, so you look out at the bonnet, its mirrors and the road; the dash with a gauge hood over a backlit cluster (canvas faces, moving needles: tach with the engine's redline, a 180 km/h speedo (140 on kei cars), fuel or boost), vents, the radio, the column and its stalks, the steering wheel turning with the steering, seats (buckets, sports seats, plain cloth, white lace covers in taxis and the luxury saloon), rear seats, a parcel shelf, the rear tubs, pedals that press (a clutch that dips through a change), a lever that moves through the gate, the handbrake, visors, the rear-view mirror and an omamori that swings with the accelerations. A look per `CabinStyle` (`CABIN_STYLE` by car type: sport, eighties, rotary, turbo, roadster, saloon, luxury, taxi (a meter, a column shift), kei, van, truck, police). It's one merged mesh on the city material (plain, `style.x` = `INDOOR`, which the city shader keeps leaves, snow, beads, car lights and most of the street light off), the moving parts their own meshes. The controls move at the pace of the hands and feet that work them, not the car's (`CabinMotion`, the cabin's `motion`, shared with `CarDriver` so he stays on what he works; the car itself answers the keys at once): the wheel geared `STEER_RATIO` near the middle, easing off toward `WHEEL_MAX` either way and no faster than `WHEEL_SPEED` (his hands keep to the rim and his arms don't cross); the right foot over the throttle, across to the brake in about a third of a second (`FOOT_ACROSS`), a pedal going down only once the foot is on it; and a gear change (`shifterOf`: a manual's every change, a floor automatic's drive and reverse, nothing on a column): the left hand off the rim to the lever's knob (`CarInterior.knob`) while the left foot puts the clutch down, then the lever moves (`motion.gear` is the gear it shows), the clutch comes up and the hand goes back. The handbrake (a manual's lever between the seats): the left hand goes to it and pulls (`pull`, `CarInterior.brakeGrip`). His posture follows the driving: in ordinary driving (`DriverInput.calm`: the city; never racing) with nothing asking much of him, the left hand rests on the gear lever for `LINGER` seconds of driving after a change (and while stopped), so through town it mostly lives there, and the right hand slides up the rim toward one o'clock (`high`, `HIGH_SPOT`, a little different each time); the wheel well over, speed, a slide or hard braking (`alert`) brings both hands back to quarter to three. The cabin and the driver share one update a frame (counted off requestAnimationFrame; the first to ask moves it on). `cockpitLayout(type)` is the same fitting as numbers (pure, `tests/carInterior.test.ts`): the driver's eyes (40 cm behind the windscreen's top, 85 cm behind its foot, under the roof; the coupe's match shooting's `EYE`), seat, wheel (low enough that the dials show through its top half), pedals, dash, mirror, the bonnet camera. `race/driveCam.ts` is the cameras for both pages: Q cycles chase, far chase, cockpit, bonnet, bumper (on a bike: his eyes or behind it), remembered in localStorage `rainyplace.driveView`; Z holds a look back; in the cockpit the driver's head (`Head`) is thrown out of bends and forward under braking on a spring and jolted by knocks, the view looks into the bend and along a slide (`lookInto`) and holds half the body's roll level (`placeInCar`); looking about, his eyes swing round his neck rather than spinning in place (`turnedEye`, `NECK`), so looking back over a shoulder never shows down his own collar, where the head is folded away (`debug-shots/lookback.mjs`; `?debug=1` exposes `__look(yaw, pitch)` in the city); `RearMirror` renders the mirror's picture (the race page always; the city only with `?mirror=1`, ~1.5 ms of GPU and ~2 of CPU, else the glass is chrome reflecting the sky). In the city your car's cabin is built the first time you take the wheel (`OwnCar.interior`), Mack sits at the wheel in the cockpit view (`CarDriver.seatIn`), rain stays out of the cabin, and `Driving.placeCamera` runs once the car's posed (cars without a cabin, the debug-taken traffic, fall back to the bumper; a bus's cockpit is its driver's seat). `debug-shots/cockpit.mjs` (race page, each sports car, every view and round the cabin), `citycockpit.mjs`, `cockpitperf.mjs` (frame times by view).

## Your car's lights

**Your car's lights** (yours while you drive it; parked, they're off): its lamps are meshes of their own over the lenses (`VehicleSpec.lampParts`, `CarView.lamps`, `setLamps` in `race/carView.ts`: headlamps, tail lamps, brake lights, reversing lamps; they crumple with the body, `race/dents.ts`; a bike's are its model's `lamps` materials), and their light is the city shader's own beams for it (`myLights`: `uMyCar`, `uMyDir`, `uMyLamps`, filled from `OwnCar.beam`; a dipped beam low and wide to ~60 m, the main beam on to ~140 m and up the walls, a red glow behind that brightens under braking, white reversing), measured from the road's line through the car so it works on the expressway, the hills and the car park, and the street under a deck stays dark. Its slot in the traffic's list is skipped (`TrafficSystem.ownLight`, `uCarSkip`; it stays for the wheel spray). F at the wheel cycles auto (on when the traffic's are), main beam, off (localStorage `rainyplace.carLights`; `updateOwnLights` in main.ts); the brake lights and reversing lamps work regardless. The race page's cars have brake lights and reversing lamps too (`lampsByMotion` for the rivals). `debug-shots/carlights.mjs`.

## Mack at the wheel

**Mack at the wheel** (`race/carDriver.ts`, every car on the race page): his rig on a mount turned half round in the car (`Bike.rider.eye` puts his eyes at the driver's eyes of that car's cabin, `cockpitLayout`, right-hand drive; his body following the cabin's `CabinMotion` (`CarDriver.cabin`): his feet on the pedals (`pedalsOf`/`padAt`/`deadPedal` in models/carInterior.ts, `Bike.rider.pedals`: the ball of each foot on its pad, the heel on the floor: `seatBody`), his left hand off to the gear lever through a change (`Bike.rider.shift`: `gripHand` carries it from the rim to the knob and back, palm down on it); both hands on the steering wheel from the driver's side of the rim (`Bike.rider.fwdL/R`, `palmL/R`: the palm on its near, outer quarter, the rim seated at the fingers' roots (`gripSeat`, `HandHold.seat`) so the fingers go out over its edge and close round behind it, each thumb hooked over its near face and round the inside (`thumbL/R`, `HandHold.thumbRest`)), the wheel's frame turning as the cabin's wheel shows it; `debug-shots/driverpose.mjs` photographs his hands and feet up close, `wheelgrip.mjs` each hand's grip from four sides, `gearshift.mjs` a gear change, `posture.mjs` how he sits to the driving (`__race.calm = true` drives as in the city); the right hand's grip is recomputed for where it is up the rim: `CarDriver.rightGrip`), his head shown except in the driver's-eye view. Free drive starts with his shotgun (`WEAPONS`' 'shotgun': F still cycles to the Type 54, which is his Type 54 in his hand too, and the paintball marker, and battles keep their pistol and paintball rules): aiming, his right hand comes off the wheel (`FirstPersonRig.raise`: no upright hold in a car) and the gun goes out of whichever window you aim through, arm straight, seen from his eyes (the cabin's driver door now has its window opening too); the page makes his shots (`Shooting.blast` from his muzzle). The side windows are their own meshes (`VehicleSpec.sideWindows`, `CarView.windows`, `rollWindows`): the one any weapon is out of winds down, and back up 1.5 s after. `debug-shots/racecar.mjs`.
