# Melee: the katana, the bat, fighting, kill moves, duels

Covers the katana, the bat, the melee moves (swings as whole-arm arcs, the stances), ordinary fighters, kill moves, gore, duels with elites, the fight page. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## The katana

`models/katana.ts` is his katana (approved; on a lacquered rack in front of the gun stand; `debug-shots/katanashots.mjs` alone, `katanarack.mjs` on the rack; not in the city yet: melee there comes with fighting in the city): `buildKatana` in the guns' frame (-z along the blade, the edge down, the origin at the tsuba): a shinogi-zukuri blade of 71 cm lofted along an arc (`spine`: it curves up toward its back, the point riding about 5 cm above the handle's line), faceted (edge, ridge either side, the back's shoulders and peak; in the kissaki the edge sweeps up to the point), the flats below the ridges carrying the hamon texture (a frosted wavy temper line, the boshi into the point), a brass habaki, copper seppa, an iron tsuba with its openings (an extruded shape with holes), the tsuka in white rayskin under black silk crossed into diamonds (a texture whose crossings fall over the top and bottom), gold menuki, the mekugi peg, fuchi and kashira; the saya in black lacquer with horn mouth and end, the kurikata and a red sageo; `sword` and `saya` apart (the sword sits in the saya as built), `grip` and `fore` `HandHold`s for the rig (untried) and the `tip`.

## The bat

`models/bat.ts` is a wooden baseball bat (approved, 2026-10-06: "the baseball bat looks good. We're keeping it"; on two pegs beside the katana's rack in the showroom and on the fight room's rack, `debug-shots/batshots.mjs <dir>`; not in the city yet): `buildBat` in the katana's frame (-z along the barrel, the origin at the top of the handle where the hands end, the knob behind at +z; it's round, so "the edge" is only the way it's swung): 84 cm, lathed from a profile (`PROFILE`: knob, a 25 mm handle, the taper, a 66 mm barrel, a cupped end), pale ash under a thin lacquer with the grain along it (a canvas texture mapped by length), the handle darkened by hands, the maker's oval burned into the barrel (an invented maker: 鳴神 NARUKAMI, 硬式用 PRO MODEL 84); `grip` (the right hand, at the top of the handle) and `fore` (the left, just above the knob) on opposite sides of the handle, each forearm from its own side, and `single` (the right hand alone, down by the knob); `base` to `tip` the part that strikes; `setBlood` as the katana's (the barrel's end bloodied: `Brawl` adds to it on a blow to the head).

## Fighting

**Shelved, 2026-10-06** (the user: "I'm not enjoying the bannerlord like combat as much as I thought, nor am I a fan of the killmoves so far. I want to keep both in the backpocket and perhaps return to them, but for now I'd like to remove those systems"). Both are off by default and their code is kept: **swings picked by the mouse's way** (`FightMode.mouseSwings`; back with `?swings=mouse`): a click is now just the next of the stance's combo, so the directed swings (`DIRECTED`: the level cuts, the thrusts from a direction, the cut at the legs, the library's lunge and chop) have no input of their own until the next scheme is chosen; and **kill moves** (`Brawl.killMoves`; back with `?killmoves=0.8`, which also brings back the U key and the panel's row): a killing blow just kills, the shotgun close up just fires, and a blow at an elite whose guard is broken finishes him (`OPEN_BLOW`) instead of a deathblow. The shot scripts that force a kill move (`killshots.mjs`) still do. What chooses a blow now is the next paragraph. Where the paragraphs below describe the mouse's way or kill moves, they describe the shelved systems.

**The fight page** (`fight.html`, `src/poc3d/fight/`; the user, 2026-10-06: the fighting moved out of the model showroom to a testing area of its own): a paved yard 20 m square with a wall on its north, east and west sides (for wall slams and blood on walls; nothing collides with them, you or the thugs), open to the south, the weapon rack by where he starts (`fight/weaponRack.ts`: the katana, its saya and the bat on a lacquered stand; to look at, the keys pick what's in hand), the floor at 0 everywhere, the paving in summer so what's on it is blood. **How a blow is chosen** (settled with the user, 2026-10-06, of four schemes put to them: the blow by what he's doing and the stances liked, a little of the game choosing for you, aiming at a height added by them, and light-into-heavy strings "fine but only incidentally and not a core fighting mechanic"). Three things, each with its own job (the user: "you're right we should keep them separate"):

- **The stance is the kind of swing** (R up, C down, as before): high comes down, mid goes across, low rises.
- **The aim is where it lands**: his head, his body or his legs (`Zone`; `FightMode.readAim`, `AIM`): with a man in front of him and in the view's line, where that line passes him at his distance (above his shoulders, below his hips, between); with nobody there, the view's tilt; a man on the floor is "legs"; a little stickiness at each boundary. A label under the crosshair says which, beside the stance's. Head and legs matter as they did: the head takes more, a blow at the legs (`Move.low`) floors a man more often than not.
- **What he's doing has an attack of its own** (`Doing`, `FightMode.doing`): running (Shift and forward: a lunge), in the air (down from over his head), stepping back (a thrust or a jab to keep them off), just out of a dodge (across, at once).

So a weapon has nine swings a side (`zoneMoves`: `kz_<stance>_<zone>_<r|l>` the katana's, `bz_…` the bat's), made by `swing` from the stance's roll (`STANCE_ROLL`) and the zone's drop and dip (`ZONE_ARC`), the sides taking turns click by click; `Melee.strike({ zone, doing, hand })` picks (`blowFor`), `Brawl.strike` and the fight page's click call it. **Held into its wind-up a blow is a heavy one** (`Melee.holding` / `heavy`, `HEAVY`: the wind-up at half pace, 1.6 times the damage and shove): the incidental part. **A little of the game choosing**: a click with a man on the floor at his feet is the stomp, and in third person he turns to the one he's hitting as the blow winds up (`turnTo`), so the camera needn't be behind him. With the library's moves on (L) the zone picks among its clips instead (`CLIP_ZONES`). The stance can be made to follow the aim (the panel's "follows aim"; off by default, the user's choice).

**Bare hands: a button a hand** (the user, 2026-10-06, after Skyrim): the left button is the left hand and the right the right (`FIST_HANDS`: the left jabs at his head and hooks at his body, the right crosses and comes up under the ribs, either kicks at the legs), **both together block** (the second within `BOTH_WITHIN` of the first takes back the punch the first began; tapped as his blow lands it deflects, as the guard always did), and either let go ends it. With a weapon the left strikes and the right guards, as before (a weapon in one hand with the other hand free doesn't yet give that hand the other button). **A sidestep is a left or a right key tapped twice** (`DOUBLE_TAP`, `SIDE_KEYS`: the arrows, or A and D; the dodge that way); E still dodges. (A flick of the mouse while guarding did it first, the user's own idea, and they had it taken out the same day for the double tap.)

When the city gains something on foot, give it to this page too (through `FpMode`'s hooks where it's the showroom's as well). **Fighting** (melee, on the fight page for review; not in the city yet). The feel (the user's, from modded Skyrim): ordinary fighters die in a few blows and hit as hard; the danger is being surrounded; kill moves roll by chance on killing blows, the chance high; duels (posture, deflects) are for bosses and elites only. `models/melee.ts` (pure, `tests/melee.test.ts`) is the moves as keyframes in the view's frame (`MOVES`: each hand's wrist with its forward and palm, or the sword's tsuba with its blade's and edge's directions, the kicking ankle in the body's frame, the chest's twist and lean; a channel a key leaves out holds the weapon's guard (`FIST_GUARD`, `SWORD_GUARD`, `FIST_BLOCK`, `SWORD_BLOCK`); eased, the sword's direction slerped (`lerpSword`, `swordQuat`) so a cut sweeps), an `active` window, damage, force, cut or blow, lunge), and `Melee`, the state: the weapon (`fists`, `katana` or `bat`), drawn or not (fists up, the sword out of its saya with `draw` and `sheathe`, the bat up in both hands with `batReady` and `batLower`), guarding, the stance (below), the attacks: with a direction (Bannerlord's: `Dir`, `DIRECTED`: the mouse moving left swings right to left: the fists' hookR, right hook, up uppercut, down cross; a weapon's by its stance, below) or without one the combo (`COMBO`: jab, cross, hook, uppercut; a weapon's by its stance; a click past `QUEUE_FROM` queues the next), the kick (a `stomp` at a man on the floor), the thrust or the bat's jab (a click from the guard), `rate` (an enemy's moves play slower), `cancel`, `striking`, `lungeSpeed`; hits are a striking part's sweep from last frame to this against a body's capsules (`sweepHit`, `segmentDistance`; `partFactor`: the head and neck take more, the limbs less). `FirstPersonRig.melee` set poses him for it (`fight`): the saya on his left hip (`SAYA_MOUTH`), the sword placed by the move with both hands on the tsuka by `hold`, the fists by IK closed tight (`FIST`, the thumb folded over: `thumbOver`), the kick by IK (`footWorld` places the foot in the world: a stomp on a head); `poseOverride` and `gunOverride` let a kill move pose him and place his gun; `strike(hitter)` gives a striking part's segment. `models/thug.ts` (`Thug`) is an ordinary fighter (the salaryman's model, posed procedurally): your own moves from his eyes, scaled to his size, slower (`RATE`) and after a tell you can see and hear (`TELL`: the fist drawn back, a breath panned from where he is), `HEALTH` 50; he closes to arm's length and, with others in on you too, drifts toward your back; hits spring his head and body the way they came, shove and stagger him, a kick can floor him (`knockDown`, he gets up), out of health he falls the way he was hit, pivoting over his heels; kill moves take him over (`beginScript`, `resetPose`, `fightPose`, `reach`, `leg`, `tip`, `endScript` 'fall' or 'hold', blended out of the last pose: `blendFrom`; `severHead`, `destroyHead`, `crushHead`, `brokenNeck`). `models/brawl.ts` (`Brawl`) is the fight for any page with the first-person body: the group (`spawn`; fighters still loading take their places in the latest group asked for as they arrive), your blows (the first one or two a swing meets), his blows swept against your capsules (blocked by your guard only from the front: `MAX_ATTACKING` at once, `ATTACK_GAP` apart, damage as high as yours), your health (down at 0, then a fresh group), gun shots (pellets or a bullet along the bore against everyone's capsules), hit-stop and shake, the dead bleeding out; `pickKill` (fits, weighted, not the same twice running), `KillRun`; full gore takes the head off (`models/dismember.ts`: `cutPart` builds a plain mesh of what hangs off a bone from the posed skin, `collapse` folds the bone away and caps it with a stump) or apart, low and off keep it on. `models/gore.ts` (`Gore`, `GoreLevel` full / low / off, `goreSetting()`: `?gore=`, localStorage `rainyplace.gore`, default full): drops in one instanced mesh with gravity that leave stains (another instanced mesh) on floors and walls (`wallSplat`), bursts, spurts from wounds (`spurt`, pulsing), flying pieces (`throwPiece`, `chunks`: a severed head bounces and rolls, bleeding), gashes stuck to bones, pools under the dead (`bleedOut`), blood on the blade (`Katana.setBlood`), spatter on the screen up close (`ScreenBlood`; **Swings are whole-arm arcs** (the user, 2026-10-06: fighting has to look good in third person, which the game will have beside first person, and the katana's cuts didn't: "tiny strokes with his wrist instead of swinging down with his whole arm from above his head"). A weapon's swings aren't keyed one by one: `swing(id, arc, hitter, roll, spec)` in melee.ts makes each from one shape (`Arc`: `KATANA_ARC`, `BAT_ARC`: the hands wound up over the head with the weapon up and back, coming through at arm's length, landing, and at the end by the hips; the bat's carries on round, `wrap`) turned `roll` degrees about the way ahead (`arcKey`): 0 straight down, 45 down across from the right shoulder, 90 level from the right, 135 rising from the right hip, 180 straight up, negative from the left; the hands turn about the chest (`CHEST`), drawn in sideways (`ACROSS`: two hands on one handle only reach so far across), kept clear of the body (`CLEAR`), a swing across carried further past the middle (`CARRY`), and `drop` and `dip` make the cut at the legs; the chest turns into it (wound back to the side it comes from, through to the other) and leans with it. So a new swing is a line, and a change to the shape changes them all; the thrusts, the jab and getting a weapon out are still keyed by hand. In first person a weapon raised over his head goes further up and back than it's keyed (`FP_RAISE` in firstPerson.ts; third person has it as keyed): in front of his forehead his hands and forearms filled the view; and the elbows go out to the sides as the hands rise (`raised` in `fight`). The kill moves' own keys are as they were, but for the home run.

**A swing is the whole body's** (the user, 2026-10-06: "all of the swing animations don't use the legs and rotation of the hips... all arm and chest, which is not how real swings look"). A key carries the lower body too (`LegsKey` in melee.ts: the hips' turn, on which the chest's `twist` sits; how far they sink and where his weight is over his feet, `shift`; each foot's step from where it stands), and `swing` writes it for every swing: the hips turned back to the side it comes from as it loads and **leading it through** (ahead of the chest), his weight back and then into it, sinking as a swing down lands (crouched to load one that rises, and up with it), the foot away from the side it comes from stepping in and out a little (the right, for one straight down or up), the other left behind. The rig poses it in `FirstPersonRig.fight`: where his feet stand (as his stance or his stride has them) is taken first, the pelvis is turned, let down and moved (the body's root, so the eyes go down by `EYE_SINK` of it: `eyeDrop`), the feet are put back by leg IK with their steps, and a foot follows the hips round on its ball as its own hip comes forward, heel up (`FOOT_PIVOT`, `HEEL_UP`: the back foot of a swing); the saya goes round with the hips, and his head stays on what's ahead. A stance has its own way of standing (`STANCE_BODY`: knees a little bent; side on where the weapon is held to that side). The sizes are after the library's sword clips on Mack (`node debug-shots/animsheet.mjs <dir> mack Sword_Regular_A`, cast.md): a deep sink, a wide step, a big turn; a timid one read as arms only. Thugs and elites don't have it yet (thug.ts poses them itself and ignores `legs`). The library's own clips can be played instead (below).

**A swing loads further than the stance holds the weapon** (the user: "they also don't load far enough. **Stances** (the user, 2026-10-06: "a lot more swing types from low stance, mid stance, and high stance"): with a weapon out, `Melee.stance` is `low`, `mid` or `high` (`setStance`, `shiftStance`; the weapon eases over in `STANCE_TIME`; asked for in the middle of a move, it's taken up when the move ends): where it's held ready (`STANCE_REST`: the katana at waki (trailing by the right hip), seigan (the old guard) and jōdan (over the head); the bat low by the right hip, ready ahead, and over the right shoulder: each is where its own swings wind up to). **The stance is the height a swing comes from, the mouse the side** (`DIRECTED[weapon][stance][dir]`, `COMBO[weapon][stance]`): the katana from high: left slashR, right slashL (kesa-giri), down overhead, up highThrust (from beside the head); from mid: left sideR, right sideL (level, dō), down overhead, up thrust; from low: left rising, right risingL, up upcut, down legCut (floors a man more often than not, as a kick does); the bat the same way: batChopR / batChopL / batDown; batR / batL (a batter's swing and its backhand) / batDown / batJab; batRiseR / batRiseL / batUp / batLegs. A swing from where the stance already has the weapon wound up plays its wind-up faster (`Melee.start`: `WIND_FAR`, `WIND_LEAST`), so each stance is quick at its own (overhead from high is about a quarter quicker than from mid). `attacksOf(weapon)` lists a weapon's attacks with the stance each belongs to; `sideOf` (thug.ts) knows which side each reaches, for an elite's guard. On the fight page: R raises the stance, C lowers it (with a weapon in hand they aren't the gun's reload and the review mirror), the panel's Stance row, and a label under the crosshair (in first person a high or a low stance has the weapon out of view). The fists have the one stance. Thugs and elites fight from mid only.

**Checking how a move looks**: `node debug-shots/movesheet.mjs <dir> <fists|katana|bat> [move ids | stances] [phases]` holds each move at several points of its swing (`Melee.rate = 0`) and lays them out as a contact sheet, a move a sheet: the game's third-person camera, his front quarter, his side, and first person (`ROWS=third,front,side,first`, `SCALE=`, `ONEHAND=1`, `CLIPS=1`, `ZONES=1` for the nine swings by stance and zone); `stances` shows each stance and the guard. `THIRD=1 node debug-shots/killshots.mjs ...` shoots the kill moves in third person. `node debug-shots/montage.mjs <out.png> <cols> <width> <files...>` puts any shots side by side. Look at a swing from outside before calling it done: first person hides most of it.

## Duels

**Duels** (`Brawl.spawnDuel`, B on the fight page): an elite (`Thug` with `tier: 'elite'`, `ELITE`: 180 health, taking a third of a blow's damage (`taken`), a katana (`Thug.katana`, both hands on its tsuka by IK, the sword placed from his eyes like yours) or fists, slower moves after a short tell, chained half the time) holds a guard on his left, his right or high (`guard`, `SWORD_GUARDS` / `FIST_GUARDS`, eased between), shifting every second or two and, by his `skill`, moving it to meet your swing (`noticeSwing`); `sideOf(move)` is where a blow comes at the one it's aimed at (a right-to-left cut at his left), and a blow at the side he covers is blocked or now and then deflected (`defend`; a high guard covers straight blows too). Blocks, deflects and hits fill his posture (`addPosture`, draining when he's left alone); full, his guard breaks (`broken`: down on one knee, `ELITE.broken` seconds) and any attack at him in front within 2.3 m is a deathblow (a kill move whatever the odds). Your guard: tapped within `DEFLECT` (0.2 s) of his blow landing it deflects (sparks, `MeleeSound.deflect`, his posture takes it); held it blocks and fills your posture (`Brawl.posture`), and full, your guard breaks and you reel (`stun`); his deflect of your swing does the same to you. `models/duelHud.ts` (`DuelHud`): his name, health and posture at the top (posture filling out from the middle), yours at the bottom, and round the crosshair his guard lit white and his coming blow's side flashing red (a dot for a straight one). `debug-shots/duelshots.mjs`, `brokenshot.mjs`; `__fp.duel()`. `models/meleeSound.ts` synthesises whooshes, thuds, cuts, blocks, the draw, an enemy's tell (panned), a neck breaking, a skull crunching, the shotgun's blast. The fight page's keys: 1 guns, 2 fists, 3 katana, 4 bat (a group of four comes on), X fists up, draw / sheathe or the bat up / down, left click a blow at where you aim (held: heavier; bare-handed each button a hand, both a block), R and C the stance up and down, H one hand or two, L keyed moves or the library's, E dodges (or left or right tapped twice), Space jumps, left click attack (swing the mouse as you click: its way, read over the 100 ms after the click (`SWING_READ`; before it is the wind-up), else just before; an arrow shows it), right button guard, F kick or stomp, the shotgun close up rolls a kill move, T a fresh group, Y gore, U kill-move chance. `debug-shots/killshots.mjs` (each kill move forced, the group), `fpfight.mjs`, `fistclose.mjs`, `stompdbg.mjs`, `probedbg.mjs` (all on fight.html); `__fp.hand/attack/kick/move/guard/draw/brawl/thugs/spawn/gore/killChance/forceKill/probe/state`.
