# Melee: the katana, fighting, kill moves, duels

Covers the katana, the melee moves, ordinary fighters, kill moves, gore, duels with elites. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## The katana

`models/katana.ts` is his katana (approved; on a lacquered rack in front of the gun stand; `debug-shots/katanashots.mjs` alone, `katanarack.mjs` on the rack; not in the city yet: melee there comes with fighting in the city): `buildKatana` in the guns' frame (-z along the blade, the edge down, the origin at the tsuba): a shinogi-zukuri blade of 71 cm lofted along an arc (`spine`: it curves up toward its back, the point riding about 5 cm above the handle's line), faceted (edge, ridge either side, the back's shoulders and peak; in the kissaki the edge sweeps up to the point), the flats below the ridges carrying the hamon texture (a frosted wavy temper line, the boshi into the point), a brass habaki, copper seppa, an iron tsuba with its openings (an extruded shape with holes), the tsuka in white rayskin under black silk crossed into diamonds (a texture whose crossings fall over the top and bottom), gold menuki, the mekugi peg, fuchi and kashira; the saya in black lacquer with horn mouth and end, the kurikata and a red sageo; `sword` and `saya` apart (the sword sits in the saya as built), `grip` and `fore` `HandHold`s for the rig (untried) and the `tip`.

## Fighting

**Fighting** (melee, in the showroom's first person for review; not in the city yet). The feel (the user's, from modded Skyrim): ordinary fighters die in a few blows and hit as hard; the danger is being surrounded; kill moves roll by chance on killing blows, the chance high; duels (posture, deflects) are for bosses and elites only. `models/melee.ts` (pure, `tests/melee.test.ts`) is the moves as keyframes in the view's frame (`MOVES`: each hand's wrist with its forward and palm, or the sword's tsuba with its blade's and edge's directions, the kicking ankle in the body's frame, the chest's twist and lean; a channel a key leaves out holds the weapon's guard (`FIST_GUARD`, `SWORD_GUARD`, `FIST_BLOCK`, `SWORD_BLOCK`); eased, the sword's direction slerped (`lerpSword`, `swordQuat`) so a cut sweeps), an `active` window, damage, force, cut or blow, lunge), and `Melee`, the state: the weapon (`fists` or `katana`), drawn or not (fists up, or the sword out of its saya with `draw` and `sheathe`), guarding, the attacks: with a direction (Bannerlord's: `Dir`, `DIRECTED`: the mouse moving left swings right to left: hookR / slashR, right hook / slashL, up uppercut / rising, down cross / overhead) or without one the combo (`COMBO`: jab, cross, hook, uppercut; slashR, slashL, overhead; a click past `QUEUE_FROM` queues the next), the kick (a `stomp` at a man on the floor), the thrust (a click from the sword's guard), `rate` (an enemy's moves play slower), `cancel`, `striking`, `lungeSpeed`; hits are a striking part's sweep from last frame to this against a body's capsules (`sweepHit`, `segmentDistance`; `partFactor`: the head and neck take more, the limbs less). `FirstPersonRig.melee` set poses him for it (`fight`): the saya on his left hip (`SAYA_MOUTH`), the sword placed by the move with both hands on the tsuka by `hold`, the fists by IK closed tight (`FIST`, the thumb folded over: `thumbOver`), the kick by IK (`footWorld` places the foot in the world: a stomp on a head); `poseOverride` and `gunOverride` let a kill move pose him and place his gun; `strike(hitter)` gives a striking part's segment. `models/thug.ts` (`Thug`) is an ordinary fighter (the salaryman's model, posed procedurally): your own moves from his eyes, scaled to his size, slower (`RATE`) and after a tell you can see and hear (`TELL`: the fist drawn back, a breath panned from where he is), `HEALTH` 50; he closes to arm's length and, with others in on you too, drifts toward your back; hits spring his head and body the way they came, shove and stagger him, a kick can floor him (`knockDown`, he gets up), out of health he falls the way he was hit, pivoting over his heels; kill moves take him over (`beginScript`, `resetPose`, `fightPose`, `reach`, `leg`, `tip`, `endScript` 'fall' or 'hold', blended out of the last pose: `blendFrom`; `severHead`, `destroyHead`, `crushHead`, `brokenNeck`). `models/brawl.ts` (`Brawl`) is the fight for any page with the first-person body: the group (`spawn`), your blows (the first one or two a swing meets), his blows swept against your capsules (blocked by your guard only from the front: `MAX_ATTACKING` at once, `ATTACK_GAP` apart, damage as high as yours), your health (down at 0, then a fresh group), gun shots (pellets or a bullet along the bore against everyone's capsules), hit-stop and shake, the dead bleeding out; the page hands `step` its view (eyes, yaw, pitch), which the fight may move and turn, and poses the rig with the `dt` it returns, rendering from `cine` when a kill move wants a cinematic angle. `models/killMoves.ts` (`tests/killMoves.test.ts`): Skyrim's kill moves, a killing blow rolling `killChance` (`KILL_CHANCE` 0.8, `?killmoves=`, the U key): `KILL_MOVES` (run_through and decapitate with the katana, the latter from a cinematic angle; neck_snap; wall_slam when there's a wall behind him (the page's probe); stomp on a man on the floor; shotgun_jaw, close with a shotgun, instead of the shot), each placing the victim (eased in), posing you (`MeleePose` keys, often from his body: hands on his head) and him, with timed events (gore, sounds, shake, hit-stop), a release (he falls or stays), a focus for your eyes, and where you're carried (`mackAt`); `pickKill` (fits, weighted, not the same twice running), `KillRun`; full gore takes the head off (`models/dismember.ts`: `cutPart` builds a plain mesh of what hangs off a bone from the posed skin, `collapse` folds the bone away and caps it with a stump) or apart, low and off keep it on. `models/gore.ts` (`Gore`, `GoreLevel` full / low / off, `goreSetting()`: `?gore=`, localStorage `citypop.gore`, default full): drops in one instanced mesh with gravity that leave stains (another instanced mesh) on floors and walls (`wallSplat`), bursts, spurts from wounds (`spurt`, pulsing), flying pieces (`throwPiece`, `chunks`: a severed head bounces and rolls, bleeding), gashes stuck to bones, pools under the dead (`bleedOut`), blood on the blade (`Katana.setBlood`), spatter on the screen up close (`ScreenBlood`; drops that fly at the eyes land there instead); low is a few dark drops, off no blood (a pale flash still marks each hit).

## Duels

**Duels** (`Brawl.spawnDuel`, B in the showroom): an elite (`Thug` with `tier: 'elite'`, `ELITE`: 180 health, taking a third of a blow's damage (`taken`), a katana (`Thug.katana`, both hands on its tsuka by IK, the sword placed from his eyes like yours) or fists, slower moves after a short tell, chained half the time) holds a guard on his left, his right or high (`guard`, `SWORD_GUARDS` / `FIST_GUARDS`, eased between), shifting every second or two and, by his `skill`, moving it to meet your swing (`noticeSwing`); `sideOf(move)` is where a blow comes at the one it's aimed at (a right-to-left cut at his left), and a blow at the side he covers is blocked or now and then deflected (`defend`; a high guard covers straight blows too). Blocks, deflects and hits fill his posture (`addPosture`, draining when he's left alone); full, his guard breaks (`broken`: down on one knee, `ELITE.broken` seconds) and any attack at him in front within 2.3 m is a deathblow (a kill move whatever the odds). Your guard: tapped within `DEFLECT` (0.2 s) of his blow landing it deflects (sparks, `MeleeSound.deflect`, his posture takes it); held it blocks and fills your posture (`Brawl.posture`), and full, your guard breaks and you reel (`stun`); his deflect of your swing does the same to you. `models/duelHud.ts` (`DuelHud`): his name, health and posture at the top (posture filling out from the middle), yours at the bottom, and round the crosshair his guard lit white and his coming blow's side flashing red (a dot for a straight one). `debug-shots/duelshots.mjs`, `brokenshot.mjs`; `__fp.duel()`. `models/meleeSound.ts` synthesises whooshes, thuds, cuts, blocks, the draw, an enemy's tell (panned), a neck breaking, a skull crunching, the shotgun's blast. Showroom first person: 1 guns, 2 fists, 3 katana (a group of four comes on), X fists up or draw / sheathe, left click attack (swing the mouse as you click: its way, read over the 100 ms after the click (`SWING_READ`; before it is the wind-up), else just before; an arrow shows it), right button guard, F kick or stomp, the shotgun close up rolls a kill move, T a fresh group, Y gore, U kill-move chance. `debug-shots/killshots.mjs` (each kill move forced, the group), `fpfight.mjs`, `fistclose.mjs`, `stompdbg.mjs`, `probedbg.mjs`; `__fp.hand/attack/kick/move/guard/draw/brawl/thugs/spawn/gore/killChance/forceKill/probe/state`.
