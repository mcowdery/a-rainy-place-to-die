# Districts and their set pieces

Covers every district and its landmarks (Kaburo, Asagiri, Kasumi-chō, the port and islands, Denkō-chō, the north, Kawabata, the outer districts, the airport), terrain, the city's edges, the sea and waterfronts. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## The districts

The private railway 西光線 Seikō Line (S01 Tōto-Chūō to S04, underground under the road between rows 9 and 10; transfer S01 and the Toto station) runs from the terminal out to **Sakuragaoka (桜ヶ丘)**, the residential district: 6×4 cells west of Asagiri (after the neighbourhoods west of Shinjuku). The residential style has `onlyZoned`, so only the cells its zone file (`zones/sakuragaoka.yaml`) paints are generated; the R cells round the centre stay open ground. (Row 13 below it is painted too, and Kasumi-chō's map reaches (7, 14), so no unbuilt strip is left between the neighbourhoods.) Zones: houses, apartments (マンション), Sakuragaoka Ginza (the shopping street along the railway road) and a park. Zone looks add `homes` (a building is a home: `HOME_FLAG` in the flags, a 3 m ground floor with a front door under a lamp and a window instead of a shop, drawn by the wall shader; no posters, no shop light), `roofs` (low homes get a pitched roof, near and far, instead of a parapet and rooftop gear) and `bikes` (mamachari parked at the door, prop `bike`). Utility poles and wires come with the narrow lanes.

**Kasumi-chō (霞町)** is a second residential area south of the core (`zones/kasumicho.yaml`: houses, flats, offices, a shopping street, a park), its main streets from `roads.yaml` (Kasumi-dōri; the radials running on to the harbour front; Kōkashita-dōri under the Toto Line's viaduct). A zone file's `area:` names its neighbourhood in the HUD (`Zone3.area`, `District.districtAt`).

**東都港 Tōto Port** (style `harbor`, `onlyZoned`, `zones/harbor.yaml`) runs along the coast south of it: warehouses on big blocks along wide truck roads, the container terminal (`yardCover`: a big block becomes a container `yard` open lot, `openLots.ts`: fenced asphalt, rows of container stacks one to four high as `container` props, which collide as walls (`WALL_PROPS`), floodlights along the lanes), warehouse nightlife, harbour towers in plazas and the fish market's streets, with Kaigan-dōri (32 m) along its north edge. ## Terrain, the city's edges and the outer districts

**Terrain** (`content/world3d/terrain.yaml`, `district/terrain.ts`): terraces raise whole L0 cells to a level (学園坂's upper campus, rows 3-4, 12 m; 東光寺's rise, 6 m; 鷹ノ台 in two steps, its upper plateau at 10 m and the summit at 22 m). Each grid corner stands at the highest level of the four cells round it and a cell's ground is the bilinear blend of its corners, so terraces are flat plateaus and the cells round them hillsides whose streets climb continuously (no steps, no walls). The chunk workers lift each chunk's geometry (`liftRaw`: per vertex onto the ground, but a building's vertices rigidly to its `footing`, the lowest ground under it, its uphill side set into the hill); landmarks stand at their footing (main.ts lifts what each adds to the scene); `District.floorAt` returns the ground's height at street level and `blocked`, `shelterAt` and the page's street-level checks work relative to it (`District.aboveGround`, main's `camAbove`); the traffic (`setTrafficGround`: vehicles on the ground, pitched with the slope; signal lamps), your car's Ground (`OwnCar.aloft()` for 'up on the deck'), the NPC ghosts, door dressing, the GPS marks and the far land round the city (`real/sea.ts`: unbuilt cells next to the hills as quads on the terrain) all follow it. A node's `floor` is from the ground under it (teleports add the ground), and a landmark's own levels (`landmarkFloor`) are from its footing. Keep terraces clear of rail viaducts, stations and the expressway (tested). `tests/terrain.test.ts`.

**The edges:** terrain.yaml's `hills` raise the land outside the city (void cells, and on past the map's edge for `pad` cells) `rise` m a cell further out, to `max`; water keeps its banks at sea level (the river runs on north between the hills), and the city's edge cells slope up into them. `real/sea.ts` draws that land cell by cell on the terrain, then level ground at the hills' top to the horizon; `real/edges.ts` covers it (forest clumps, and at the foot of the hills a fringe of low houses in rows with lit windows at night; instanced in blocks shown within ~1.7 km); the mountains beyond are in the sky shader (`Sky.uniforms.uMountains`: a near range and a paler one round the north and west, the bay open to the south, and a snow-capped cone to the west-south-west).

**The outer districts** (`real/suburbs.ts`, `SUBURB_KINDS`, with the kit landmarks): **鷹ノ台 Takanodai**, the north-west heights (`zones/takanodai.yaml`: big houses behind hedges on wide lots, the summit woods, Takanodai-shita's low flats at the foot), with `lookout` (鷹ノ台展望公園 on the summit: a timber observation deck on posts over the slope's roofs, its stair and rails in `suburbFloor`/`suburbRaisedColliders` (`LOOKOUT_DECK`), telescopes, the shelter; spawn `takanodai_lookout.rail` on the deck), `villa` (the house on the heights, the top of the MC's housing ladder: story door `takanodai_villa.front_door`), `ryokan` (旅館 鷹の湯: gate, lanterns, garden, the wooden inn, steam over the bath fence; story door `takanoyu.genkan`) and `flood_shaft` (the flood tunnel's shaft house; story door `flood_shaft.shaft_door`).

**西原 Nishihara**, the western suburbs (`zones/nishihara.yaml`: houses, danchi estates, the shōtengai on the Seikō Line's road, the city's edge), with `danchi` (都営西原団地: three slab blocks, laundry, bicycle sheds, the playground, the water tower; the salaryman's home is story door `nishihara_danchi.room_402`) and `ruined_park` (西原ドリームランド, closed: the chained gate, a rusted wheel and a coaster track that stops in mid-air, both girders (`mb.beam`), weeds, a gap in the fence at the far end). The Seikō Line runs on to S05 西原 and S06 西原団地前.

## The airport island, the sea and the waterfronts

**羽根島 Hanejima**, the airport island (`zones/hanejima.yaml`: its north half, landside hotels, car parks and air cargo), over 羽根島大橋 (a street bridge on Kūkō-dōri) with the expressway's airport branch (two-way off the Wangan's west end, ramps on the island) on top; set pieces `airport_terminal` (東都空港 TŌTO AIRPORT: the glass hall, the wing of a roof over the departures kerb, taxis and the limousine bus; story door `airport.check_in`) and `control_tower`. Its south half is the airfield (`real/airport.ts`, `AIRFIELD`, not generated: fenced, outside the district): the runway with markings and edge lights, approach lights over the water with the sequenced strobe, the taxiway's blue lights, airliners at the terminal's jet bridges, and the traffic on a 150 s cycle (an arrival down a 3° glide slope out of the west on its landing lights, touchdown and rollout; a departure rolling east and climbing out south over the bay; strobes, beacons, windows lit at night). The Wangan now runs on east over the river to its end at col 41, where the Yūnagi tunnel bores into the headland (an exit's `hill`, drawn as a wooded mound round the portal); returning from a pass puts you on the route heading away from its tunnel (or, at a loop's corner, on the loop past it).

**The sea** (`real/sea.ts`): water over every L0 water cell and on to the horizon where the bay opens, a seawall where built land meets it, and plain dark ground on the land not built yet and past the map's land sides, so the world has no holes; the water (`real/waterGlsl.ts`, GLSL shared by the sea's shader, the ponds' in `real/city.ts` and the reflection pass, so the three work out the same ripple): wind ripples (`waterSlope`: five trains from 5 m to 25 cm fanned about `uWind`, drifting, bent by slow noise, calmer and rougher patches; ripples smaller than a pixel fade, `waterFoot`) tilt the lighting normal, so the sun and moon glitter on it; the sky in the ripples by Fresnel (`waterFresnel`, `waterSky`), dark beneath you; and all the horizon's colour at the edge of the view, so the open sea meets the sky without a seam. Water marks its pixels in the scene's alpha (`WATER_ALPHA`; nothing after the reflections reads it), and `real/ssr.ts` reflects in it wet or dry (`SsrPass.setWater`): the same ripple's ray walked evenly across the screen (`marchWater`, up to 640 m: the far bank, a bridge, the park's trees, lamps and lit windows at night), what it hits taking the sky's place (a hit adds the difference, so a dark tree darkens the water), ripples too small to see drawing a light out into a streak toward you, rain rings breaking it up. Lines without depth (the tower's night outline) don't reflect. The pass running dry costs ~0.2-0.3 ms of GPU with no water in view and up to ~1 ms looking across the river or the bay (`debug-shots/waterperf.mjs`). `debug-shots/water.mjs` (pond, river, bay; day and night). The HUD calls open water 東都湾 Tōto Bay.

**Waterfronts** (`plan.ts` `RIVER_WALK`, `isRiverWalk`, `Road3.water`): land beside water gets the Sumida's banks and the bay's promenades: a paved walk along the water (a 'coast' strip with `water` 'river' or 'bay': no traffic; cherries along the river, black pines along the bay, lamps and benches facing the water, `riverWalk` in `real/props.ts`; a knee-high flood wall along the water, `real/ground.ts`) and a street behind it; cross streets end in a T at that street instead of running into the water, except where a bridge carries one on (roads.yaml's bridges mark their landings in the edge map: `EdgeSpec.bridge`; the walk stops either side). A waterfront a set piece already holds (Kawabata's river walk, the ferry terminal, the quay cranes) keeps its plain edge, but its cross streets still end level with the next cell's (`held`). The city's edge gets the same layout (`Road3.verge`, `isVerge`, `planCell3`'s `built`): where the next cell isn't built (an unzoned cell, the hills past the map), an outer street runs along the edge behind a grass verge with a row of camphors and a low guardrail, and the cross streets end at it; `real/edges.ts` carries the woods and the fringe of houses on over unzoned residential cells inside the map, so there's no bare ground beyond. 桜湯 Sakura-yu (`sento`, a kit kind in `real/asagiri.ts`) is the public bath: gabled entrance, noren, lit sign, tiled bath hall, tall chimney.

## Kaburo's mega-sign

`megaSign.ts` + `dragon.ts`: Kaburo's landmark on the central crossing: a corner tower wrapped in LED screens that crossfade through `MEGA_ADS` (art in `assets/ads/kaburo/mega/`), a scrolling news ticker, a red dot-matrix 歌舞路 corner sign, and a red neon dragon on the roof (the great red dragon of Revelation 12; tubes on Catmull-Rom curves with a travelling pulse, HDR colours for bloom; steel pylons show by day). Its rounded corner faces south-west.
