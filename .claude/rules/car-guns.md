# Guns in the city: shooting from your car, shots, chases

Covers shooting from your car, the draw and reload, recorded gunshots, the muzzle flash, bullets marking cars, car chases. One of the area docs indexed in the root CLAUDE.md; one topic a paragraph.

## Shooting from your car

**Shooting from your car** (`district/carGun.ts`, the race page's car gun fighting brought to the city; the pistol only for now): at the wheel of your car Mack has his Type 54 (`seat()` in main.ts: the gun he had on foot is back in his hand when he gets out). The right button aims: the view goes to his eyes along an aim that holds its place in the world while the car turns (`Driving.startAim`, `aim`), and the world slows to 30% while the focus lasts (2.5 s, refilling over 6 s; G turns it off; main.ts scales the whole frame's `dt` by `CarGun.timeScale`, `realDt` is the wall clock's). The left fires: aimed, at what's under the crosshair (a shot that would just miss a chase car is pulled most of the way onto it); not aimed, from the hip with four times the spread: in the views on the car (his eyes, the bonnet, the bumper) where you look, and from the chase cameras (which look at your own car from wherever you've swung them) the way the camera looks past the car, level from his window, or at the chase car nearest that line (`DRIVE_BY`: a drive-by: swing the view its way and he shoots at it). The windows are the race page's (`sideFor`: his arm out of the right window from just ahead round to behind, across the car only what the passenger window frames, the windscreen and the back no line of fire: the HUD says so), they wind down for the gun and up after (`CarDriver.update`, which now runs from any view while the gun is out: `CarGun.shown`), R reloads.

**His gun arm swings free** (race/carDriver.ts; both pages; the user's direction, 2026-10-05, after a version that pinned the hand at a point outside the window and turned only the wrist): raised, his whole arm goes straight from the shoulder at what you aim at, as on a bike, the left hand alone on the wheel; shooting from his own window he leans to it (`LEAN`: his eyes and all above the seat 17 cm toward the door and 9 forward, `CarDriver.eyeShift`, which the cameras at his eyes take too: `Driving.eyeShift`), and aiming near straight ahead he leans right out of it (`LEAN_OUT`, 30 cm across and 15 forward: his head and shoulder in the window's opening; all of it within `OUT_WITHIN[0]`, ~29 degrees, of ahead, only `LEAN` past ~54), which is what gives a line of fire nearly ahead (the user asked for more of one in front, keeping the free arm). Where the gun would still be inside the car pointing through the windscreen there is no line of fire: `CarDriver.outFrom` is how far to the window's side of straight ahead he must aim for his hand to be past the door's skin (`SKIN`; from his shoulder as it is leant right out and the arm's reach, 53 cm: about 3 degrees in the hatchback, where it was 19 before the lean out), and `driverLine` (carDriver.ts, over `sideFor`) blocks his own window nearer ahead than that. From his eyes leant out, the screen pillar stands just to the left of that limit, in the blocked part.

**With no line of fire** (`DriveView.blocked`, the rig's `pulledBack`): he pulls the gun back in and holds it in front of his chest, muzzle up (`PULLED_AT`, `PULLED_TILT`), and the crosshair is a red cross in a red ring, pulsing, over NO LINE OF FIRE in red (models/crosshair.ts `update(..., blocked)`; the race page's `#reticle.blocked`); pulling the trigger makes the note jump. So a car dead ahead or to the left front can't be shot: have it a little off your right front. Aiming across the car the arm goes straight at the passenger window, the gun inside. From the chase cameras the hip shot's direction is the way you've swung the view (`Driving.look`), not where the easing camera has got to. After a shot from the hip the gun stays where the shot went for its moment (`aimAt` and `window` are set only while aiming or on the trigger): it used to follow the view, so swinging the camera round the car with the gun still up swung his arm about the cabin and wound the other window down too, and the car was seen with no door glass on either side (the user's snapshot, 'window not showing').

**Drawn, not conjured** (firstPerson.ts `draw`, everywhere he has a gun: on foot, on a bike, in a car): the empty hand goes to his belt at the front of the right hip (`HOLSTER`, the gun unseen until the hand has it, `GRAB`), the gun comes up in front of the chest (`DRAW_VIA`) and out to the hold, 0.42 s, and goes back the same way; a shot asked for meanwhile fires as soon as it's in hand (`queued`); in the car's slow motion he draws in real time (`drawDt`).

**The pistol's reload** (firstPerson.ts, `R`'s shares of 1.6 s; the shotguns keep their dip): the gun's brought in where he's looking, muzzle up and canted (`RELOAD_AT`, `RELOAD_TILT`), the empty magazine slides out of the grip and falls (`mag`), the left hand leaves the wheel (or the gun) for his belt, comes up under the grip with a fresh one and pushes it home, the slide (locked back on the last round) runs forward, and the hand and the gun go back; `CityGunfire.update` plays its sound as it starts. For checks `?debug=1`'s `__chase.drawAt(k)` and `reloadAt(k)` hold either part way, `arm()` gives his shoulder, hand and reach in the car's frame, `seated()` the driver (its `outFrom`).

## The shot: sound, flash, damage

**The shot itself** (the city: real/gunfire.ts `muzzleBlast`; the user's word on the first try, 2026-10-05, a spiked star with a drawn tongue of flame, a bright tracer and a synthesised report: "I want it to be realistic and it looks cartoony. It doesn't sound like a pistol at all"; so: recordings, and a flash as a pistol's really is).

**The pistol's report is a recording** (race/gunSound.ts, both pages): real shots in `assets/audio/guns/` (`<pistol|shotgun>_<voice>_<near|far>_<n>.ogg`, 30 shots, and `foley_<name>.ogg`, ~800 KB in all; CC0, its CREDITS.md), cut by `scripts/audio/cut_guns.py` (run with ACE-Step's Python, for its soundfile) from the Free Firearm Sound Library (opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library: each of its files a few single shots from one microphone, 96 kHz; the script finds each shot, cuts it to where its tail dies, brings it to 48 kHz Opus). `GUN_VOICES` (race/gunVoices.ts): `9mm` (the default: a 9 mm pistol), `45`, `380`, `tokarev` (single shots of a submachine gun in the Type 54's own 7.62x25: the round is right, the gun isn't a pistol) and `synth` (the old synthesised report, also what plays until the recordings have decoded). A shot plays one of its set, never the same twice running, a few percent faster or slower (lower in slow motion); a shooter further off is heard more and more from the mid-distance set (`FAR`, 18-75 m: the range's own echoes are in those), quieter and duller. The close recordings are dry (a 20 ms crack, 50 dB down by a tenth of a second: a shot in the open), so a share goes into a short synthesised street reverb (`echo`). The debug menu's Sound tab: Pistol shot (the voice), Gunshots (level), Gunshot echo (`?gun=&guns=&gunEcho=`; main.ts `gunfire.setSound`; the guns now follow the Sound volume too). `node scripts/gunSounds.mjs` renders every voice as the game plays it (near, three in a row, 40 m and 120 m off) beside the synthesised one into `debug-shots/guns/` with measurements, and the cut script writes `debug-shots/guns/recorded.html` (the raw cuts). What measuring the recordings showed about the synthesis: a real close shot is centred at 1-1.8 kHz in its first 30 ms with 60% of it between 500 Hz and 2 kHz and almost nothing under 150 Hz; the synthesised one had a bass thump. Claude can't hear: which voice, how loud and how much echo are the user's to settle. The shotgun's shot is recorded too (`shotgun_a`, `shotgun_b`: two 12 gauge pumps from the same library; the debug menu's Shotgun shot, `?shotgun=a|b|synth`; `synth` is models/meleeSound.ts' blast), and so is the handling (`foley_*.ogg`: the pistol's reload as the magazine's catch, a magazine pushed home and the slide, each at its moment in the animation, from zer0_sol's "handgun reload sound effect"; the dry click is that catch; the shotgun's lever after each shot is one click of LFA's "Equipment Clicks III", a bolt action being worked, picked by its length and unheard: `foley_lever_b|c|d` are other candidates on the listening page; both CC0, in the folder's CREDITS.md).

**The flash is footage** (models/muzzleFlash.ts, `MuzzleFlashes`; the user turned down two drawn ones the same day, the star and then a small ball of noise: "still not satisfied", "can we look for a better flash"): `assets/vfx/muzzle_side.webp` and `muzzle_front.webp` are a simulated flash's frames, 5 x 4 at 320 px, seen from the side and from the front (CGHEVEN's "Muzzle Flash 01", realistic, a free asset under CC0: `assets/vfx/CREDITS.md`; fetched from its 4K 5x5 flipbooks through the site's open API, api.cgheven.com/api/assets, and cut down by `scripts/vfx/flash_atlas.py`). A shot plays frames `FIRST`-`LAST` in `LIFE` 60 ms (in the car's slow motion you watch it bloom and break up): from the side a card along the bore turned to face the eye, the muzzle at `SIDE_AT` in the frame, the footage's top upward (some shots flipped); from in front or behind a sprite at the muzzle (`FRONT_AT`); between, both, weighted by how end-on the gun is seen (`update(dt, eye)`). A frame is `CELL` 44 cm wide for a pistol (the fire about half that), 1.7-1.8x for a shotgun; each shot starts a frame or two in, a little bigger or smaller. Thin by day and without its halo (`dark`, from the street lamps' level: `CityGunfire.dark`). It rides with the shooter's velocity (`CityShot.vel`). With it a thin wisp of smoke (`Particle.thin`), a speck of brass thrown out to the right, and at night a faint warm light on what's in front of the gun: `CityGunfire.light` is the newest flash as one of the screens' lights (`ScreenLight.on`, added to `ScreenLights` in main.ts: the city shader already loops over those, so nothing recompiles; it borrows the nearest of the 8 slots for ~80 ms; `FLASH_LIGHT` is small on purpose: at 0.3 it whitened the whole junction). The tracer is a faint streak for a frame or two. The rig's own plain flash sprite is off in the city (`ownFlash`); the race page uses the same flashes (`Shooting.flashes`, set and updated by race/main.ts).

**Bullets damage cars** (real/carHits.ts, `CarHits`, `CityGunfire.carHits`; the user asked for gun damage to the car, its windows and windscreen, and to other cars): where a shot meets a car (one of its hit volumes, or the traffic's box: `CityGunfire.carAt`, `TrafficSystem.objectAt`) it's cast again against the car's own skin (`skin(car, body, windows)` for your car and the chase cars; else the car's meshes) and leaves its mark there as a child of the car, so it rides with it: a hole in bodywork, in glass a hole in a web of cracks (`crackTexture`, seen from inside too). Glass is a side window's own pane, or the body between 67% and 94% of its height (the screens are raked too far back to tell by their normals). A side window takes `SHATTER` 2 rounds and is gone (`userData.broken`, which `rollWindows` leaves out; bits of glass fly); `mend(car)` puts everything back (the debug menu's repair). A round into your own car costs its bodywork a little (`onCarHit` in main.ts: `OwnCar.struck`, 1.4 a body hit, 0.5 through glass). Parked cars are scenery and keep the old world-space holes. The traffic doesn't react to being shot yet, and the screens don't break out (they only crack). `debug-shots/_lean.mjs <dir> holes` fires test rounds into your car and logs where each lands.

`debug-shots/_lean.mjs <dir> aim,hip,flash,flashout,side,smoke [clock] [spawn] [view]` photographs all of this (flashes held at their first frame). The shots are the city's: `CityGunfire.shot` (real/gunfire.ts: from a muzzle along a direction, with a tracer and a muzzle flash, no light) strikes the street, the traffic and car hit volumes (`bodies`, `onBody`; race/shooting.ts `castVolumes`, shared with the race page), and `pick` is what's under the crosshair.

## Car chases

**Car chases** (for trying it out; the debug menu's World tab, Car chases; `district/chase.ts` pure, `tests/chase.test.ts`): `CHASES` are the jobs, of two kinds. A `hunt`: a runner (the bagman's saloon; the lieutenant's, quicker, with a gunman) is set down ahead of you on the road you're on and makes a run for it; stop it by shooting it to pieces (`SHOT`: 8 a round in the body, 14 through the glass), its driver through the glass, two tyres (one and it limps, slower and loose at the back: `LIMP`), or ramming it (`ramDamage` by the closing speed; walls and the traffic it ploughs into cost it too); lost if it's over 330 m off for 9 s or the clock runs out. `hunted`: two or three cars of Rindō-gumi gunmen are set down behind you; see them all off, lose them (all over 210 m off and out of sight for 12 s), or in the getaway reach Yotaka Garage (the GPS is set); their rounds cost your job's health (`SHOT_AT_YOU`) and now and then burst a tyre (`OwnCar.burst`), rams cost your bodywork (`OwnCar.struck`). `ChaseState` is the countdown, the clock, the health and how a job ends (also your car wrecked, or you out of it for 4 s). A chase car (`district/chaseCar.ts`, `ChaseCar`) is a car on the racing model with the street as its ground (buildings are walls; parked cars, the traffic and poles it ploughs through, losing speed), driven over the GPS's road network by the auto drive's fourth style, `pursuit` (autoDrive.ts: flat out, corners at `corner` 5.2 m/s², junctions nothing to it, `pace` its own share of the speeds, `shove` to push through a queue it's held up in, `carryOn` to keep its controls across a route planned again): a runner from goal to goal away from you (`fleeGoals`), a pursuer to where you'll be in a moment, planned again as you move, until it has a clear line and is within 46 m, when it drives straight to its place about you (`pursue`, `SLOTS`: on your right level with you, so its gunman's window faces you; on your tail; on your left) and holds it at your speed; held up, it backs out and tries again, and a pursuer that's got nowhere for 7 s out of sight is put back on the road behind you (`CityChase.rejoin`). Its gunman sits on the left and shoots from his window by the race rival's rules mirrored (`Trigger`: a moment to bring the gun up, bursts of two to four, pauses; `missBy`), his arm and pistol out of it. `district/cityChase.ts` (`CityChase`) runs a job (the cars, knocks between them with `separateCars`, whose rounds struck whom, the traffic braking for the chase cars as it does for people in the road, smoke from the shot-up ones) and `district/chaseHud.ts` its screens (the job's line and clock, each car's health and distance with a marker over it or an arrow at the screen's edge, your health, a red flash when you're hit, the result: Enter again, Esc done). It pays into the race profile. `?debug=1` exposes `__chase` (`start(id)`, `end()`, `state()`, `tail(true)` drives your car after the runner by itself, `aim(i)`, `fire()`, `gun`); `node debug-shots/carchase.mjs <dir> [job] [tail|run|stunts|gun] [spawn]` drives a job (or the stunts, or the gun's views) in the game and logs it. `tests/cityRace.test.ts` (the rival alone drives each to the line off no walls at a beatable pace; scoring). `tests/crash.test.ts` (a head-on wall, 100 vs 145 km/h, a wall slide keeping speed, the glancing hit on a taxi's side, a crawl through a car, pole clips, hedges, people, parts).

**The long run** (a third kind of job, `gauntlet`: `CHASES`' `long_run`, from Nishihara in the far west (`nishihara_danchi.front`) to the detective's flat in Kawabata in the far east (`kopo.front`), about 3.7 km; the user's brief, 2026-10-08: the whole Rindō-gumi out for you, more coming however many you kill or outrun, spawning ahead, ramming and shooting, roadblocks). `CityChase.startGauntlet` sets your car down on the road at the start node (`CityChaseHost.place`, wherever you were) with a 5 s countdown for the district to load and the GPS set to the end; `ChaseState` has no end but arriving (`kills` counts cars put down, shot or rammed; `maxHealth` is 260 against an ordinary job's 100, since there are always a few gunmen on you; `add`/`drop` keep `cars` and `defs` in step as cars come and go). The swarm is `CityChase.swarm` each frame: a `Spawner` (chase.ts, pure) says when and from where (78% ahead, the rest behind; fast while 3 or more under `swarmTarget`, which goes from 4 to 9 cars on you as you cover the way) and `spawnAttacker` finds the place: a route out from you at a spread of angles off your heading (the routes part at the turns, so a car turns up out of a side street as often as off your own road), a spot 150-300 m along it that you can't see (`ChaseHost.seen`), the car facing you on its own side and already rolling at 9 m/s, then it hunts like any pursuer. Who is sent is `pickAttacker` by weight from the `SwarmDef` roster (saloon and luxury gunners, minivan and luxury rammers, a quick sports gunner from a quarter of the way, harder driven and better shot with the progress); a rammer (`ChaseCarDef.ram`) takes `RAM_SLOTS` (its nose at your back corners) instead of `SLOTS` and has no gunman. Cars are cleared away when put down for 14 s (or 5 s out of sight behind you), when 420 m off, or when behind you, 240 m off and unseen, and a new one takes their place; only 5 have an engine sound running (each is an audio context). Roadblocks (`Blocker`, every 24-40 s, two standing at most, the first at 14 s; `placeBlock`): out of sight 300-410 m down the route to the end on a straight stretch, cars of `SwarmDef.blocks` stood across the carriageway (`roads()` gives its width) 4 m apart, slanted, with a gap about 65% of the time on a road wide enough for three; their cars are `ChaseCar`s in the `'block'` role (brakes and handbrake on, held still unless knocked hard), solid to you and to the chase cars (`separateCars`, your bodywork pays), shootable for their holes only, some with a gunman who fires as you pass, and the traffic brakes for them. The HUD shows the nearest seven cars and each roadblock; it pays `pay` + `killPay` a kill, won only. `node debug-shots/longrun.mjs <dir> [secs] [spawn] [block|ram]` runs it with the auto drive (it doesn't shoot back, so the swarm boxes it in and kills it in about a minute: the test of the swarm, not of the difficulty, which Claude hasn't played), `block` puts your car 100 m short of the first roadblock, `ram` drives it at one. Untried by the user: how many cars, how hard they shoot and the health pool are the numbers to turn (`SWARM`, `health`, `SHOT_AT_YOU`).
