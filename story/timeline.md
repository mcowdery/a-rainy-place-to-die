# Timeline

One year, one chapter a season, starting in spring. The story turns the season when a chapter ends (the flag
`season_summer` and so on; the game moves it and clears the flag). Inside a chapter the clock runs day by day.

## The calendar the game keeps

- Day 1 is a **Friday**, starting at **22:00**. So day 7 is the first Thursday, day 8 the next Friday.
- Times of day the story reads (`world.time`): day, dusk, night, dawn; `world.late` after the last train
  (00:40) until 05:00.
- A scene can ask for a time: `time_morning` (07:00), `time_noon`, `time_evening` (18:00), `time_night` (22:00),
  `time_late` (01:30). The clock runs on to it.
- Sleeping (booth 17, the futon) runs to 07:00 and autosaves.

## What each season brings to the city

What the world already does, for scenes to use:

| Season | In the city |
|---|---|
| Spring | Cherry blossom along park paths, schools and the riverside (hanami); petals drift; showers |
| Summer | Opens with the rainy season (梅雨), grey and steady rain; then heat waves (猛暑) with evening downpours; cicadas; typhoons late in the season |
| Autumn | Ginkgo gold, zelkova rust, cherry red, dogwood crimson; leaves drift and pile; windier; typhoons early in the season |
| Winter | Bare trees, pines and camphors green; snow that settles, with tyre tracks; snow on the mountains |

**Summer's festivals** (to be scenes, and things to build in the city): the river fireworks on the Sumikawa
(yukata, the embankment packed, the sky over Kawabata), the old town's summer festival (the portable shrines
through the streets, the yatai stalls, lanterns, bon odori in a square), Obon, and the conventions (Denkō-chō's
otaku, the big summer convention on Shiomi-jima).

**Winter's decorations** (things to build in the city): Christmas illuminations (Japan does them big: trees of
light along the avenues, the department store's windows, couples out on Christmas Eve, Christmas cake at the
konbini), then the New Year (pine and bamboo kadomatsu at doorways, the crowds at Tōkō-ji for the first visit
of the year).

**Open:** other seasonal events as story dates (Golden Week, the Dome's big shows, New Year at Tōkō-ji).

## The map, season by season

The city opens as the story goes, so each season's districts come first and its stories are chosen from the
people in them. **Proposed:**

| Season | Opens | What it brings |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | **Kaburo**, **Kawabata** (the old town and the river), **Gakuenzaka** and Gakuenzaka-shita, **Asagiri**, and the C1 loop | The opening (the university, hanami on the river); Mack's booth, office and Michael's workshop; the night world; the police HQ and the terminal |
| Summer | **Tōto Port**, **Ebisu-jima**, **Shiomi-jima**, **Denkō-chō**, **Sakuragaoka**, the Wangan | The triad and the container terminal; the PA meets and the Wangan; the conventions (Denkō-chō's otaku, the big summer convention on Shiomi-jima) and the bay hall; the idol theatre and the manager; the ladder's next rung |
| Autumn | **Suidō-chō**, **Kasumi-chō** | The Dome and its big show; the hospital, the cemetery avenue and the redevelopment site (the bones) |
| Winter | **Nishihara**, **Takanodai**, **Hanejima** | The danchi and the ruined park; the heights, the villa and the flood tunnel (what's under the city); the airport |

Races and car fights can open the whole map while they last. The avenues, bridges and expressway stay open
through closed districts; only a district's side streets, buildings and people wait for its season. The housing ladder follows the map: booth 17
(Kaburo, spring), Kōpo Sakura (Sakuragaoka, summer), the one-room flat (Kasumi-chō, autumn), the doorman flat
(Suidō-chō, autumn), the harbour tower (the port, open from summer, affordable later), the house on the heights
(Takanodai, winter).

## Chapters

Every chapter ends with **one big reveal about the order** and **one big battle with another of its machines**.
**Proposed** ladder, each reveal a layer deeper and each machine worse:

| Season | The reveal | The machine |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | The 200 are real: a circle that buys people's ruin and watches it | The first hunter sent to take him back: a man-shaped machine in a crowd that doesn't stop (the first *Terminator*) |
| Summer | The yakuza, the triad and the bought police are all paid by one hand, the Amakudari-kai | A machine at the wheel on the Wangan: a car fight at 250 km/h |
| Autumn | The order is worldwide; the programme that built Mack; the Beast, worshipped by the tangled | One that wears faces: it could be anyone he trusts |
| Winter | The Two Hundred, the man behind it all, and what's under the city | The last: closer to the Nephilim than to a machine |
