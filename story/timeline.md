# Timeline

One year, one chapter a season, starting in spring. The story turns the season when a chapter ends (the flag
`season_summer` and so on; the game moves it and clears the flag). Inside a chapter the clock runs day by day.

## The calendar the game keeps

- Day 1 is a **Friday**, starting at **22:00**. So day 7 is the first Thursday, day 8 the next Friday.
- Times of day the story reads (`world.time`): day, dusk, night, dawn; `world.late` after the last train
  (00:40) until 05:00.
- A scene can ask for a time: `time_morning` (07:00), `time_noon`, `time_evening` (18:00), `time_night` (22:00),
  `time_late` (01:30). The clock runs on to it.
- Sleeping (booth 17, the futon) runs to 07:00 and autosaves.

## What each season brings to the city

What the world already does, for scenes to use:

| Season | In the city |
|---|---|
| Spring | Cherry blossom along park paths, schools and the riverside (hanami); petals drift; showers |
| Summer | Opens with the rainy season (梅雨), grey and steady rain; then heat waves (猛暑) with evening downpours; cicadas; typhoons late in the season |
| Autumn | Ginkgo gold, zelkova rust, cherry red, dogwood crimson; leaves drift and pile; windier; typhoons early in the season |
| Winter | Bare trees, pines and camphors green; snow that settles, with tyre tracks; snow on the mountains |

**Open:** the river's fireworks (in the city plan) and seasonal events (Golden Week, Obon, the Dome's big shows,
New Year at Tōkō-ji) as story dates.
