# The pool: ideas not yet placed

Ideas for stories, to pick from, combine or throw away. Many hang off things already in the city (ads, set
pieces, nodes). Each names its character, the hook, the theme, and who could stay in Mack's life afterwards.

## Sizes

The main story is one per season ([main/](../main/README.md)); everything here is optional side content, in two
sizes ([side/](README.md)): **side cases** (proper stories of their own) and **side stories** (a few beats; a
relationship; sometimes a service). Some ideas here are already placed, as chapters or side content; the rest wait.

## Spring candidates

What spring could introduce: Kaburo and its night economy, the bought police, Mack's car and the racing, and
the first 200s.

- **No.1** (a [side case](no1.md)): Rui, the No.1 host at Club Adonis; his regular Natsumi sold into debt by
  Cash One. Sex as commerce, the hierarchy of beauty. Stays: Rui, an informant.
- **Papers** (now [spring, chapter 3](../main/spring/03-papers.md)): a young kōban officer stops Mack for his papers, then finds his photo on
  the wall of fallen officers. Corruption, belonging. Stays: an ally in the police.
- **The end of the blossom** (now [spring, chapter 5](../main/spring/05-the-end-of-the-blossom.md)): Koharu's finale; the order's circle offers her
  forgiveness.
- **Monday.** The player is one of Mack's own blackmailers: the Cash One collector, at the bottom of his own
  ladder, with a sick mother and a boss who hits him. The man squeezing Mack, from the inside. Hierarchy, debt.
  Stays: a complicated enemy, maybe an eventual friend.
- **The queen.** The dominatrix of a small SM club in the back alleys. A regular, a powerful man who pays to be
  humiliated, dies at her feet, and he matters to someone. The psychology of power and submission, early.
  Stays: an informant who knows what the powerful want in private.
- **The front desk.** The woman behind Hotel Rouge's little window, who sees everyone and never their faces.
  A room that was booked and never left; the cameras she didn't know about. Secrets, voyeurism. Stays: the eyes
  of the Love District.
- **Blessing.** A Nigerian tout on Kaburo's streets, in debt to the triad for his visa, who sends money home
  and prays for the men who beat him. Immigration and racial tension from the street, and the quiet light.
  Stays: the street's eyes, and a friend.
- **The Wangan.** A kid from the PA meets who races to pay a family debt, on the triad's bets. Brings in the
  racing and Mack's car early. Stays: a racing rival or ally, a way into the scene.
- **Tsukuyomi.** The back-alley fortune teller (her poster is up): a fraud, and a brilliant one. Her clients
  tell her everything, and she remembers it. One told her something about a powerful man that she should never
  have heard, and now someone wants to know what else she knows. Stays: a contact who hears the city's
  confessions.
- **Wanted.** The man on the wanted notices (they're on Kaburo's walls). Mack finds him first, and he didn't do
  it. Stays: a fugitive who owes him.

## More ideas (the user's, developed)

- **The oyabun's wife, or his daughter** (autumn fits). The Rindō-gumi's family house in Kawabata, in the
  spirit of *Fading Afternoon*. Two ways in:
  - *The wife*: much younger than the ageing oyabun, and bored or afraid. Mack sleeps with her, because he
    would, and now a dying old man's honour is at stake and Mack is the one who stained it.
  - *The daughter*: raised behind the plastered wall, wants out of the family. The oyabun hires Mack to find her
    when she runs, or she hires Mack to help her disappear.
  Stays: a debt with the yakuza, for good or ill.
- **The maid café girl** (Denkō-chō, めいどかふぇ ♡ぴゅあ♡). The "pure" persona she sells and the woman under it;
  the regulars who think they own her; one who follows her home. The hierarchy of cuteness, the loneliness on
  both sides of the counter. Her work trades on looking young, so her story stays non-sexual, or she's plainly
  an adult off the clock. Stays: eyes in the electric town.
- **The informant.** Someone feeding Japan's Public Security police (公安, Kōan), or the Americans at the US
  base, about the triad, or about something stranger. They know more about the order's edges than they should.
  A way into the state and foreign-power threads, and into Mack's father's base. Stays: a source who lies to
  everyone, Mack included.
- **The teacher** (already a story character: Kōpo Sakura 102, Mack's neighbour if he moves up the ladder).
  Her own adult life, never her students': a secret she's being squeezed for (a married colleague, a weekend job
  she can't be seen in), or a student gone missing (the trafficking allusion, winter). Stays: the neighbour.
- **The former idol: the face in all the ads** (the `ohwx julie` face: the Julie album, the love hotel billboard,
  the drinks). Once an idol; now gravure, a basement show at LIVE HOUSE 地下室, a love hotel's billboard. The
  idol manager (already a story character) offers her the way back up, in exchange for sex. The same story
  from both sides: her ambition and his power. The inequality theme at its sharpest: beauty as the only thing she
  has to trade. She's an adult now, and her past as an idol is never sexual. **Open:** is she also the idol on
  every screen, the one the user has in mind for later?
- **Another detective.** Two ways:
  - *His old partner* at the Tōto police, who carried his coffin. Now a detective, and good at it. Close to
    finding out.
  - *The rival agency on the posters*: 霧島探偵事務所's "人探し・浮気調査・秘密厳守" (missing persons, cheating
    spouses, discretion). A slicker operator who works for the rich, catching unfaithful spouses. The name
    stays; the old prototype character doesn't. Twist: his photographs of cheating spouses are what Cash One
    buys, Mack's own included.
  Stays: a rival, or a mirror of the detective Mack could have been.

## More ideas (Claude's, for the fights and the maps to come)

- **The ring.** An underground fight club under a pachinko hall, where the yakuza bet on men who fight for
  their debts. Mack fights on foot, and wins too easily; someone notices what he is. Stays: a fighter who owes
  him, and a reason to fear being seen.
- **The container.** The triad brings people in through the container terminal. One of them, an adult woman
  who was promised a job, gets out and runs. A gunfight among the stacks and the gantry cranes. Immigration at
  its rawest, and a good Chinese character: the dock worker who hides her. Stays: the dock worker.
- **The fixer.** A politician's fixer (the candidate on the posters, Tadokoro?) hires guns to make a journalist
  go quiet. Mack is between them. Car gunfights through the city at night. Stays: the journalist, a source.
- **Maria.** A Filipina singer at a snack bar in the back alleys, working to send money home to Manila; her
  contract belongs to someone else. The first seed of Manila. Stays: a friend, and a door to the Manila map.
- **Seoul.** A Korean detective arrives chasing the same trail from the other side of the sea: a case in
  Seoul with a 200 in it. Rival, then ally. The first seed of Seoul and of how far the order reaches. Stays: a
  counterpart abroad.
- **The night driver.** A taxi driver who drives Kaburo from dusk to dawn and has seen everything in his
  mirror. One fare didn't get out alive. Stays: the city's other set of eyes, and a ride.

## Denkō-chō: the otaku district

Denkō-chō leans into anime, manga, eroge, cosplay and hentai culture. It opens in summer, the season of the
conventions. Any sexual material in it (hentai manga, eroge, adult OVAs) depicts adults; the lolicon side of the
real culture stays out.

- **The superfan** (the user's pick). A grown man who lives for one idol at the STELLA THEATER, queues every
  night with his light stick, spends everything on her. Devotion with nothing coming back.
- **The maid café girl** (the user's pick; above). The persona she sells and the woman under it.
- **The ero-mangaka.** A struggling hentai artist in a six-tatami room, his rights owned by a publisher with
  yakuza money. His assistant used to work a politician's private parties, and the new serial is drawn from what
  she saw there, the faces barely changed. It sells. Then the people in it recognise themselves. Desire on
  paper, a scandal hidden in a hentai comic, and the men who want it pulped (and him with it).
- **The seiyū.** She voices pure heroines in anime by day and, under another name, the women in adult games by
  night. A fan works out who she is, and the blackmail that follows Mack everywhere follows her too. Two
  voices, one woman, and the shame between them.
- **The eroge studio.** Five people in a cramped office on Denkō-dōri making adult PC games on deadline. The
  scenario writer is the best in the business and drinking himself to death; the studio's money now comes
  from Ryujin Kogyo. A side story or a side case.
- **The cosplayer.** The queen of the summer convention on Shiomi-jima, photographers twenty deep around her,
  her photobooks sold out. One of the photographers follows her home. The hierarchy of attention. (Summer, at
  the convention.)
- **The 2D husband.** A man in love with an anime girl: the body pillow, the figures, the anniversary dinner
  with an empty chair. Funny and sad, and kind. Loving someone who can't love back. A side story Mack keeps coming
  back to.
- **The doujin circle.** Two friends drawing hentai parody comics of a real Stella Production idol, until the
  agency's lawyers (and the men behind the lawyers) come for them. The otaku world meets the idol world. Side
  story.
- **The adult bookshop.** The old woman who has run Denkō-dōri's hentai bookshop for forty years, knows every
  customer's taste and never forgets a face. Comic relief, and an informant.
