# Light stick (working title) — side case, summer, Proposed

**Character:** the superfan. A grown man, a clerk by day, who lives for one idol at the STELLA THEATER in
Denkō-chō: front row every night, light stick in her colour, every cent on her. The player is him.

## The story

- **His idol** (an adult, twenty-three, a Stella Production girl climbing toward the bay hall) is in trouble,
  and nobody notices but him: the bruise under the makeup, the song she stopped singing, the black van after
  the show.
- **The manager.** Her manager (the idol manager, a story character of his own later) is passing her to a
  "sponsor" for her next step up. The sponsor is a man from the circle.
- **The fan hires Mack**, with his savings in an envelope: the most a man has ever paid Mack to do the right
  thing.
- **The night.** Mack follows the van.

## Themes

Devotion that asks nothing back; the idol industry's ladder and what it costs to climb; the one person who
noticed.

## Fights

On foot: the sponsor's men, in a Denkō-chō back street under the elevated line.

## What it adds

Stella Production: the agency's sponsors and the circle are the same men.

## Who stays

**The superfan**, a friend who'd do anything for Mack now. **The idol**, perhaps.
