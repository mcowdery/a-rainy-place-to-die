# No.1 (working title) — side case, Proposed

**Character:** Rui (ルイ, a stage name), the No.1 host at CLUB ADONIS on Host Street (its billboard is already
up: "No.1 ホスト 今夜も君を待つ"). The player plays his nights.

## The story

- **His world.** The ranking board, the champagne towers, the sales. Love is the product; he's the best at
  selling it. Other hosts under him, a boss over him, his own debts to the club.
- **Natsumi**, a nurse, his most loyal regular. Her tab (urikake) reached millions. Cash One covered it with a
  loan, and then showed her how to pay: an "esthe" (Momogen's posters are in the back alleys), then worse.
  She has vanished.
- **The case.** Her sister brings it to Mack. Mack finds Natsumi alive, working where Cash One put her, recorded
  by its cameras like everyone else.
- **Rui's turn.** Under the act, he did love her, in his way, and he wants her out. The price is Cash One's:
  money Mack doesn't have, and a favour he'll regret. Getting her out costs Mack a Monday.

## Themes

Sex as commerce; love as a product; the hierarchy of beauty (the No.1 and the men under him); debt as a leash.
Rui is Mack's mirror: a beautiful man who used women and is starting to pay for it.

## What it reveals

Cash One is more than a loan shark: it feeds women into the businesses whose cameras record them. Someone above
it collects the recordings.

## Who stays

**Rui**, an informant in the night world, owing Mack. **Natsumi**, perhaps. Natsumi's debt and what the esthe did
to her stay off screen and are never erotic.
