# The queen (working title) — side case, spring, Proposed

**Character:** the dominatrix who runs a small SM club up a narrow stair in Kaburo's back alleys: sharp,
unshockable, more honest about desire than anyone she serves. The player plays her nights.

## The story

- **Her clients** are the most powerful men in Asagiri, paying to be powerless for an hour. She knows exactly
  what each of them needs, and why.
- **The death.** One of them, Kiryū, the senior partner at Todo & Kiryu (the law offices in Asagiri), dies at her
  feet: his heart, or so it looks. She did nothing wrong. It won't matter; his kind of death has to be someone
  else's fault.
- **The cover-up.** By morning, men from his firm are in her club, and she's the story they're writing. She
  brings it to Mack.
- **Asagiri.** Mack works his way up through Kiryū's world: the firm's offices (its junior associates who know
  too much), his tower, his widow at the department store, his partner Todo. The firm drafts the agreements that
  keep the circle's victims quiet: the river girl's family signed one.
- **Another client.** Among the queen's regulars, the CEO from The Peak: a man who owns half the skyline and
  kneels on her floor on Thursdays. He's alive, he says nothing, and he's seen. (His own story comes later.)

## Themes

The psychology of power and submission: the men who rule all day and pay to be ruled at night; desire as
something to understand, not judge; the hypocrisy of the people who judge it.

## Fights

On foot: the firm's hired muscle, sent to finish the cover-up, in her club's narrow stair.

## What it adds

The law: the circle's money is protected by the best lawyers in Tōto, and its victims are paid to be silent.

## Who stays

**The queen**: she knows what the powerful want in private, and she owes Mack.
