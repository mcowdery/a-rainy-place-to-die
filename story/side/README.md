# Side content (optional)

Everything outside the main story is optional, found in the open world, in two sizes:

- **Side cases:** proper stories of their own, with a character at the centre, several chapters, a fight.
- **Side stories:** small ones, a few beats over days or weeks; a relationship that grows (a regular who greets
  Mack, a favour owed, a friend), sometimes a service (parts, information, a ride). Many start at people already
  standing in the city (the oden keeper, the night clerk, the arcade regular, the monk).

People Mack helps can turn up at a season's climax (see [main/](../main/README.md)). The dating
([dating.md](../dating.md)) is side content too.
