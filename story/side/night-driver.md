# The night driver (working title) — side case, spring, Proposed

**Character:** a taxi driver who works Kaburo from dusk to dawn: thirty years behind the wheel, a widower, a
cassette of city pop in the deck. He has seen everything in his mirror and says nothing. The player drives his
nights: fares, the C1 loop, the city through a windscreen.

## The story

- **The fare.** A young woman, an elevator girl at the department store, he took from Asagiri to Kaburo at 2 a.m. Two men followed his cab in a black sedan.
  She was found in the river the next week, and the police wrote it up as a drowning. He knows it wasn't, and
  he's the only one who saw the car.
- **Mack.** He comes to Mack because nobody else listens, and pays in rides.
- **The guns.** The men in the sedan were hired, by someone with money and a reputation to lose. When they
  learn there's a witness, they come for the driver: a car gunfight round the C1 at night, Mack at his own
  wheel, the driver's cab ahead of them.
- **What she was.** She'd been at a party in Asagiri: rich men, an envelope, a camera. Another of the circle's
  ruined people, and a thread that runs back to Minato's video.

## Themes

The ones nobody sees (the driver, the dead girl); loneliness at night; the city as a confessional.

## Fights

A car gunfight on the C1 against hired guns: Mack at his best.

## Who stays

**The driver**: Mack's ride when the car can't go, and the city's other pair of eyes.
