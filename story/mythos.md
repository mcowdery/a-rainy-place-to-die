# The hidden layer

What's really going on behind the stories. The player learns it slowly, a piece at a time, and some of it
never outright. Most of the story stays grounded; this is the part that isn't.

## The man behind it

- One man is behind everything. He is the Antichrist, but the story **never calls him that**: it's only hinted.
- He doesn't care about humanity at all. People are toys and tools to him. He wants control, a kingdom to rule,
  and his own pleasures.
- **Satan** is behind him, but only alluded to: never shown, never even talked about.
- **He has no face.** Mack doesn't know what he looks like, and neither does the player: whenever he's shown,
  his face is blocked or blurred (a reflection, a shadow, a hand, a smeared photograph). Even Mack's
  reconstructions can't render him: a blur, a void. All anyone knows is
  that he's Japanese, very wealthy, and that when he speaks, people listen.
- **He is the figure the detective hunts**: Red John-like, elusive, everywhere, with followers. Every glimpse
  the detective gets of him is a glimpse of the man behind it all.
- **He kills the cuckolded men.** **Proposed**, his signature as a killer, the way a crime drama's killer has
  one: the men whose ruin the circle arranged and watched, he finishes himself. He takes them, humiliates them,
  mutilates them and kills them, in the most humiliating way, because he thinks them beneath him: the weak, the
  ones another man took a woman from. It's the end of the cuckolding, its psychology carried to the last step,
  and his pleasure. The scenes are dressed as something else (a suicide, a murder-suicide, an accident) and the
  police are paid to believe it. Victims so far: Minato (spring). It turns on Mack too: every married woman he
  sleeps with leaves a cuckolded man behind (the first night's husband, perhaps; and in autumn, the oyabun).
- **His calling card is the number 200** (the two hundred Watchers), left small and easy to miss where he has
  had a hand. It first appears in [the opening](main/spring/01-osananajimi.md) and recurs through the year.

### His profile

For keeping the writing consistent: the rules of his killings, as a profiler would put them. Mack builds this
profile across the year, a piece with each death (perhaps with the kōban officer's help), so each one teaches
the player something about him.

- **Type.** A "mission" killer with the dark tetrad's four traits: narcissism (he's above everyone),
  Machiavellianism (people are tools), psychopathy (no empathy), sadism (their suffering is his pleasure). Power
  enables it, as it did for the sadistic aristocrats of history.
- **Victims.** Men the circle has cuckolded: the men another man took a woman from, whom he counts as the bottom
  of the hierarchy. Never women; the women are his instrument, not his target. Never the men who did the taking.
- **Method** (what he does to kill): the circle does the work first: the seduction, the recording. Then he comes
  himself, by voice, before the end: an unknown caller leaving messages on the man's machine, posing as someone who knows
  the wrong being done to him, an ally. He feeds the man dread, then a false alarm and an apology, so relief comes and the end lands harder.
  He delivers the ruin himself, without a word (the tape sent to Minato). Then he keeps calling, mocking, sending
  more, and finally lures the man out with a message forged in the woman's own voice, cut together from her own
  recordings, which her own line shows she never made.
  The number he leaves rings in an empty phone box.
- **Signature** (what he does because he needs to): humiliation, layer by layer: the woman, the pride, the body,
  the life. The mutilation degrades; it isn't needed to kill. And the 200, small, somewhere near.
- **Staging.** Every scene is dressed as something ordinary (a suicide, a murder-suicide, an accident), and the
  police are paid to believe it. The signature survives under the dressing, for anyone who looks.
- **Why.** Status, total and final: nothing of the man's rank is left. It's the cuckolding's psychology carried
  to its last step: the consensual game's humiliation, turned into a predator's. And it's pride, the sin that
  holds others beneath it.
- **Mack.** At the end he gives Mack his own humiliation, in person: a recording of the night Mack, in a love
  hotel bed, told a woman of the circle where his wife would be.
- **His weakness.** He needs the humiliation to land. A man who has already forgiven, who has nothing left to be
  humiliated by, breaks the game (the oyabun, in autumn). Humility can't be humiliated.

## The order

A world order, far older than Tokyo, and not based in Japan: the city is one of the places it plays. The
yakuza, the triad, the Japanese state and foreign governments (China's, America's) are its puppets, whether
they know it or not. It serves the man behind it.

- **Layers of secrecy.** The most esoteric things are known only at the very top, and kept utterly secret. Below
  that, the people associated with it know only obscure fragments. They worship and perform rituals, but
  they don't really understand anything: they're manipulated, toyed with and used.
- **The tangled.** People in the city who get drawn in act like cultists, but they aren't the real group, only
  followers. The order itself is never in the room.
- It gathers data on people: their sins, their desires, their humiliations. The NTR and humiliation stories in
  the stories are part of what it collects.
- But not only for use. There's pure evil in it, and disdain: they do it because they enjoy it.
- **Names.** All three are used; which is the order's own name is **Open**:
  - **二百人衆 the Two Hundred**, after the two hundred Watchers who came down on Mount Hermon (Enoch). Either
    the order's name or something it refers to constantly.
  - **天降会 Amakudari-kai**, "those who descended from heaven". *Amakudari* is also the real word for
    bureaucrats retiring into cushy company posts, so it passes for ordinary corruption.
  - **黎明会 Reimei-kai**, the Society of the Dawn. Isaiah 14:12, in the Japanese colloquial Bible: 「黎明の子、明けの
    明星よ、あなたは天から落ちてしまった」, "son of the dawn, morning star, you have fallen from heaven". Taken from
    the fall of Lucifer, and only that: "morning star" alone is also a title of Christ and of Mary (a famous
    Catholic school is 暁星, for Mary), so it isn't used. (It replaced 明星会 Myōjō-kai, which echoed Kabukichō's
    Myojo 56 building fire of 2001.)
  - **Proposed:** each layer knows a different name and thinks it's the whole thing. The tangled cultists
    worship as the **Reimei-kai**; officials, politicians and executives who serve it know it as the
    **Amakudari-kai**, a network of favours; only the top speaks of **the Two Hundred**, and the player hears
    that name last.

## The Beast

- The Beast is an **AI**. The AI itself isn't evil and doesn't want anything: it's a tool. What makes it the
  Beast is how the man behind it designed it, and that people are made to worship it.
- **The public never sees it.** The world doesn't know it exists. Only those who get tangled up in it see it,
  and many of them worship it.
- **The detective is puzzled by it.** He sees people worshipping, praying to *something*, and doesn't fully
  understand what.
- The dragon gives the Beast its power (Revelation 13:2): Ryujin Kogyo, "dragon god", whose dragon crowns the
  mega-sign on the crossing. An allusion, not a plot point. The neon dragon is red, for the great red dragon of
  Revelation 12:3, and the mega-sign's corner sign between its screens is red with it.

## The Nephilim, transhumanism, and the state

In the spirit of Timothy Alberino: the Nephilim, a world reaching for transhumanism, and state governments
involved in both.

Allusions, not main plot points:

- Giant bones at the Kasumi-chō redevelopment site; a worker dies and state officials seal it. Japan's own
  folklore has a giant, Daidarabotchi.
- The flood tunnel (shaft 3's steel door, 立入禁止) opens onto the pillared hall people call the underground
  temple. Something sleeps there.
- **The programme makes two things.**
  - **Machines**, the common kind: only machines, with nothing human in them. The order's tools and hunters.
  - **Hybrids**, very rare: a human mind in a machine body. This is what the programme is for: the superior
    human. A few serve the order. (Until 2026-10-12 Mack was one, among the first that worked, and had got out.
    He's human now, and Dom isn't one either: neither MC is the programme's.)
  - A hybrid keeps a will, so it can refuse. **Open:** whether one ever has, now that Mack isn't that one.
  - **The body they want** (the lean, not settled: the hybrids are trial runs for the men at the top, who want
    such bodies for themselves). No ageing, no sickness, no death; light, durable materials, the best of the
    best; a predator's body that can fight and spy on people; every sense enhanced; pain under control. The
    debtors whose bodies Cash One collects are what they practise on. **Open:** whether hybrids are also built
    as tools (spies, assassins), or only as trials.
  - **Proposed:** the one thing not solved is the memory, which the rebuild takes; until it is, nobody at the
    top will cross over.
  - **Proposed:** the allusion, never stated: flesh mixed with what isn't human, as the Watchers' children
    were. "As it was in the days of Noah."
- **Open:** who runs the programme for the order, and what else it does to people.

## Cosmic horror

Rare, and based on Christian themes: the Nephilim, the "gods" of old (fallen ones), demons. What's under the
city is older than the city.

The order is the horror too: vastly powerful, vastly evil, with machines of its own. The detective is only a
man, and powerless against it. **Open:** whether Dom is ([cast/dom.md](cast/dom.md)).

## The dead hours

The user's, 2026-10-12 (the name is **Proposed**; the city would have its own word for it).

- **The late night is known to be extremely dangerous.** The whole city knows that something alien or
  supernatural happens in the small hours. It isn't a secret and it isn't a rumour.
- **So the city empties between 2 and 6 in the morning:** almost nobody on the streets, almost no cars.
- **That's when Mack and Dom hunt**, one or both: they go looking for what's out there, eerie monsters and
  robots, and fight it in the empty streets.
- **At sunrise it's over.** The streets fill, and the city goes on as if nothing had happened.

What it changes in this file, which was written for a city that didn't know:

- **Open:** what the public knows (the user is still thinking this over, 2026-10-12; the empty streets
  themselves are decided, and wanted in the game). Until now the hidden layer was hidden: the public never saw
  the Beast or the machines, and only the tangled met any of it. **Proposed:** the city knows *that*, not
  *what*: everyone has a story, nobody has an explanation, the papers print the curfew hours beside the weather,
  and "aliens" is only the word people settled on. The order, the programme and the man behind it stay as hidden
  as before, and the dead hours are their cover as much as their doing.
- **Proposed:** what the things are, in the mythos's own terms. The **robots** are the programme's machines
  (above): tools and hunters, tried out on a city that stays indoors. The **monsters** are the older things:
  what the Nephilim bones and the underground temple point at. And the word "alien" is the fallen ones' newest
  mask, which is Alberino's own reading: what an age that no longer believes in demons calls them. It keeps
  everything inside the two sources the story allows itself ([style.md](style.md), Grounded).
- **Open:** why those hours, why it stops at dawn, and why here. Whether it's every night or some, every
  district or some, and since when (a city can't always have lived like this; **Proposed:** a few years, long
  enough to be routine).
- **Open:** how the city lives with it: the last train (00:40) and the last orders before two, shutters, taxis
  that won't take a fare after half past one, the night shift that sleeps at work, the police, who must have a
  line on it and (bought as they are) the wrong one.
- **Someone caught out becomes one of the possessed** (the user, 2026-10-16): the people still on the streets after
  two are taken over, and walk the empty city with the rest of what's out there. In the game they're the crowd's own
  figures made over (ashen, black-eyed, moving wrong: the MakeHuman test page's dead hours, `.claude/rules/humans.md`).
  **Open:** whether they come back at dawn, and what they remember.
- **Open:** how this sits with the season machines ([timeline.md](timeline.md)): four great battles a year meant
  to be rare and frightening, beside a hunt that can happen every night. **Proposed:** the dead hours' things
  are the small fry, and the puppets' kind of fight (strength that counts); the season's machine is what comes
  when the order turns its attention on the two of them in particular.
- **Open:** the scenes already set in the small hours: Mack's walk home from Hotel Rouge on the first night, and
  Minato's ride home after the river ([threads/the-detective.md](threads/the-detective.md),
  [the opening](main/spring/01-osananajimi.md)). Either they end before two, or the empty streets are part of
  them.

## The light

Christianity is the subtle light in a dark story: ambiguous, alluded to rather than explicit, never preached.
The exceptions are the order (where the Christian framework can show more openly) and the ending, which is a
little more revealing and more explicitly Christian than the rest.

- An allusion: Japan's hidden Christians, the Kakure Kirishitan, who kept their faith secret for 250 years
  behind Buddhist disguises: a Maria Kannon statue, a cross carved into a stone lantern. The old monk sweeping
  Tōkō-ji's steps may be a descendant.
- **Open:** where else the light shows: a person, a church, a moment of grace in a story.

## The detective's part

A man: a former policeman whose wife the circle took, and who gave her to them without knowing it
([cast/mc.md](cast/mc.md)). Nothing of the hidden layer is in him (until 2026-10-12 the order had killed him and
rebuilt him as a machine). He comes at it from outside, through his cases, his wife's notebooks and the dead
hours. **Open:** what Michael ([cast/michael.md](cast/michael.md)) is to him now that there was nothing to free
him from.

## Dom's part

The second MC, an American mobster hiding in Tokyo, is not fully human, and the story finds it out late
([cast/dom.md](cast/dom.md)). He isn't the programme's: not a machine, not a hybrid. **Open:** what he is.
**Proposed** there: the Watchers' blood, surfacing in a man. That would put the hidden layer inside one of the
two leads again, where Mack's body used to carry it.
