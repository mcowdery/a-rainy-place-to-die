# The main story

One main story per season, told in chapters, building to the season's reveal about the order and its battle with
one of the order's machines. Everything else is optional ([side/](../side/README.md)).

- **Each season has a lead.** The story is often not about Mack: a season's main story centres on its lead
  character, and chapters can hand the point of view to other people (the opening is Minato's).
- **The main story carries everything the year depends on:** Koharu's arc, Megumi's leads, the Mondays with
  Cash One, the reveals, the machines. None of that is ever left to optional content.
- **Side content feeds the climax.** People Mack has helped in side cases can turn up at the season's climax (the
  night driver blocking a road, the dock worker opening a gate). Players who did them get a richer finale; players
  who didn't still get a complete one.

| Season | Main story | Lead | Reveal | Machine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spring | [The 200](spring.md) | Koharu (and Minato, in the opening) | The 200 are real: a circle that buys people's ruin and watches it | The first hunter, in the crowd at the crossing |
| Summer | [One hand](summer.md) | The informant | The Amakudari-kai: one hand pays the yakuza, the triad and the police | A car that drives itself, on the Wangan |
| Autumn | [Gentian](autumn.md) | The oyabun's wife, Sayo | The order is worldwide; the programme that built Mack; the Beast | One that wears faces |
| Winter | [The first dawn](winter.md) | Mack himself | The Two Hundred, the man behind it all, what's under the city | The last: closer to the Nephilim than a machine |

All of it is **Proposed** until the user locks it, except the opening (spring, chapter 1).
