# Winter: the first dawn

**Districts:** Nishihara, Takanodai, Hanejima, plus everything open before. **Reveal:** the Two Hundred, the man
behind it all, what's under the city. **Machine:** the last, closer to the Nephilim than a machine. **The ending:**
probably Mack dies; a small hope from the Bible, more openly Christian than anything before. **The season:**
snow, Christmas lights, then the New Year.

**Lead:** Mack himself (chosen from four options: the salaryman, the teacher, the CEO, Mack). All year the story
has been other people's; the last season is his. The salaryman and the CEO lead chapters within it. The teacher's
story stays available for side content or later.
