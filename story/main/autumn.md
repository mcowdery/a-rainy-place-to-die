# Autumn: 竜胆 Gentian (Proposed)

**Lead:** the oyabun's wife. **Districts:** Suidō-chō (the Dome), Kasumi-chō (the hospital, the cemetery avenue,
the redevelopment site), plus everything open before; the Rindō-gumi's house in Kawabata has stood since spring.
**Reveal:** the order is worldwide; the programme that built Mack; the Beast and its worshippers. **Machine:** one
that wears faces.

In the spirit of *Fading Afternoon*: the last autumn of an old yakuza family. The Rindō-gumi's oyabun is dying,
the old code is dying with him, and Ryujin Kogyo (the new money, the Amakudari-kai's front, as summer showed) is
taking the family apart from the inside. 竜胆, the family's name, is the gentian: it blooms in autumn, when
everything else is going.

## The people

- **The wife.** **Proposed:** 竜胆 小夜 **Rindō Sayo** (*sayo*, "the night"). In her late thirties, married young to a
  man thirty years older, for protection, out of debt. She respects him; she was never in love with him. Behind
  the plastered wall she has become the family's real mind while he dies. She's the lead: the player lives her
  days in the house.
- **The oyabun.** **Proposed:** 竜胆 鉄心 **Rindō Tesshin** (*tesshin*, "iron heart"). In his seventies, dying in the
  hospital in Kasumi-chō. The last man in Tōto who still keeps the old code: no drugs, no women sold, the
  festival stalls and the old town protected.
- **The wakagashira**, his second, who has been selling the family to Ryujin Kogyo, or so it seems.

## Carried through

- **Koharu**, at the agency: the closeness neither admits, and Sayo between them. She knows where Mack spends his
  nights this autumn.
- **Megumi:** **Proposed:** in chapter 2, an old fixer of the family, who remembers how the port worked before
  Ryujin Kogyo, recognises her photograph: a woman like her was sold on through the port this spring, abroad,
  perhaps to Manila. Alive, then. Or someone like her. Mack pulls away from Koharu.
- **The Mondays** (Cash One is Ryujin Kogyo's, which makes Mack's blackmailers part of the war).

## Side content that can feed the climax

The kid from the car meets (a getaway), the kōban officer (the police kept away from the gate for one night), the
queen (what Ryujin Kogyo's men want in private), the dock worker (the family's old friends at the port).

## The reveal

The order is worldwide: Tōto is one city of many. It built Mack, in a basement under a hospital, as one of a
series. And some of the men who serve it worship something, the Beast, that Mack can't see or understand.
