# Spring: the 200 (Proposed)

**Lead:** Koharu, after the opening's Minato. **Districts:** Kaburo, Kawabata, Gakuenzaka, Asagiri, the C1 loop.
**Reveal:** the 200 are real: a circle that buys people's ruin and watches it. **Machine:** the first hunter.

Minato's death looks like a jealous boy who snapped. Mack, hired by the boy's mother, follows the 200 from
Koharu's frightened silence up into the towers of Asagiri, where rich men buy ruin and watch it, and in the end
the order learns that its property is alive.
