# Spring: the 200 (Proposed)

**Lead:** Koharu, after the opening's Minato. **Districts:** Kaburo, Kawabata, Gakuenzaka, Asagiri, the C1 loop.
**Reveal:** the 200 are real: a circle that buys people's ruin and watches it. **Machine:** the first hunter.

It opens cold: a young man at the river with lanterns, his phone lighting up. Then Mack. Days later the young
man's death reaches Mack's door: the police call it a fight between students. Mack, hired by the boy's mother,
rebuilds the boy's last week in his reconstructions and follows the 200 from Koharu's frightened silence up into
the towers of Asagiri, where rich men buy ruin and watch it, and in the end the order learns that its property is
alive.
