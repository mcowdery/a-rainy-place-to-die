# Spring, chapter 3: Papers (working title) — Proposed

**Character:** a young patrol officer at Kaburo's kōban, honest and new. **Open:** name. The player walks her
beat.

## The story

- **The meeting.** She stops Mack in the street and asks for his residence card: a foreigner stopped for being
  one. Her English is careful and textbook; his Japanese is gone. He has forged papers and a smile; she lets him
  go, and remembers his face.
- **The file.** She learns the Saeki case (the opening) was closed on an order from above, in a day. She asks
  why, and the station goes quiet. Her own senpai takes an envelope from Ryujin Kogyo's men every month.
- **The face.** In an old file on a dead foreigner, a newspaper clipping: "American reporter drowned in Tōto
  Bay", the Tōto Shimbun's crime reporter, his photograph above it. The man she stopped. She knows who Mack was,
  before he does: she's the one who tells him his name.
- **Her choice.** Report him, or keep his secret; stay quiet about the envelopes, or not.

## Themes

Corruption; belonging (who gets asked for papers); what an honest person does inside a bought institution.

## What it reveals

The police are bought, and someone paid to close the Saeki case fast.

## Who stays

**The officer**, an ally inside the police who knows Mack is alive, and a danger to him for the same reason.
