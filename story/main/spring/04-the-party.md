# Spring, chapter 4: The party (working title) — Proposed

**Character:** an elevator girl at the Tōto Department Store, Asagiri: the uniform, the white gloves, the
announcements floor by floor ("Going up. The second floor: ladies' fashions"), the same smile to every customer.
The player works her shifts: the food hall, the cosmetics floor, the rooftop garden on her break.

## The story

- **Her friend** was the girl the night driver drove at 2 a.m., found in the river. They worked the same lift.
- **The invitation.** A charming older customer, generous with gifts, invites her to a party at the top of a
  tower: the same kind of party her friend went to.
- **The choice.** She wants a different life, the kind the customers in her lift have, and the invitation is a
  door to it. Mack can see where the door leads.
- **The party.** Mack goes in her place, or with her: a suite in an Asagiri tower, rich men, a camera behind a
  mirror, an envelope on a tray. The circle in the flesh, and its guards.

## Themes

Inequality (the women who serve the rich, the rich who think they can buy them); wanting a better life and the
price on it; the smile as a uniform.

## Fights

The party's guards and the tower's security: on foot, up a skyscraper.

## What it adds

The circle up close: who comes to its parties, how its girls are chosen, and the first faces Mack can put names
to. It sets up the finale.

## Who stays

**The elevator girl**: a friend in Asagiri, and perhaps more of the story's light than she knows.
