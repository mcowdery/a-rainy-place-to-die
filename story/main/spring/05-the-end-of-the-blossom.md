# Spring, chapter 5: The end of the blossom (working title) — Proposed

The spring finale. **Character:** Koharu, the girlfriend who survived the opening.

## The story

- **After.** Everyone knows. The video is out there. Minato's mother won't look at her. She drops out, can't
  go home, and ends up working nights in Hoshikuzu Yokocho, at BAR 黒猫 Kuroneko, whose mama takes her in.
- **Mack's case closes in.** His reconstruction of Minato's last night (played as Minato) and what Koharu
  finally tells him lead to the circle that bought the videos: rich men, a party, the phrase "the two hundred".
  For a moment, in the background of a recording, a man: the first glimpse of the figure behind it all.
- **The offer.** A gentle circle of women finds Koharu and offers what she can't find anywhere: forgiveness.
  They are the Reimei-kai's followers, the order's lowest rung.
- **The end of spring.** The last blossom falls. The number 200 turns up where only Mack will see it: they
  know he's looking.
- **The machine.** **Proposed:** the reason they know is Monday. Cash One's collectors got greedy, someone above
  them noticed the money, and the order learned its property is alive and in Kaburo. The first hunter comes for
  him in the crowd at the scramble crossing under the mega-sign: man-shaped, relentless, untouched by everything
  that put down the season's other enemies. Mack doesn't win. He survives, barely, and
  has to be put back together.
- **The reveal:** the 200 are real: a circle that buys people's ruin and watches it.

## Themes

Guilt and the hunger for absolution; the false absolution the order offers.
