# Summer, chapter 4: Bayshore (working title) — Proposed, the finale

**Character:** an informant: a woman inside the triad's accounts (Chinese, born in Tōto, never fully either)
who has been feeding Japan's Public Security police for years. The player lives her last week of doing it.

## The story

- **The proof.** She has the books that show it: the triad, the yakuza, the customs officers, the police brass
  and men in the ministries, all paid from the same accounts, all part of one network of favours. Its members
  call it the Amakudari-kai.
- **Her handler** at Public Security goes quiet. She comes to Mack because the Wangan kid told her he can drive.
- **The run.** Get her and the books across the bay to the airport, by the Wangan, at night. (Hanejima opens
  in winter, but a car fight opens the whole map while it lasts.)
- **The machine.** On the Wangan, a car that drives itself: the order's second machine, at the wheel at 250 km/h,
  through traffic, untouchable. Mack doesn't beat it. He gets her across, barely, and loses his car to it.

## The reveal

The yakuza, the triad and the bought police were never rivals: they're paid by one hand, the Amakudari-kai.

## Who stays

**The informant**, if she makes it out: a source abroad.
