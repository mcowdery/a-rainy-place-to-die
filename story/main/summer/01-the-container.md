# Summer, chapter 1: The container (working title) — Proposed

**Character:** a Chinese dock worker at the container terminal: twenty years on the cranes, a family in Fujian
he sends money to, a man who keeps his head down. The player works his shifts among the stacks.

## The story

- **The woman.** One night a container's seal is broken from inside. A woman (adult, from the mainland,
  promised a factory job) runs into the stacks, and he hides her in his crane cab.
- **The triad** wants its cargo back. The Japanese customs officer who waves the containers through wants the
  problem gone. The dock worker brings it to Mack, because Mack is the only man in Tōto who isn't paid.
- **The ledger.** The triad's manifests list women by number and price. Mack goes through them looking for one
  name, Megumi's, and finds a page torn out at the right month. Not proof of anything; enough to keep him
  looking.
- **The stacks.** A gunfight among the containers and under the gantry cranes, the triad's men against Mack.

## Themes

Immigration at its rawest; people as cargo; a good Chinese man in the middle of the triad's business.

## Fights

On foot, a gunfight through the container yard.

## What it adds

The triad's trade, the customs officers paid to look away, and the first lead on Megumi.

## Who stays

**The dock worker**, a friend at the port. **The woman**, if she wants to stay.
