# Summer, chapter 2: The Wangan (working title) — Proposed

**Character:** a kid from the car meets at Ebisu-jima PA, twenty-two, racing a patched-up coupe to pay off his father's debt on the triad's bets. The player drives his nights.

## The story

- **The meets.** The PA at midnight: tuned cars in rows, the crews, the bets. The triad runs the book; Ryujin
  Kogyo runs the next table over.
- **The fix.** The kid is told to lose a race on the Wangan. He won't.
- **Mack** comes in through the kid's mother, and through his own car: he can drive.
- **The race.** The Wangan at night, the triad's car and Ryujin Kogyo's both trying to put the kid into the wall.
  A car gunfight at full speed.
- **The money.** Both books, the triad's and the yakuza's, pay out to the same account.

## Themes

Speed as the only way out; debt passed from father to son; the scene's code against the money's.

## Fights

Races, and a car gunfight down the Wangan.

## What it adds

The racing scene (and SPEED LAB, the tuning shop by the port). And the first sign that the triad and the
yakuza, supposed rivals, are paid by the same hand.

## Who stays

**The kid**: a racing rival and friend, and a way into the scene.
