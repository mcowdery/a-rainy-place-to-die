# Dating (the open world)

Decided: dating sim elements in the open world, leading to sex scenes with Mack. Completely optional. Each
district has women Mack can pick up and try to date. They're one-night stands and short stories, nothing too
involved, but what happens can affect the romance options later. Mack resists anything long-term: the life he
leads is too dangerous, and he knows it.

## How it plays

**Proposed:**

1. **Meet.** She's somewhere in the district at her times of day: a bar counter, a café, the bathhouse's coffee
   milk fridge, the observatory, the arcade. A first conversation: choices that read her (what she wants
   tonight, what she's escaping), and his looks open doors his words then have to keep open. She can say no;
   some of the best scenes are the ones where she does.
2. **The date.** One evening somewhere in the city, from the places already built: dinner in Omoide Lane, the
   City Hall observatory, a drive round the C1 at night with city pop on the radio, the Ferris wheel (summer),
   the arcade, a yatai by the river. Dates cost money (and Mondays come round).
3. **The night.** A love hotel (Hotel Rouge's themed rooms), her place, his car. The standard edition fades
   and picks up in the morning; the uncensored edition has the scene, written in Cursor (Grok) from a brief.
4. **After.** The morning, and a choice: she may want more. Mack leaves before breakfast. A text or two on
   KAIWA afterwards; then the story ends, or comes back once, later.

Each one is a short story with one small human truth in it (a lonely nurse between shifts, an office worker
after her divorce, a bartender who's heard every line), not just a conquest.

## What it touches

**Proposed:**
- **The romance later.** Koharu knows what he is, and the more she sees of it the harder the autumn gets. The
  women he's been with can come back at the worst moments.
- **The blackmail.** Every night in a love hotel is another recording for Cash One. The more he sleeps around,
  the more they have on him, and the higher the Mondays climb.
- **His reputation** in a district: the bartender who warns the next woman, the mama who teases him.
- **His resistance.** Any choice toward commitment is one Mack turns down, until the very end.
- **Morality.** Sleeping around brings his morality down: see [premise.md](premise.md), Morality.

## Rules

- Every woman is 21 or older and plainly an adult; everything is consensual.
- They're women with their own lives and agency. Never the story's victims: not the women caught in debt,
  trafficking or someone else's contract (Natsumi, the container woman, Maria under her contract).
- A woman whose work trades on looking young (the maid café) is a romance option only as the adult she is off
  the clock.
- Mack is 41; the women range widely in age, not only the youngest.
