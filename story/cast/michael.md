# Michael

**Role:** a mysterious, knightly figure who shows up now and then. (He was the one who freed Mack from the order,
when Mack was its machine; Mack is a man since 2026-10-12, so **Open:** what Michael is to him, and to Dom.)
**Story character:** no.
**Name:** people call him **Michael** (ミカエル). Whether it's his name, a baptismal name, or something else, nobody
asks him twice.
**Home:** unknown. **Look:** **Open** (ageless; plain clothes; very still).

## What he is

Read one way, a man. Read another, an angel: the archangel Michael, who in Revelation leads the fight against the
dragon. **It's never stated**, not by him, not by the story. Everything else about him stays mysterious too: where
he comes from, why he helps, what he wants.

The hints, for the player to notice or not:
- He never seems to age.
- He knows things he can't (what Megumi used to call Mack; where Mack is).
- Clocks stop when he's in the room.
- The order's machines won't touch him, or can't.

Near the end, where the order is involved, the story can let a little more show.

## In a fight

Very powerful. He never breaks a sweat, against anyone: the puppets, and the order's machines too, the ones that
leave Mack hopeless. He's never hurried, never hurt, never surprised.

So he's used sparingly, or he'd take the fear out of the order:
- He comes rarely, when Mack is at his lowest, and never stays.
- Often he doesn't come at all, and doesn't explain. ("It isn't time." "Not this one.")
- He doesn't fight Mack's battles for him; he makes sure Mack is still standing to fight them.
- Mack's own story stays Mack's: Michael can't, or won't, save him from what he's done.

## What he asks

**Proposed:** one thing, said once: "Don't kill the ones you can save."
