# Mack (the first MC)

**Role:** one of the two main characters, a detective and an ex-cop. The thread through every season's story, though
the stories are often not about him. The other is Dom ([dom.md](dom.md)), whose "manager" he becomes.
**Home:** the housing ladder (see below). Starts in booth 17 at Manga Café Tsukiyo, Kaburo (`tsukiyo.booth_17`,
`tsukiyo.sleep`).
**Office:** up the stair of a narrow tenement in Kaburo's back alleys, near Cash One (`kirishima.case_board`,
`kirishima.mailbox`; moved from Kōpo Kawabata room 201 on the user's word, 2026-10-11: that flat stays, `kopo.*`,
as a possible home for Mack later): the office of 霧島探偵事務所 Kirishima Investigations (the agency on Kaburo's posters, "missing persons, cheating spouses,
discretion"), run for forty years by an old detective, now ill and half retired, who takes Mack on soon
after the story begins: the room, the name on the door, the phone, the clients. Japan in this era doesn't license
private detectives, so nobody asks for papers. The old man is rarely there; when he's
gone, the agency passes to Mack, and in the end to Koharu. **Open:** his name, and what he and Mack were to each
other before (the guide had Mack as his client, a foreigner who paid him to find a missing wife; a former police
detective would more likely have known him from the job).
**Heritage:** Japanese ("pure Japanese": the user's, 2026-10-12). A Tokyo man, who passes in any crowd. The one who
stands out is Dom.
**Name:** 真壁 **Makabe**; **Mack** is what he's called, and what the story calls him. Megumi is 真壁 恵 Makabe
Megumi: the name is his, and hers by marriage. **Proposed:** his given name is 誠 **Makoto**, "sincerity" (the guide
had it as Megumi's pet name for him; as his own name it carries the same weight, and his thread with Koharu hangs
on it): 真壁 誠 Makabe Makoto, so "Mack" comes from either end. **Open:** who made him "Mack": Megumi, who studied in
the States; or Dom, who won't trouble to say Makabe.
**Looks:** very attractive; it got him the women, and so fed what ruined him.
**Age:** 41. (**Proposed**, if the year is 1992: born 1951, the same year as Dom.)
**Look:** **Open.** **Proposed** (the showroom model, `mack`, built to try it): dark hair swept back, grey eyes; a black tee, dark jeans, black boots, and a black leather single-rider jacket with a stand collar worn open (a Japanese cut, not the films' jacket). **Open:** where the jacket came from, now that the night he took it from a bōsōzoku leader is gone; the shotgun: a sawn-off lever-action or a sawn-off side-by-side (what a Japanese hunting licence covers, likelier in a yakuza stash).

**Revised 2026-10-12** (the user's): Mack is **Japanese**, an **ex-cop**, and **human**. Until then he was an American
whom the order had killed and rebuilt as a machine, and most of this file was about that body: its senses, its
repairs, the memory the rebuild took, the hunters sent to take the order's property back. All of that is gone (it's
in this file's git history, before this date), and Dom isn't the rebuilt machine either. What it leaves unsettled is
marked **Open** below.

## Who he is

- A noir detective, with a noir detective's feelings and vibe.
- **A man.** No stronger, faster or harder to kill than a fit policeman of forty-one: he fights, shoots and drives
  as one. The one nobody can beat is Dom.
- A lone wolf by habit, who ends up with a partner. He knows a lot of people and he's friendly; the city talks to
  him. With Dom he's the one who talks, finds the work and points: the "manager" of a "mad dog"
  ([dom.md](dom.md)).
- **A detective because he has to know.** Megumi was taken, and someone is out there (a Red John-like figure: the
  man behind it all, [mythos.md](../mythos.md)) with some kind of organisation behind him. Finding the truth, and
  her, is why he does this work. He knows the work: the force taught it to him.
- Not rich. He struggles with his own life, and the story shows it.
- **A complex, sinful man.** Even while he looks for his wife, he drowns his sorrow in his vices. We meet him in
  bed with a married woman. He redeems himself as the story goes on.
- **Blackmailed** by the debt collectors (the loan shark's people, fronting for the order), with his affairs:
  the sex he had with women other than his wife:
  - The loan shark is **キャッシュ・ワン Cash One** ("ブラックOK 即日", its posters already on Kaburo's walls).
  - A couple of its collectors squeeze him as a side business of their own: prints and recordings of his
    affairs, old ones and new (love hotels have cameras, and Cash One's people own some of them).
  - The threat is his wife. They say they know where she is, and that she'll get everything if he doesn't pay.
    He pays every Monday, most of what he earns: why he stays poor, and in booth 17.
  - **Proposed:** there's a debt under it too: what he borrowed to keep looking for her when the force told him
    to stop (his past, below). A former policeman in debt to a loan shark, with photographs: they own him twice.
- **Resists commitment.** He'll take a woman to bed but not into his life: the life he leads is too
  dangerous for anyone close (see [dating.md](../dating.md)). That holds until the very end.
- **The married woman** he's with when we meet him: someone who doesn't matter, for now. She may return.

## His past

What's decided is that he's Japanese and was a policeman, and (from before) Megumi, her case, and his part in her
taking. The rest is **Proposed** or **Open**.

1. **A policeman.** A Tokyo man, on the Metropolitan Police. **Open:** what kind (a detective, by the game's
   chases and the words "ex-cop detective"), for how long, in which division.
2. **Megumi**, 真壁 恵 Makabe Megumi. *Megumi* means grace, blessing (恵み is the word the Japanese Bible uses for
   grace). She studied journalism in the States and came home to a job on the 東都新聞 Tōto Shimbun, one of the
   first women on its police beat. **Proposed:** which is how they met: the reporter the detectives wouldn't
   talk to, and the one who did. She saw something in him nobody else did.
3. **The night world.** **Proposed:** his beat was Kaburo, and the night world, the women, the lust he'd had all
   his life, found him there: a policeman who was owed favours in every bar.
4. **Megumi's case.** She stumbled onto rich men's parties and the girls who vanished from them: the circle, the
   200. Her paper didn't want it. She kept going.
5. **His fault.** The woman he was sleeping with was the circle's. He talked in bed: about his wife and her
   story, where she'd be, when. They took Megumi on a night he was with that woman. He never forgave himself for
   the night; he never told anyone. (When he was a machine he had forgotten all this until the end.
   **Proposed** now: he has always known where he was that night, and never known that the woman was the
   circle's, or that his own words were how they found his wife. That's what the end shows him.)
6. **Looking for her.** **Proposed:** the force closed her case fast and told him to leave it. He didn't, and it
   cost him the badge: how he came to be an ex-cop. He borrowed from Cash One to keep looking. **Open:** whether
   he resigned or was pushed, and who gave the order.
7. **What happened to her.** Taken because of her story; most likely trafficked, or dead. Mack doesn't know, and
   the story may not tell, perhaps not in the first game. Her story is never erotic, in either edition.
8. **Now.** A former detective with a kept licence, a debt, and a booth in a manga café.

**Gone with the machine,** each leaving a question:

- *His death and his memory.* He wasn't killed, isn't legally dead, and hasn't lost his memory. **Open:** whether
  any of it should survive in a human form (a beating that left a gap; a year he can't account for).
- *The body as collateral.* Cash One still collects debtors' bodies for the order's programme
  ([mythos.md](../mythos.md)); Mack's isn't one of them. **Proposed:** it's what his own contract says will happen
  if the Mondays stop.
- *The hunters.* The order's machines were sent to take back its property. **Open:** why they come for him now
  ([timeline.md](../timeline.md), Chapters).
- *Michael* was the one who freed him from the order ([michael.md](michael.md)). **Open:** what Michael is to him
  now.
- *The empty flat* where he and Megumi lived, which he walks through: it can stay, as a scene.

## What he can do

- **A detective's work:** a scene read properly, an interview, a file, the people he knows on the force and in
  the night world, the licence and the siren (below).
- **The reconstruction** (decided as the game's way into every story character's point of view; its basis is
  **Open** now). It was his machine mind rebuilding what happened from the evidence. **Proposed:** it's simply
  how a detective thinks, shown: Mack walks through what happened as the evidence lets him, and the player plays
  it as the person it happened to. Its rules keep it a tool for the mystery, never a solution, and four of the
  six still hold:
  1. **It only rebuilds what the evidence supports.** Cameras, phone records, objects, what witnesses said. Where
     there's no evidence there are gaps: static, grey, missing time. Each piece of evidence Mack finds unlocks or
     fills in a stretch. Investigation is how you earn a reconstruction.
  2. **It believes the evidence, lies included.** If a witness lies, the reconstruction shows the lie. The forged
     message in Koharu's name plays as Koharu sending it, until Mack proves she didn't, and then that moment
     breaks and is rebuilt. The player has to doubt what they've played.
  3. **It shows behaviour, not minds.** What people did and said, not what they thought. Inner lives come through
     what they left behind: messages, a tape, the people who knew them.
  4. **It can't see the order.** Its people scrub the cameras, and nobody has seen the faceless man's face, so he
     is a blank in it: a blur, a void.

  Gone: that it cost his body to run (rule 5), that it was the enemy's tool turned on them (rule 6), the trace
  his senses read from a room, and his own memories breaking into it (the spring fragment). **Open:** whether a
  reconstruction can still be wrong in a way that matters to him personally.
- **The story characters' side.** In the present, the story can simply cut to them (the story character at home
  tonight); the past comes through his reconstructions.
- **Not his any more:** the enhanced senses (sight, hearing, smell, taste, touch, pain turned down) and the
  "senses" view proposed for play. With them goes the dating side's tell, the scent of who would say yes
  ([dating.md](../dating.md)): his lust is only a man's now.

## In a fight

A trained man: fists, what comes to hand, a gun, a car. Good, and breakable.

- **Open:** how defeat works in play. For the machine it was a shutdown: ordinary people took him for dead and
  left, robbed or dumped him, and he woke hours later. **Proposed:** the same, without the body: beaten and left
  (an alley in the rain, a car boot, the bay's edge), he comes round with the clock moved on and his pockets
  lighter.
- **Open:** what he does in the dead hours, against things a man can't fight. Dom is the answer to most of it

## What the game already gives him

- The story starts on a Friday night in spring (day 1, 22:00).
- A car of his own at Yotaka Garage, a rented unit in a railway arch by the Toto Line. A new game starts him
  with an 80s hatchback (the Tōkai Kestrel 86 GT) and ¥60,000.
- The racing: the passes, the expressway, the PA meets. Races pay.
- **The glovebox siren, and the kept licence** (the user's call, 2026-10-11: an ex-cop with a kept licence, so that Mack
  has a legal reason to be in car chases, which the game is full of; confirmed 2026-10-12: the ex-cop is Mack
  himself). In the glovebox of his car is a magnetic red
  police beacon with its siren. **Decided:** the police let him use it, so a chase with it on is lawful, not a crime of
  his own; no other detective has one. In the game: B at the wheel steps the beacon through lights and siren,
  lights only and off (`district/siren.ts`); it stays on when he gets out, lights the street, and the traffic ahead
  pulls over for it; putting it up is acted (his left hand takes it from the glovebox, his right sets it on the roof
  out of his window) and so is taking it down.
  - **Whose licence it is.** Mack's own: he was a Tokyo policeman, and kept a *kyōryoku* 協力 "cooperating
    investigator" licence when he left, with the card and the beacon that go with it. **Open:** why a man who
    left under a cloud was allowed to keep it: someone at HQ who still vouches for him, or someone who wants to
    know where he drives.
  - **Open:** who at HQ knows he has it, and whether it's ever withdrawn (a bought officer could pull it at the worst
    moment, or use it to say he's impersonating one). Whether it covers any more than the siren: a pass through a
    cordon, a look at a file.
  - **His old partner** on the force is an idea already in the pool ([side/pool.md](../side/pool.md), Another
    detective): now a detective, and good at it.
