# Cast

One file per character that carries story; everyone else is in [minor.md](minor.md). New files start from
[_template.md](_template.md).

## A big cast

The story needs a lot of interesting characters beyond Mack: the story characters, and the people he helps
along the way who stay in his life afterwards (allies, informants, friends, debts). Each story should leave at
least one person behind who can come back.
