# The CEO

**Role:** a story character.
**Home:** THE PEAK's penthouse, Asagiri: 130 m up, glass all round; a butler in the foyer, a grand piano, dining
for eight, a study with a model of the city, the master suite in a glass pavilion on the roof, an infinity pool
on the terrace. Reached from the lobby (concierge, `the_peak.concierge`) by a private elevator. Node:
`the_peak.ceo` (on the terrace).
**Look:** **Open.**

## In the story

- **Spring** (a side case, [the queen](../side/the-queen.md)): one of the queen's regulars, kneeling on her floor on
  Thursdays. Seen, saying nothing.
- **Winter, chapter 2** ([main/winter.md](../main/winter.md)): dying, rich and terrified, offered a way to live
  forever by the order; inside the top tier, he opens the door to the Two Hundred. He has never seen the faceless
  man's face either.
