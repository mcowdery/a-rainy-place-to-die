# Name 名前

**Role:** one line (what they are to the story).
**Episode character:** yes / no (the centre of an episode).
**Home:** the place and its node ids.
**Look:** a line, and their entry in Krea Studio's Cast (name, outfits, LoRA) once they have one.

## Voice

How they talk: register, habits, words they use and never use. Two or three sample lines.

## History

What happened before the story starts. What they hide.

## Wants

What they want, what they need, what stands in the way.

## Arc

Where they are at the start of the year, and where they end, chapter by chapter if known.

## Relationships

- **Someone:** what they are to each other.

## In the game

- Nodes: where the player meets them.
- Scenes, chats, flags they set or read.

## Open

- **Open:** questions.
