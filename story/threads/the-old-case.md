# The old case: the killer across three decades, and the city as it was

**Proposed**, all of it, except what's marked as the user's. Drafted 2026-10-13 so that the two older versions of
the city each have a reason to exist before any of their art is made.

## What's the user's (2026-10-13)

- **The year is an unspoken 1992** ([premise.md](../premise.md), The year).
- **Two older versions of the city are wanted**: the 1970s and the 1960s. "The idea of seeing how the city changed
  over time sounds cool to me"; and of the sixties, "tempted to do all of the 60's too for no other reason as I
  find it fascinating".
- **The killer may have been killing since then**, and the story reaches those years by "memory trips".
- **No story for it yet**: "narratively I don't have any ideas". So what follows is offered, not decided.
- Manila is kept for now, as a test and a possible sequel's place; it doesn't fit the present story.

## How the story gets there: the telling

A memory trip can't be Mack reading a mind: he's a man, and his reconstructions never were that
([cast/mc.md](../cast/mc.md), What he can do). It's the reconstruction the game already has, with a witness as the
evidence:

- **Someone who was there tells it**, and the game cuts to that year and puts the player in the person they
  describe. The city round them is that year's.
- **It believes the teller, lies included** (rule 2). Two people tell the same night differently, and the player
  plays both. Later evidence breaks one and it's played again.
- **Its edges are what the teller didn't see or won't say**: grey, silent, missing time (rule 1).
- **It's small**: a district, a few blocks, a night. Never the whole city at once.
- **Open:** whether the older city can also be walked freely, outside a telling, once it exists.

## The pattern

The faceless man's killings ([mythos.md](../mythos.md), His profile) didn't begin with Minato. The same signature
is in two older cases nobody joined up: a man whose woman was taken from him, humiliated layer by layer, the scene
dressed as something ordinary, the police paid to believe it, and the number 200 left small nearby.

- **He comes when the city is being remade.** Each time, land is changing hands and a great deal of money with
  it, which is the order's business: the rebuilding for the Olympics, the scandal years of the seventies, the
  wreckage after the bubble. A season or two of deaths, then nothing for years.
- **Open:** the years. **Proposed:** 1964 and 1977. (1977 is Claude's pick for the seventies; the sixties year
  could as well be 1968, the year of the riots.)
- **Open:** his age. A wealthy man killing in 1964 is old in 1992, or he is something that doesn't age. The mythos
  leaves room for either and this thread doesn't choose.
- **Open:** how many, in each run. One each is enough for the story; more is truer to him.

What this would settle, if taken up (each is an **Open** elsewhere):

- **What the old detective and Mack were to each other before** ([cast/mc.md](../cast/mc.md)): the rookie at the
  scene in 1977 who asked the one right question. The old man remembered him for fifteen years.
- **Why the mama takes Koharu in**: she was Koharu, fifteen years earlier. Seduced, filmed, her husband dead and
  called a suicide. She never told anyone, and she recognises it in the girl before a word is said.

## What the older city has to show

For whoever builds it: the places these tellings need, and what differs in them.

- **1977, the neon district:** the love hotel new; fewer and lower buildings round the crossing; no giant
  screens, painted boards and neon in their place; the cars, the clothes and the telephones of the year.
- **1964, the old town and the docks:** wooden houses where car parks are now; the docks worked by hand, by
  gangs of men the family supplies; streets being dug up for the Games.
- **In both:** the same street plan, so the player knows where he is and sees what went.
- **A first cut exists** (2026-10-13): `?era=1977` on the city's page builds the same map with a lower roofline,
  fewer towers, no ad art, plainer cars, and the crossing's sign and the discount store not yet built
  (.claude/rules/district.md, The city in another year). It uses only what the game already has: nothing in it
  was made for the year.
