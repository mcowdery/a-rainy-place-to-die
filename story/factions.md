# Factions

The groups with power in Tōto. Each entry: what's in the world already, then what the story hasn't decided.
Behind all of them is the order ([mythos.md](mythos.md)); most of them don't know it.

## 竜胆組 The Rindō-gumi

The old yakuza family, based in the old town. Their family house in Kawabata: a plastered wall, a heavy gate
with the crest's lanterns, a pine, black sedans, young men outside. Nodes: `rindo.gate`, `rindo.guard`.

- **Open:** the oyabun, the lieutenants, what they run (the old town's festivals and stalls, construction,
  the port?), their code.
- **Open:** how they stand with Ryujin Kogyo: the same organisation (Ryujin a front of theirs), rivals, or
  an old family being pushed out by a new one.

## 竜神興業 Ryujin Kogyo

A yakuza front company on Kaburo's Host Street: a granite block behind a gated forecourt, black sedans, CCTV,
a gold 竜 crest, guards. Their dragon crowns the mega-sign on the crossing. Nodes: `ryujin_kogyo.door`,
`ryujin_kogyo.guard`.

- **Open:** who runs it, what the "kōgyō" (entertainment promotion? construction?) really is, and how much of
  Kaburo pays them.

## ステラプロダクション Stella Production

The idol agency, in an Asagiri tower. A glamorous surface, darker underneath. Its idols live in the dorm in
Sakuragaoka; their stages run from the basement live house in Kaburo to the Stella Theater in Denkō-chō, Tōto
Bay Hall and Tōto Dome. The idol manager (an episode character) works for it. Nodes: `stella_production.*`,
`stella_dorm.*`, `idol_theatre.*`, `bay_hall.*`, `dome.*`, `manager_flat.*`.

- **Open:** what's underneath: whose money, what the girls are asked to do.

## 東都警視庁 The police

The Toto Metropolitan Police HQ in Asagiri (stone, flags, patrol cars), and in Kaburo's plan a kōban with wanted
notices. Nodes: `police_hq.entrance`, `police_hq.officer`.

Corrupt: officers are bought, cases buried. There are honest ones too.

- **Open:** who in the police matters to the story, and whether the detective used to be one of them.

## The states

- **Japan's government** is untrustworthy: bought, compromised, covering up.
- **China's and America's governments** play their own games in Tōto, at times as hostile foreign powers.
- In every one of them there are good people as well as bad.
- All of them are puppets of the order, knowingly or not.

## Foreign crime

- **The triad.** Chinese organised crime, working the port and Kaburo. **Open:** its name, its boss, its trade
  (smuggling, drugs, people, gambling).
- **African crews, backed by the triad.** In Kaburo's streets and clubs. **Open:** who they are and what they
  run.
- There are good Chinese and good Africans in the story too, not only the ones doing harm.

## The racing scene

Street racers on the mountain passes (Kurokami, Yūnagi) and the Tōto Expressway, meeting at night at Ebisu-jima
PA. SPEED LAB by the port tunes their cars. Races so far: the C1 lap and the Wangan sprint, offered by the racer
at the PA (`ebisu_pa.racer`); rivals on the passes (the red coupe in battles).

- **Open:** the scene's names, crews and rivalries, and how racing ties into the rest (does yakuza money
  run the betting? does the detective race to get by?).
