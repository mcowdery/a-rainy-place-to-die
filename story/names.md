# Who's who and where

A quick reference to every place and person named so far: what the name means and what it is. Japanese names
are hard to keep straight (for us and for players), so each place has a **plain English handle** as well.
Handles marked *(proposed)* are suggestions.

## The city: 東都 Tōto

"Eastern capital": the game's Tokyo. On the bay 東都湾 Tōto Bay; the river 隅川 Sumikawa ("corner river") runs
down its east side.

| District | Means | Handle | What it is | Opens |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Roads and places outside the districts:** 東都高速 the Tōto Expressway (its inner loop is **C1**; **the
Wangan**, "bayshore", runs along the port); the mountain passes 黒神峠 Kurokami ("black god") and 夕凪峠 Yūnagi
("evening calm"), where the racing is; Manila (a race venue; maybe a map later) and Seoul (maybe later).

## Remembering names

**Proposed**, for players as much as for us:

1. **Show the handle with the name.** The HUD and the map say "KABURO · The Neon District"; dialogue mostly uses
   the short Japanese name, the handle explains it.
2. **Few names at a time.** The map opening by season helps: spring has four districts, not fifteen.
3. **People go by one short name.** Mack, Koharu, Rui, Julie, Minato, Jingūji. Full names only where it
   matters (a police file, a gravestone).
4. **A people record.** Mack's case board, and a page in the phone: everyone he's met, with a face and one line
   ("Koharu: Minato's girlfriend. She survived."). Players can look anyone up.
5. **No sound-alikes.** Two clashes to fix now:
   - **Rui** (the host) and **Rei** (Jingūji's given name) sound alike. Jingūji already goes by his surname;
     the clash is gone if his given name changes too, to something like 颯 **Hayate**.
   - **Asagiri** ("morning mist") and **Kasumi-chō** ("haze town") both mean mist. Their handles (The Towers,
     Southside) keep them apart; a rename of either would too.
