# Who's who and where

A quick reference to every place and person named so far: what the name means and what it is. Japanese names
are hard to keep straight (for us and for players), so each place has a **plain English handle** as well.
Handles marked *(proposed)* are suggestions.

## The city: 東京 Tokyo

Tokyo, on Tokyo Bay. Until 2026-10-12 it was 東都 Tōto ("eastern capital"), an invented city standing in for
Tokyo. The river 隅川 Sumikawa ("corner river") runs down its east side.

### The naming rule

The user's, 2026-10-12: **only districts and brand names are renamed**, as the *Yakuza* series does it (real
Tokyo, an invented Kamurochō, invented shops and companies). So:

- **Real:** the city, the country, its geography and its history; real institutions that aren't businesses;
  and real people and events (Dom's backstory names the Gambino family, Castellano and Gotti), told
  accurately: nothing invented about a real person that would badly mistell their story.
- **Invented:** every district (the table below), and every brand: companies, shops, makers, newspapers,
  stations and channels, bands, songs, apps.
- The game's layout stays its own: an invented arrangement of invented districts, not a map of the real wards.

What that does to the names that had Tōto in them. The first two rows follow straight from the rule (which the
user restated, 2026-10-12: the only made-up names are districts and brands); the rest are judgement calls about
what counts as which, **Proposed**, and until they're settled the guide and the game keep the old name:

| Was | Becomes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 東都 Tōto, the city | **東京 Tokyo** | Decided |
| 東都湾 Tōto Bay | **東京湾 Tokyo Bay** | Geography |
| 東都警視庁 the Tōto Metropolitan Police | **警視庁, the Tokyo Metropolitan Police** | **Proposed:** a real institution, not a brand (the *Yakuza* series uses it too) |

| 東都タワー Tōto Tower | **Tokyo Tower** | **Proposed:** a landmark. But it stands in Kawabata, across the river, where the real one doesn't: **Open** |

| Tōto Airport, on Hanejima | **Tokyo Airport** | **Proposed:** the island keeps its invented name |
| 隅川 the Sumikawa | 隅田川 the Sumida, or stays | **Open:** a river is neither a district nor a brand, but the old town on its far bank is invented |

**In the game** (2026-10-12): the rows marked decided or Proposed are applied: the city (東京 Tokyo), Tokyo Bay, the Tokyo
Metropolitan Police (警視庁), the Shuto Expressway (首都高), Tokyo Tower, Tokyo Port (東京港) and Tokyo Airport are on the
signs, boards, maps, menus and docs; the avenue Tōto-ōdōri and the bridge Tōto-bashi became Tokyo-ōdōri and Tokyo-bashi;
the city hall, the central wholesale market, the flood tunnel, the education board, the municipal schools and the
forecast (東京地方) say Tokyo. The airport's own control tower is now "Airport Tower", so it doesn't clash with Tokyo Tower.
Kept as brands (the table's rows): Tōto University and its high school, Tōto Shimbun, Tōto TV, Tōto Bank, the Tōto
Department Store (and its café and shrine), Tōto Dome, Tōto Bay Hall, Hotel Toto Inn, the Toto Line and the Tōto
Monorail, Tōto-Chūō (the terminal, its west exit and mall), and the small firms on trucks, planes and signs (Tōto
Unyu, Tōto Gas, Tōto Life, Tōto Air, Tōto Taxi, a tuning shop and a ferry line that carry 東都). Still **open**: the radio
stations' and tracks' names (Tōto Jazz and the songs "Tōto Express", "Tōto City Lights", "東都ベイ・ドライブ", and the
bands' names are in generated audio and `content/radio/stations.yaml`), the Sumikawa, Tōto-Chūō's name, and whether Tokyo
Tower may stand in Kawabata. Ids (`station_totochuo`, `toto_bank`, the `toto` line and city ids, file names) are unchanged.

## Remembering names

Decided since: the language mechanic ([language.md](language.md)) does this as gameplay: names start in
Japanese, and their readings and English handles arrive as Dom learns them (the mechanic is his since
2026-10-12), and the notebook keeps a record.

**Proposed**, for players as much as for us:

1. **Show the handle with the name.** The HUD and the map say "KABURO · The Neon District"; dialogue mostly uses
   the short Japanese name, the handle explains it.
2. **Few names at a time.** The map opening by season helps: spring has four districts, not fifteen.
3. **People go by one short name.** Mack, Dom, Koharu, Rui, Julie, Minato, Jingūji. Full names only where it
   matters (a police file, a gravestone).
4. **A people record.** Mack's case board, and a page in the notebook: everyone he's met, with a face and one line
   ("Koharu: Minato's girlfriend. She survived."). Players can look anyone up.
5. **No sound-alikes.** Two clashes to fix now:
   - **Rui** (the host) and **Rei** (Jingūji's given name) sound alike. Jingūji already goes by his surname;
     the clash is gone if his given name changes too, to something like 颯 **Hayate**.
   - **Asagiri** ("morning mist") and **Kasumi-chō** ("haze town") both mean mist. Their handles (The Towers,
     Southside) keep them apart; a rename of either would too.
