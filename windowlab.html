What the numbers mean

Yaw 90 faces right, −90 faces left, 0 faces you. Pitch −90 lies on the back, 90 face-down. Lean bends the spine (50 is a bow); don't use pitch to bend someone who is standing. Arm raise 90 is level in front, 180 overhead; elbow 0 is straight, 140 brings the hand to the face. Leg raise 88 and knee 88, with a seat height, is sitting. L and R are the viewer's left and right when the figure faces you. The inner guide lines are a hotel window; the outer ones are as wide as a narrow room still shows. The horizontal line is the sill at 0.9 m.
