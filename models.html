Showrooms

Where designs are reviewed before they go into the city. Each room is a page of its own; the Manila (Filipino) rooms are apart from Tōto's (Japanese) ones. Keys in every model room: WASD / Q E fly, L labels, 1 / 2 / 3 lighting, X wireframe, B bloom, R turntable.

Tōto (Japanese)

  • models-cars.htmlCars: every car, van, truck, scooter and patrol car in two paints, the taxi ads, the middle-distance models, the working vehicles.
  • models-mack.htmlMack's gear: Mack and his outfits, the shotguns, katana and bat, the three motorcycles, the helmets, and first person (V, ?fp=1).
  • models-plants.htmlPlants: zelkova, ginkgo, sakura, pine, camphor, dogwood, azalea, box; the foliage variants and seasons; M the old street tree.
  • models-buildings.htmlBuildings and street: the Kaburo mega-sign, the tanuki statue, the billboard and poster wall, the street and park furniture.
  • models-transit.htmlTransit: the train and subway car, the city bus, the airliners; M the district's previous models.
  • mob.htmlThe passers-by: bodies, hair, poses, outfits, the street of people.
  • characters.htmlThe named characters built on the mob's bodies.
  • humans.htmlThe MakeHuman test: the salaryman, the maid, Julie, a generated crowd.

Manilaya (Filipino)

  • models-cars-ph.htmlVehicles: the jeepney in each livery, the tricycle in six paints, the low models, and a size comparison with a sedan and a kei truck.
  • models-plants-ph.htmlPlants: coconut and royal palms, banana, rain tree, mango, bougainvillea, banyan, flame tree, and Manila's shrubs.
  • models-boats-ph.htmlBoats: the bangkas, the passenger launch, the lighterage barge, the container ships and tankers.
  • models-buildings-ph.htmlBuildings and street: hollow-block house, corrugated roofs, shanties, kiosks, carts, shrine, jeepney stop, parols, poles and wires, footbridge, basketball court.
  • mob-ph.htmlPeople: the Filipino outfits (under review), in Manila's skin tone.
