# docs/city-plan.md). Districts are sized like real ones: Kaburo (N, the neon core) is 6 x 5 cells, about
# Kabukicho; Asagiri (T, the business district) is 6 x 5 cells west of it, like West Shinjuku; the Toto Line
# runs between them. Around the core: the electric town (E, Denko-cho) east to the river, the old town
# (O, Kawabata) across it, the campus (U, Gakuenzaka) north, residential rings (R: Sakuragaoka, Takanodai,
# Nishihara, Suido-cho, Kasumi-cho), the port along the coast (H) and the bay's islands, the beach (B) past
# the river mouth. Only districts with a 3D style (plan.ts STYLES3) are generated so far.
